A design guide that helps intentionally design palette, typography, and layout, and avoid template‑ or AI‑like designs when creating standalone self‑contained HTML deliverables (landing pages, static documents/reports, independent dashboards, demos/games, pre‑development UX/UI alternative comparison mockups). Load it before writing HTML/CSS. It is activated for requests that create or compare standalone HTML artifacts, such as “make a landing page,” “make this content look nice in a single HTML,” “compare UX alternatives in HTML,” “handle the design,” and is not used for modifying in‑app components of existing apps (React, Vue, etc.) or for pure backend/logic work.
