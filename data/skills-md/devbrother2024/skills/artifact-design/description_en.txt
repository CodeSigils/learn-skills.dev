A design guide that helps you deliberately design the palette, typography, and layout, and avoid template‑ or AI‑like designs when creating a standalone self‑contained HTML deliverable (landing page, static document/report, independent dashboard, demo/game, UX/UI alternative comparison mockup before development). Load it before using HTML/CSS. It is activated for requests that create or compare standalone HTML artifacts such as “make a landing page”, “render this content nicely in a single HTML”, “compare UX alternatives in HTML”, “handle the design”, and is not used for modifying in‑app components of existing apps (React, Vue, etc.) or for pure backend/logic work.
