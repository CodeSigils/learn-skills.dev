The platform is engineered for long-form web-novel serialization, covering the entire workflow from zero to launch: initial concept, main-line/outline planning and optimization, next-chapter continuation, single-chapter expansion/rewrite/polish, context recovery, three-tier memory maintenance, and chapter quality control. Use it whenever you need to plan a novel’s main arc, optimize reader retention and payoff, continue an existing project, restore progress on an old one, or keep project memory—outline, foreshadowing, timeline—in sync.
