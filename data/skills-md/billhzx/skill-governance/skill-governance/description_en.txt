Discover, inventory, and securely manage Agent Skills in Codex, Claude Code, CC Switch, WorkBuddy, Hermes, and other local AI tools. Use this when a Skill appears to be duplicated, copied or linked, has become invalid, has inconsistent enabled status, is managed by multiple installers, is difficult to update or delete, or when a unique source of truth needs to be established, a machine‑readable asset ledger generated, and a safe cleanup plan created. An audit must be performed first, and explicit user authorization for the precise target list must be obtained before any changes.
