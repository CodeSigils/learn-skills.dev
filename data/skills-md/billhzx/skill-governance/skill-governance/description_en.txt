Discover, inventory, and securely manage Agent Skills in Codex, Claude Code, CC Switch, WorkBuddy, Hermes, and other local AI tools. Use this when a skill appears to be duplicated, copied or linked, has become obsolete, shows inconsistent enabled status, is managed jointly by multiple installers, is difficult to update or delete, or when there is a need to establish a unique source of truth, generate a machine‑readable asset ledger, and devise a safe cleanup plan. An audit must be performed first, and explicit user authorization for the precise target list must be obtained before any changes.
