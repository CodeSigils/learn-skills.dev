Formats and publishes content to Notion with full control over block types and formatting. Use this skill whenever the user wants to push, send, publish, paste, or save content into Notion. Also triggers when the user says things like "put this in Notion", "send to Notion", "publish to Notion", "format for Notion", or "save this to my Notion page". The skill asks which formatting profile to use, navigates the user's Notion workspace to pick a destination page, and publishes using the Notion API.
