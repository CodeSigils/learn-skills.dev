Session handoff — persists what happened in the current session to memory, SESSION_REPORT.md, and the research journal. Use when wrapping up a work session, before `/compact`, or when the user says "checkpoint", "save progress", "sync", "wrap up", "log this", or "handoff". Optionally pushes to Obsidian if the user has configured `.claude/state/obsidian-config.md`. Does NOT run briefings, calendar, or mail. Just gather, confirm, save.
