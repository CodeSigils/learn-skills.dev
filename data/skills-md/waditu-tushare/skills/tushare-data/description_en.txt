Tushare data research skills for Chinese natural language. Converts requests like “How’s this stock lately,” “Check the financial trend for me,” “Which sector is strongest recently,” “What are north-bound funds buying,” or “Export a market data file” into executable workflows for data retrieval, cleaning, comparison, filtering, export, and brief analysis. Applicable to A-shares, indices, ETFs/funds, financials, valuations, capital flows, announcements/news, sector concepts, and macro data research.
