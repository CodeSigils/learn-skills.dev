ruoyi-vue-pro (yudao/芋道) full‑stack development specifications and module knowledge base. It is used when adding, extending, or refactoring business modules, designing database tables and entity classes (DO), generating CRUD operations, designing REST APIs, or choosing design patterns such as factory, strategy, or template method in the yudao project. It covers domain models, data tables, code standards, and extension guides for 14 business modules (system, infra, pay, member, mall, crm, erp, bpm, ai, iot, mp, report, mes, wms, im).
