CSDN blog article publishing skills: skills for generating and publishing CSDN blog articles. Enables creation, draft saving, updating, and publishing of articles on the CSDN blog site. Applicable scenarios: (1) When a user requests "Help me write an article about XX and save it to CSDN"; (2) When a user requests "Update a certain article on my CSDN"; (3) When a user requests "Publish my CSDN article", this skill is invoked.
