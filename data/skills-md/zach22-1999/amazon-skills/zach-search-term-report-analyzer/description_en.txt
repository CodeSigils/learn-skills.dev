Analyze Amazon Ads SP / SB / SD search term reports. A deterministic script handles cleaning, time‑window aggregation, stem clustering, and decision calculations, while an AI assistant or human handles stem‑level semantic classification; by inheriting stems, it reduces the proportion of long‑tail terms pending determination and outputs six result formats: Markdown, CSV, HTML, and JSON.  

Use cases: decide whether a search term should be negated, control cost, continue testing or scale up, analyze 7‑, 14‑, and 30‑day CVR and ACOS changes, or extract attribute and scenario terms that can be fed back to the listing.
