A specialized skill for generating high-quality figures for academic papers, supporting two output formats:
(1) LaTeX/TikZ code: ideal for structured diagrams such as system architectures, data-flow graphs, and geometric sketches; ready to embed in papers;
(2) draw.io XML: ideal for decorative figures like technology roadmaps, research presentations, and academic slides; supports gradients, shadows, free layout, and can be opened/edited at app.diagrams.net.

Both formats follow a unified workflow: analyze input (text/image/paper) → drawing instructions → code generation → compile & verify → perfect delivery.
Automatically identifies the paper’s field and designs figures as a domain expert.
