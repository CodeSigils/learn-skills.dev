Open Spec-Driven Development — a workflow for the specification phase before coding. It provides a standardized generation process for six types of documents—requirements specification, acceptance criteria, architecture design, detailed module design, task plan, and entry guidance—for AI agents. These deliverables serve as contractual references for the subsequent autonomous coding phase by the AI. [Note: This capability is invoked manually by a human; the AI should not automatically trigger the entire process unless explicitly requested by a human.]
