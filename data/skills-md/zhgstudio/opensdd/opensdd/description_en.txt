Open Spec-Driven Development — a pre‑coding specification phase workflow. It provides AI agents with a standardized generation process for six types of documents—requirement specifications, acceptance specifications, architecture design, module detailed design, task plan, and entry guide—with the resulting deliverables serving as the contractual basis for the subsequent AI autonomous coding phase. 【Note: This capability is invoked manually by humans; AI should not autonomously trigger the entire process unless explicitly requested by a human.】
