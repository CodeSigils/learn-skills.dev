One-click comprehensive analysis of a stock/company. Parallel sub-agents simultaneously collect data across five dimensions—stock price, news sentiment, industry comparison, market environment, and company website—then the main thread performs cross-analysis, causal attribution, and trend forecasting, outputting a standardized HTML report (including intraday quote and 7-day trend). Trigger phrases: “analyze XX stock”, “analyze TICKER”, “how is XX”, “is XX worth buying”. Supports A-shares, HK stocks, and US stocks.
