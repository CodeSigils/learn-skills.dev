General Chinese Writing and Revision Skill. Used for Zhihu answers, forum long posts, public account articles, blogs, comments, character stories, historical narratives, news and industry analysis, popular science, tutorials, reviews, personal narratives, novels, stories, dialogues, voiceovers, and speeches. By default, write as if a person who has seen the events, researched the materials, and is willing to explain the whole story is speaking, emphasizing the lively feel and natural Chinese rhythm of long answers and posts on the Chinese internet, while avoiding vague institutional tone, slogan‑style speaking tone, marketing tone, and model tone. For non‑fiction long texts, first check whether the material is sufficient; if it is not, conduct research, ask follow‑up questions, or shorten, and never repeat explanations merely to pad word count. For factual content, additionally verify facts, quotations, data, and the user’s personal experience; fictional content can create characters, scenes, dialogues, psychology, and plot. The final manuscript must strictly avoid colons, dashes, “not … but …” and similar reformulation sentences, and must remove commercial jargon and model‑specific buzzwords. Do not create an author profile, personal rule base, or personal writing Skill.
