Horizontal-Vertical Analysis: an in-depth research Skill. Conceived by Digital Life Khazix, it merges Saussure’s diachronic-synchronic analysis, social science’s longitudinal-cross-sectional research design, the core ideas of business-school case-study methods, and competitive-strategy analysis.  
Use it when you want to systematically investigate a product, company, concept, technology, or person. Its essence is dual-axis analysis: the vertical axis traces the full life cycle from birth to the present (told as a narrative story); the horizontal axis performs a systematic side-by-side comparison with rivals or peers at the current moment. The two axes are then crossed to generate unique insights. The final deliverable is a beautifully laid-out PDF research report.  
Trigger phrases include but are not limited to: horizontal-vertical analysis, research this for me, help me analyze, deep research, do a study, investigate, competitive analysis, help me see how this thing is, what’s the story with this product/company/concept, help me figure it out, help me understand, do a deep research for me.  
Even if the user simply says “help me understand XX” or “what’s the background of XX,” trigger the Skill whenever the context implies a need for systematic, in-depth research (rather than a simple definition). It also applies when the user drops a product name, company name, or tech term and says “research this for me.”  
Do not use for simple noun explanations (user just asks “what is XX”), do not use for WeChat article writing (use khazix-writer for that), do not use for pure headline or summary generation (use wechat-title).
