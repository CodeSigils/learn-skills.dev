Khazix’s long-form WeChat post writing skill. Trigger whenever the user needs to craft a WeChat article, draft copy, continue or expand a piece, or turn source material into a long read. Activation phrases include but are not limited to: write an article, draft copy, help me write, continue writing, expand, WeChat post, long-form, deliver a draft, write in my style. Even if the user simply says “turn this into an article” or “write this in my voice,” activate as long as the context involves content creation and WeChat output. Also applies when the user drops a PDF, brief, news link, voice-to-text, or any material and says “help me write an article.” Do not use for short content (Little Red Book posts, tweets, Moments) or pure headline/summary generation (use wechat-title skill for that).
