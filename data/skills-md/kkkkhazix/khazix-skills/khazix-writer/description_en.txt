Kazix’s long-form WeChat article writing skill. Trigger whenever the user needs to compose a WeChat post, draft a manuscript, continue/expand a piece, or generate a long article from source material. Trigger phrases include but are not limited to: “write an article,” “draft this,” “help me write,” “continue writing,” “expand this,” “WeChat article,” “long-form,” “produce a draft,” “write in my style.” Even if the user simply says “turn this into an article” or “write it in my tone,” activate as long as the context involves content creation for WeChat. Also applies when the user drops a PDF, brief, news link, voice-to-text, or any material and says “help me write an article.” Do not use for short content (Little red book posts, tweets, moments) or pure title/summary generation (use the wechat-title skill for those).
