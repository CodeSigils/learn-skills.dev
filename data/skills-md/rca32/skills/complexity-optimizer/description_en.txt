Analyze a software codebase for algorithmic complexity and performance hotspots, then propose or implement safe optimizations without breaking behavior. Use when Codex is asked to scan many files, find inefficient loops, nested iteration, repeated scans, costly rendering/recomputation, N+1 queries, avoidable O(n^2) or O(n) operations, or reduce complexity such as O(n^2) to O(n log n) / O(n), while preserving tests, APIs, outputs, and maintainability. Do not use for a general Standards/Spec code review or to diagnose an unexplained performance regression before its symptom and cause are isolated.
