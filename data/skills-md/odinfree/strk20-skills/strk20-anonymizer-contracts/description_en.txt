Write, review, or audit Cairo anonymizer (helper) contracts for STRK20 private DeFi. Covers the privacy_invoke entry point the pool calls, OpenNoteDeposit returns, the balance-delta idiom, and the swap, Vesu lending, and escrow patterns. Use for the contract side of any STRK20 DeFi integration, or when a privacy_invoke call is being designed or debugged. Pairs with strk20-wallet-api (how a dapp reaches the helper), strk20-privacy (concepts), and cairo-contracts or cairo-security for general Cairo work.
