Generate vocal‑free, lyric‑free instrumental music / BGM, suitable for background music, atmosphere setting, and emotional rendering, with a duration of 30–120 seconds. It is triggered only when the user explicitly requests pure music/background music. During execution, it reads Meitu credentials, invokes the local `meitu` CLI, and sends the audio generation request to the Meitu OpenAPI, writing the result to the local output directory.
