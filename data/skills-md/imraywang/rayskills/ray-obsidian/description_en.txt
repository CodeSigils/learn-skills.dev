Create, review, or progressively adapt a locally new Obsidian knowledge base oriented toward long‑term knowledge and content production, reusing Ray’s materials, knowledge, candidate topics, writing tasks, research, articles and voice‑over drafts, publication feedback, and layered knowledge feedback loops. This is used for commands such as “help me set up an Obsidian knowledge base,” “prepare a local knowledge base for ray‑writer,” “integrate an existing vault into the content production pipeline,” or “check whether the knowledge base structure is complete”; it is also employed when ray‑writer cannot find a compatible knowledge base. Only missing structures are added; existing notes are not overwritten, moved, or deleted, and no plugins, sync services, or Git installations are forced.
