WeWrite self-learning module: learns writing preferences from users' manual edits (playbook flywheel), imports sample texts to build a style library, and learns layout themes from public account articles. Trigger keywords: “learn my edits,” “I made changes, learn it,” “import sample text,” “learn this article,” “view sample library,” “learn layout,” “study layout.” It should not be triggered by generic terms like “learn” or “summarize this article”—context from the public account/WeWrite is required.
