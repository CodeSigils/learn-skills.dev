A-Share full‑stack data toolkit — covering six layers of data sources: market data (mootdx + Tencent), research reports (East Money + iWenCai), signals (Tonghuashun hot topics + northbound flow + Baidu PAE + Dragon‑Tiger list + share unlockings + industry), news (akshare), fundamental data (mootdx financial/F10), and announcements (Juchao). It embeds all the calling code, is self‑contained with zero external file dependencies, and is suitable for scenarios such as individual stock valuation, research‑report retrieval, theme attribution, Dragon‑Tiger list tracking, unlock warnings, industry rotation, industry‑chain research, batch screening, and more.
