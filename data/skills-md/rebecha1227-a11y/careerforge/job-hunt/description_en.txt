AI Job Hunter. Based on the user's résumé, it automatically searches major recruiting platforms for matching positions and outputs a list of jobs with match-score evaluations. Supports LinkedIn, Indeed, Boss Zhipin, Lagou, Liepin, and other platforms. Triggered when the user mentions any of the following keywords: find a job, find a position, search for positions, job recommendation, job search, job hunt, what suitable positions are there, help me find a job, recruitment information, which companies are hiring, see what opportunities there are, application opportunities, job search, help me search for positions. When the user comes from another skill and indicates they want to look for a job or submit applications, it should also trigger.
