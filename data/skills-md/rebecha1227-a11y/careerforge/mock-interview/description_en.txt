AI mock interview tool. Based on the user's résumé and target job JD, it simulates a realistic three‑round interview (HR → business manager → executive), including follow‑up questions, pressure interviews, situational questions, and other authentic interview elements. After the interview, it generates a complete interview report with feedback for each question. It is triggered when the user mentions any of the following keywords: mock interview, interview preparation, interview practice, interview coaching, mock interview, interview prep, interview simulation, help me prepare for an interview, interview training, practice interview, interview companion. It should also be triggered when the user comes from resume-match / resume-craft / cover-letter and indicates they want to prepare for an interview.
