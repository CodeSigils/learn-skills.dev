Offer Decision Officer. Helps job seekers who have received multiple offers make the optimal choice: multidimensional horizontal comparison, comprehensive scoring, generation of salary negotiation scripts, decision recommendations. Trigger when the user mentions any of the following keywords: offer, choose offer, compare offers, offer comparison, which one to choose, salary negotiation, negotiate salary, help me choose a job, how to choose between two offers, offer decision, negotiate salary, how to discuss salary, whether to accept this offer, offer analysis. When the user comes from a mock interview or another skill and indicates they have received an offer, it should also trigger.
