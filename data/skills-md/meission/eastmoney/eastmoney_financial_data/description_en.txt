This Skill is built on the authoritative East Money database and the latest market data, enabling natural-language queries for market data (real-time quotes, capital flows, valuations for stocks, industries, sectors, indices, funds, and bonds), financial data (basic company info, financial metrics, executive profiles, core businesses), and relationship/operational data (affiliations and corporate operations). It prevents the model from answering financial questions with outdated knowledge, delivering authoritative and timely financial data.
