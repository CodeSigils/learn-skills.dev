Jacky-OPC is a one‑person content company’s full‑process system. It is triggered when a user says “Help me write something,” “I have a topic idea,” “Help me edit the draft,” “Record an inspiration,” “Help me search,” “Help me dissect this viral piece,” “Analyze this account,” “Help me do a review,” “Research this direction,” “Organize the topic pool,” or “How can this article be optimized.” It covers the entire chain: user profiling, benchmark analysis, topic research, copy structure, opening optimization, writing execution, publishing and operations, data review, and experience consolidation. It does not trigger for pure technical questions or non‑content‑creation tasks.
