Review the quality of financial research data tables, produce quality inspection reports (PASS/BLOCK/WATCH) and monitoring scripts/SQL that can be scheduled for re‑execution. Used when a user says “Are these data correct? / Help me quality‑check (accept) the XX table / Generate a data quality report / Why is this market cap so off? / Primary key duplicated / Data missing on a certain day / Why hasn't the financial report been updated? / It doesn’t match another database / Why did we pull a bunch of dead tickets?” Also investigate anomalies such as out‑of‑range market‑value figures, primary‑key duplication, missing fields, gaps in market/financial time series, delayed updates of announcements and financial reports, inconsistent time zones in unit naming across tables, ambiguous annotation definitions, contamination of industry/theme tags, query‑interface truncation or retrieval of invalid securities. The scope covers market data, financials, announcement events, securities master data, index funds, macro‑industry and other research databases. It is read‑only exploration; all conclusions are fully traceable and re‑runnable. Does not handle table creation (use finance‑ddl‑design), pipeline development (use finance‑pipeline‑dev), or query‑interface design (use finance‑query‑contract).
