Patterns for storing and editing long-form (and optionally localized) content with Tiptap (Medium-style WYSIWYG) on Prisma + MongoDB. Covers markdown vs editor-JSON vs MDX storage, locale strategy (per-row variant vs Translation table vs slug-keyed siblings), Tiptap setup, sanitization, and the authoring-UX checklist. Use when adding a CMS-like surface, a rich-text field, or blog/changelog/newsletter authoring on a Mongo stack.
