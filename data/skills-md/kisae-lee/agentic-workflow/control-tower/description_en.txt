Workflow orchestrator — the single entry point for all project work. Auto-detects where the user left off by scanning artifacts, guides them to the next step, and keeps them on track through the full workflow. Trigger whenever the user starts a new feature, resumes work, says 'where was I', 'what's next', 'start', 'resume', 'jump to X', 'go back to X', or mentions any workflow stage. Also trigger when the user describes an idea or feature without explicitly invoking a specific skill — control-tower will route them to the right one.
