Skill that determines the QA test level. It evaluates risk comprehensively by combining story descriptions, code diffs, and a dialogue format, and proposes an appropriate QA level.

Used in the following situations:
- When you want to determine the QA level of a PR
- When you want to judge the need for a test executor or double‑check
- When you want to set the level of test perspective review

Can also be invoked with terms such as “QA determination,” “test level,” or “risk assessment.”
