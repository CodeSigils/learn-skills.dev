Short web‑novel deconstruction. It breaks down the story core, structure, emotional line, twist design, writing techniques, and resonance layers of viral short novels (e.g., Tomato short stories, Story Club, Zhihu Salt Selection, “Chase the Wife,” social‑life themes, rebirth, villain‑abuse, and other popular genres). A single, end‑to‑end deconstruction pipeline: after completing Stages 2–6, generate a complete deconstruction report and save it to DeconstructionLibrary/{book title}/; downstream story‑short‑write reads the deconstruction report together with plot nodes, writing techniques, the original text, and _meta.json to produce a new piece. Trigger commands: /story‑short‑analyze, /短篇拆文, “拆短篇”, “拆这篇短文”, “短篇拆文”, “精细拆解短篇”, “8000 字短篇拆解”, “番茄短篇拆文”, “故事会拆解”, “盐言故事拆解”, “分析这篇短篇” — all funnel into the same pipeline.
