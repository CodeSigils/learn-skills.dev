Personalized daily arxiv paper digest with preference learning. Use this skill whenever the user asks about new arxiv papers, wants a daily paper digest, asks "what's new on arxiv", wants paper recommendations, or mentions reading/browsing arxiv. Also trigger when the user says things like "papers today", "new preprints", "arxiv update", or "what should I read". This skill fetches, ranks, and presents papers tailored to the user's research interests, and iteratively learns their preferences over time.
