YouTube video smart clipping tool. Downloads video and subtitles, AI analyzes to generate fine chapters (minute-level), users select segments for auto-clipping, translates subtitles into Chinese-English bilingual, burns subtitles into video, and creates summary copy. Use case: when users need to clip YouTube videos, generate short clips, create bilingual subtitle versions. Keywords: video editing, YouTube, subtitle translation, bilingual subtitles, video download, clip video
