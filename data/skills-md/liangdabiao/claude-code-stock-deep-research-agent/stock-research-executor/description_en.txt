Stock investment research execution engine, implements an eight-stage investment due diligence process. Receives structured research instructions generated by the stock-question-refiner, deploys multiple agents for parallel research, and produces investment due diligence reports with citations. Covers: corporate fact base, industry cycles, business decomposition, financial quality, equity governance, market divergences, valuation moat, and comprehensive report. Use this capability when the user needs to conduct stock investment research, fundamental analysis, or investment due diligence.
