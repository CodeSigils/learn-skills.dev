Stock investment research execution engine, implementing an eight-stage investment due diligence process. It receives structured research instructions generated by the stock-question-refiner, deploys multiple agents for parallel research, and generates investment due diligence reports with citations. Coverage includes: company factual foundation, industry cycles, business breakdown, financial quality, equity governance, market divergences, valuation moat, and comprehensive report. Use this capability when users need to conduct stock investment research, fundamental analysis, or investment due diligence.
