Perform multi-source cross‑validation on academic references item by item, comparing each field—author, title, year, volume/issue, and page numbers—while flagging issues such as volume‑year/DOI‑year conflicts, abnormal author order, and page‑number discrepancies, and producing a structured verification report. It can batch‑process the reference list of an entire thesis or proposal, as well as validate single entries, and supports synchronization and correction with Zotero.
