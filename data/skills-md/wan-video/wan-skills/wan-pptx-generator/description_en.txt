Based on Alibaba Cloud qwen-doc-turbo and Wan2.7-Image models, it supports automatically converting documents, text, or thematic descriptions into professional PPT slides with AI‑generated images. It mainly covers the following scenarios: 1) Document‑to‑PPT – directly input PDF, DOCX, TXT, MD and other format files to generate a presentation; 2) Text‑to‑PPT – generate slides according to the user’s entered text content or thematic description; 3) Stylized generation – generate a PPT by specifying a visual style (e.g., “Doraemon style”) and a narrative style (e.g., “TED talk style”). Trigger condition: as long as the user’s intent involves creating, generating, or producing a PPT or presentation—whether it’s a direct command (e.g., “Help me make a PPT”), file‑driven (e.g., “Convert this document into slides”), or theme‑driven (e.g., “Make a presentation about artificial intelligence”)—this skill should be activated.
