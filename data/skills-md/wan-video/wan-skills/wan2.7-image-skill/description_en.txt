Image editing—such as “replace the car in the image with a bicycle,” “change the background of this image to outdoors,” “change the clothing color from blue to green,” etc.; 3. Style transfer—such as “convert this photo to <xxx> style,” “redraw this architectural image in <yyy> style,” “reference the style of <yyy> to alter the style of <zzz>,” etc. Trigger condition: Whenever the user’s intent involves AI-driven image creation, generation, editing, modification, reconstruction, or style adjustment—whether the expression is a direct command (e.g., “generate an image”), colloquial request (e.g., “draw me a puppy”), or implicit need (e.g., “make this image more dreamy”)—this skill should be activated.
