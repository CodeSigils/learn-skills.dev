Based on the wan2.7-image model, it supports a wide range of image generation and editing capabilities, including text-to-image, text-to-multi-image composition, image-based multi-image composition, single-image editing, and image synthesis using multiple reference images, suitable for diverse creative scenarios. It mainly covers three types of tasks: 1. Image generation—e.g., “draw a cat,” “generate a photo of a woman in a red dress in a café”; 2. Image editing—e.g., “replace the car in the image with a bicycle,” “change the background of this picture to outdoors,” “change the clothing color from blue to green in the image”; 3. Style transfer—e.g., “convert this photo into <xxx> style,” “redraw this architecture image in <yyy> style,” “change the style of <zzz> to match the painting style of <yyy>.” Trigger condition: Whenever the user's intent involves AI-driven image creation, generation, editing, modification, reconstruction, or style adjustment—regardless of whether the expression is a direct command (e.g., “generate an image”), a colloquial request (e.g., “draw me a puppy”), or an implicit need (e.g., “make this image more dreamy”)—this skill should be activated.
