Periodic health check for an llm-wiki (single-wiki or monorepo). Use when the user says "lint the wiki", "wiki health check", "find contradictions", "check orphans", "find stale pages", "suggest gaps", "what should I ingest next", or asks for a structural/semantic audit. Produces a lint-report with verdict (PASS/WARN/FAIL), auto-fixes structural issues, flags semantic issues for user review, and appends curiosity seeds to questions.md.
