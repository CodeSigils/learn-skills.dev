Receives PRD, planning documents, requirements, etc., and generates a sitemap (screen structure diagram) as a Mermaid `.mmd` file. It visualizes the hierarchy from main menu → sub-menu → functional screen in a flowchart-based structure and outputs with a dark theme + handDrawn style. Triggered by requests such as "create a sitemap", "screen structure diagram", "IA design", "menu structure", "page structure", "screen flowchart", "navigation design", "sitemap", "information architecture".
