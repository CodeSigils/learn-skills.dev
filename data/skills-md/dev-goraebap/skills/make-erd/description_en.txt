Receives PRD, domain descriptions, table lists, etc. and generates an ERD (Entity-Relationship Diagram) as a Mermaid `.mmd` file. Supports all three types of ERDs—Conceptual, Logical, and Physical—and outputs them in dark theme + hand-drawn style. Triggered by requests such as “Create ERD,” “table relationship diagram,” “DB design visualization,” “entity relationship diagram,” “data modeling,” “conceptual ERD,” “logical ERD,” “physical ERD,” “mermaid ERD,” “draw DB schema,” “table design,” etc.
