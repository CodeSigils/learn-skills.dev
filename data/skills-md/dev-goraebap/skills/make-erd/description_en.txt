It accepts inputs such as PRD·domain descriptions·table lists and generates an ERD (Entity-Relationship Diagram) as a Mermaid `.mmd` file. It supports all three types of ERD—Conceptual, Logical, and Physical—and outputs them in dark theme + handDrawn style. It is triggered by requests like "create ERD", "table relationship diagram", "DB design visualization", "entity relationship diagram", "data modeling", "conceptual ERD", "logical ERD", "physical ERD", "mermaid ERD", "draw DB schema", "table design", etc.
