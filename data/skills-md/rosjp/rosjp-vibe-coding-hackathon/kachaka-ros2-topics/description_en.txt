kachaka の ROS 2 トピック一覧・読み取り・QoS の扱い。Use when the user wants to read kachaka topics (battery, odometry, lidar, imu, camera, map, tf, locations), finds `ros2 topic echo` produces no output, hits QoS mismatches (kachaka topics are BEST_EFFORT), needs the `--qos-reliability best_effort` flag, or sees the error `Failed to create log directory: //.ros/log`. キーワード: ros2 topic, echo, list, QoS, BEST_EFFORT, SensorDataQoS, battery_state, odometry, lidar/scan, manual_control.
