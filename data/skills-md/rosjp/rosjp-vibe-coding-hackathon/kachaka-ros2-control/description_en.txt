kachaka を ROS 2 から動かす (teleop, cmd_vel, goal_pose, rviz2) と、自前コンテナから 接続する際の実行方法。Use when the user wants to drive kachaka via teleop, send `/kachaka/manual_control/cmd_vel`, publish to `/kachaka/goal_pose` for autonomous navigation, visualize in rviz2, or separate multiple kachakas via `ROS_DOMAIN_ID`. キーワード: teleop, cmd_vel, goal_pose, rviz2, ROS_LOCALHOST_ONLY, ROS_DOMAIN_ID, マルチロボット.
