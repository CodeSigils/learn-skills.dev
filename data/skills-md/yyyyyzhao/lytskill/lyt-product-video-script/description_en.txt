The lyt series e‑commerce product short‑video shooting script skill. It is used when the user already has a specific product, product images, product link, product information, confirmed selling points, or existing product copy, and wants to generate a short‑video script, storyboard, UGC script, demonstration/comparison/review/scene script, advertising‑material script, or influencer‑sample script that can be filmed directly for TikTok Shop or cross‑border e‑commerce; it can also organize existing product copy into a shootable storyboard, or generate target‑market language with a Chinese back‑translation. It is not intended for selecting products from scratch, judging whether a product can be made, analyzing data from already published videos, directly generating/editing videos, or handling non‑product personal‑IP content.  
Trigger commands: /lyt-product-video-script, /product‑video‑script, /shooting‑script, /product‑short‑video‑script, “How to shoot this product”, “Write a script based on this product”.
