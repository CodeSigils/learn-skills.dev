The lyt series e-commerce business data analysis skill. It is used when users provide textual metrics, images/screenshots, CSV/XLSX tables, PDF/reports, competitor data, or backend exports; it first performs a data health check and calibration verification, then breaks down TikTok Shop, product cards, short videos, live streams, GMV Max, advertising, costs, profits, and store operation issues. It is suitable for post‑mortems, comparisons, problem identification, scaling/pause decisions, profit calculation, and report generation; it does not replace product selection from scratch, validation of existing products, explanation of platform traffic mechanisms, or vague queries about operational problems.  
Trigger commands: /lyt-data-analysis, /数据分析, /数据复盘, /经营复盘, “帮我看数据”, “分析这个表格”, “复盘这组截图”.
