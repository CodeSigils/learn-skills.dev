It is used when users upload multiple competitor main images, Top store main images, high‑sales product‑card screenshots, image‑folder paths, links, or sample tables; it then organizes sample boundaries, tags main‑image expression labels, abstracts high‑frequency common expressions, and derives testable main‑image formulas and editing briefs. It only performs sample‑expression summarization and hypothesis testing, without directly attributing sales, rankings, or Top status to clicks or transactions caused by the main image.
