LYT series product main image and detail page diagnosis skill. It is used when users upload their own product main images, secondary images, long detail-page images, product first-screen shots, product card screenshots, competitor pages, before-and-after versions of modifications, or related business data, to determine whether the page enables users to quickly understand the product, generate reasons to click and purchase, build trust, and eliminate doubts. Only when there is sufficient evidence will it output page attribution, modification priority, and a single-variable validation plan. It is applicable to TikTok Shop and cross‑border e‑commerce product pages, but not for selecting products from scratch, feasibility validation of existing products, batch commonality summarization of top/competitor main images, or full store data reviews.

Trigger methods: /lyt-product-page-diagnosis, /商品页诊断, /主图诊断, /详情页诊断, “帮我看看这张主图”, “详情页哪里有问题”.
