The LYT series business learning skill handles only learning topics related to cross‑border e‑commerce, TikTok Shop, e‑commerce operations, product selection, advertising, data, content, supply chain, business models, or AI workflows; non‑business or non‑e‑commerce topics should be declined. It is used when a user says “Teach me a topic,” “Continue to the next article,” “Write the next one based on my feedback,” “Create a continuous learning plan,” or “Save the course.” In these cases, the topic is broken down into a series of learning articles, the difficulty is adjusted according to the previous round of feedback, and together with lyt‑knowledge‑check forms a closed loop of “learn, test, fill gaps, relearn.”  
Trigger commands: /lyt‑learning, “Teach me a topic,” “Continue to the next article,” “Write the next one based on my feedback,” “Create a continuous learning plan.”
