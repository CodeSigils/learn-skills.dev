Use when the user wants to manage discount activities on a shoplazza store through the CLI — promotional campaigns, coupon codes, flash sales, buy-X-get-Y offers, rebates / tiered satisfy-N-get-M discounts, free-shipping codes, M-for-N (Nth-item-off) discounts, discount stacking / combination rules, or coupon (voucher) campaigns. Triggers include 满减 / 折扣码 / 优惠码 / 闪购 / 满X减Y. NOT gift cards (redeemable store credit → shoplazza-products `gift-cards`).
