A deep-dive analytical skill set that merges Warren Buffett’s investment philosophy with a modern multi-dimensional framework for listed companies, covering U.S., Hong Kong, and A-shares. Triggered whenever a user requests analysis of a company, stock, ticker, or investment opportunity. Scope: background analysis, management assessment, business model, moat evaluation, in-depth financial review (four-quarter tracking + balance-sheet health + cash-flow quality), earnings-call dissection (latest plus prior two quarters), eight-model valuation (DCF, relative, PEG, DDM, NAV, sum-of-the-parts, owner earnings, reverse DCF), competitive landscape, technical & candlestick analysis (trend, moving averages, RSI, MACD, Bollinger, Fibonacci, chip distribution), risk matrix, Buffett scorecard, plus actionable buy/sell/hold and position-size guidance with price targets.
