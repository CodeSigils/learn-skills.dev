Skill Evolver (Taotie) – strengthens a target skill by “devouring” and analyzing the strengths of other skills. It must be triggered whenever the user wants to: merge two skills, optimize one skill with another, compare and analyze the pros and cons of two skills, extract the advantages of one skill into another, or says things like “feed X to Y,” “optimize Y with X,” “combine these two skills,” “devour this skill,” “skill evolution,” “skill upgrade,” or “merge skills.” Even if the user doesn’t explicitly say “Taotie,” as long as the intent involves transferring capabilities between two skills, comparative analysis, or advantage extraction, this skill should be used.
