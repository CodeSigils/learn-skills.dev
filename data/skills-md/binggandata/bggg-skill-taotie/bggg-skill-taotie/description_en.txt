Skill Evolver (Taotie) — Reinforces a target skill by "devouring" and analyzing the strengths of other skills. It must be triggered whenever the user wants to: merge two skills, optimize one skill with another, compare and analyze the pros and cons of two skills, extract the advantages of one skill into another, or say things like "feed X to Y", "optimize Y with X", "merge these two skills", "devour this skill", "skill evolution", "skill upgrade", "combine skills", etc. Even if the user doesn't explicitly say "Taotie", as long as it involves capability transfer, comparative analysis, or advantage extraction between two skills, this skill should be used.
