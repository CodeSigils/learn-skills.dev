Skill Evolver (Taotie) — strengthens the target skill by “devouring” and analyzing the advantages of other skills. When the user wants to: integrate two skills, use one skill to improve another, compare and analyze the pros and cons of two skills, extract the strengths of one skill into another, or says “feed X to Y,” “use X to optimize Y,” “integrate these two skills,” “consume this skill,” “skill evolution,” “skill upgrade,” “merge skills,” etc., this skill must be triggered. Even if the user does not explicitly mention “Taotie,” as long as it involves ability transfer, comparative analysis, or advantage extraction between two skills, this skill should be used.
