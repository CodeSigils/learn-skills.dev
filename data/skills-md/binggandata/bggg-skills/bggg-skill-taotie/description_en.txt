Skill Evolver (Taotie) — Strengthens a target skill by “devouring” and analyzing the advantages of other skills. When the user wants to: integrate two skills, use one skill to optimize another, compare and analyze the strengths and weaknesses of two skills, extract the advantages of one skill into another, or says “feed X to Y,” “use X to optimize Y,” “integrate these two skills,” “eat this skill,” “skill evolution,” “skill upgrade,” “merge skills,” etc., this skill must be invoked. Even if the user does not explicitly mention “Taotie,” as long as it involves ability transfer, comparative analysis, or advantage extraction between two skills, this skill should be used.
