Reviews and strengthens implementation plans through adversarial rubber-duck dialogue. Identifies weakest areas across completeness, feasibility, scope, testability, risk, and assumptions, then asks pointed questions one at a time to expose gaps. Updates the plan with resolved answers. Use when asked to "review my plan", "rubber duck this", "stress test this plan", "is this plan ready", "challenge my plan", "what am I missing", or before starting implementation on a non-trivial plan.
