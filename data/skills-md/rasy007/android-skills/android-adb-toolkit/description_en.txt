Android ADB debugging toolkit with automation scripts. Smart logcat filtering by package/tag/level, screenshot, screen recording, app data export (SharedPreferences, database), memory/CPU profiling. Use when debugging Android apps, capturing logs, taking screenshots, recording screen, dumping app data, checking device info, profiling performance. Covers device management, app install/uninstall, shell commands, intent firing. Works with emulator and physical devices. Pure bash — zero dependencies.
