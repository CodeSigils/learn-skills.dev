Remove AI-generated traces from Chinese text to make it sound more natural and human. Ideal for editing or polishing Chinese manuscripts. Detects and fixes common AI writing patterns: overstated significance, hollow analysis, vague citations, triple parallelisms, high-frequency AI words, negative parallel structures, dash abuse, officialese, jargon stacking, etc. Supports both formal documents (academic/official/reports) and general writing. Based on Wikipedia’s “AI writing traces” guide and localized for Chinese.  
Trigger words: de-AI, polish, humanize, human touch, AI traces, remove machine flavor.
