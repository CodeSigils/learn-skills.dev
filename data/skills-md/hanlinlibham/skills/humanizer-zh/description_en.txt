Remove AI-generated traces from Chinese text to make it sound more natural and human. Ideal for editing or polishing Chinese manuscripts. Detects and fixes common AI writing patterns: exaggerated significance, empty analysis, vague citations, triple parallelisms, AI buzzwords, negative parallel structures, dash abuse, officialese, and jargon stacking. Supports both formal documents (academic/official/reports) and general writing. Based on Wikipedia’s “AI writing traces” guide and localized for Chinese. Trigger words: de-AI, polish, humanize, human touch, AI traces, remove machine flavor.
