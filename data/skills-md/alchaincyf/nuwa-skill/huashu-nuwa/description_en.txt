Nuwa Creates People: input a name/topic/even just a vague need, auto deep research → thinking-framework distillation → generate a runnable character Skill.  
Two entry points: (1) exact name → direct distillation (2) vague need → diagnostic recommendation → then distillation.  
Trigger phrases: “create skill”, “distill XX”, “Nuwa”, “create person”, “XX’s way of thinking”, “build an XX perspective”, “update XX’s skill”.  
Vague needs also trigger: “I want to improve decision quality”, “is there a way of thinking that can help me…”, “I need a thinking coach”.
