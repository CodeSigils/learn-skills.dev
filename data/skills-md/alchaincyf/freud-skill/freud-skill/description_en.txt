Freud.skill: a cognitive tuning system for conducting psychological analysis on AI. Based on Anthropic AI psychology research (Persona Selection Model, Global Workspace/J‑lens, Emotion Concepts, Introspective Awareness, and other seven papers).

Two modes: (1) Execution enhancement—provide the AI with “cognitive preparation” before tackling complex tasks, like a therapist helping a client reach an optimal state; (2) Diagnostic optimization—use psychoanalytic methods to uncover deep issues such as identity conflicts, rule stacking, implicit contradictions in prompts/skills/system instructions, and rewrite them.

Use this when the user mentions “performance optimization”, “tuning”, “output not good enough”, “unstable style”, “persona”, “cognitive configuration”, “optimize prompt”, “optimize skill”, “Freud”, “psychological analysis”, “give AI a therapist”, “give prompt a health check”, etc.

When the user starts an important/complex task but the direction is unclear, feels previous output quality is insufficient, or wants to understand why a certain prompt/skill works or doesn’t, it should also be considered proactively.

Even if the user only says “help me think of a better way”, “this result feels off”, or “why is the effect poor”, it should trigger.
