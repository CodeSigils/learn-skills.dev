Finds non‑obvious corners of a task by intersecting states, data, order of actions, failures, and parallel changes; turns them into expected behavior and testable scenarios. Use before implementation or review of a non‑trivial product, UX, or technical task; when changing stateful/async behavior, persistence, synchronization, permissions, integrations, and multi‑step flows; when the user asks to think through edge cases, corner cases, extreme cases, or to verify the completeness of the specification. Do not use for small mechanical edits and until the task itself or the scale of the solution is chosen—first nodumb.
