Helps break down a product or UX task before designing a solution: to see hidden assumptions, separate the product image from the model and technology, and find the most valuable question or next step. Use when you need to come up with or discuss a feature, screen, flow, redesign, concept, interface principle, or product strategy; when the request immediately suggests a solution; when the options seem equally possible; or when a demanding design consultant is needed. Do not use for implementing an already agreed‑upon solution, a specific visual tweak, or technical implementation without a product fork.
