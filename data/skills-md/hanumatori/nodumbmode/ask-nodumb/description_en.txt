Helps break down a product or UX problem before designing a solution: to see hidden assumptions, separate the product image from the model and technology, and find the most valuable question or next step. Use it when you need to come up with or discuss a feature, screen, flow, redesign, concept, interface principle, or product strategy; when the request immediately suggests a solution; when options seem equally possible; or when you need a demanding design consultant. Do not use it for implementing an already agreed-upon solution, a specific visual tweak, or technical implementation without a product fork.
