Verify the solution approach before the error cost has increased. Use it: before a non‑trivial feature or an expensive fork; when a request describes a ready-made solution instead of a problem; before a redesign, migration, unification, or other large‑scale change; before a second edit without new facts; after two failed fixes, a repeated report of the same symptom, or a single false victory. Do not use it as a ritual for minor obvious edits and the continuation of already agreed‑upon work.
