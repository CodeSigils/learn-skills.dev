Hold what a session settled so it survives a compaction, a model change, a resume, or a handoff: the objective in hand, the requests parked behind it, and every decision with the alternative it rejected. Use when a decision is made, a constraint is stated, an approval is given, the objective changes, or a second request arrives before the first is finished. Use again on the far side, when you are working from a summary rather than the conversation that produced it. A decision that lived only in the conversation did not survive it, and the one nobody wrote down is the one that gets re-argued at the worst moment. Not for facts with evidence, which belong wherever the host already keeps them, and not for durable records a later reader needs, which is a documentation decision.
