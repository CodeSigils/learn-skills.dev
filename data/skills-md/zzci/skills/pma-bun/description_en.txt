Bun implementation guide for PMA-managed backend and full-stack monorepos. Covers Bun workspaces with `apps/*` and `packages/*`, API modules under `src/modules`, strict linting with ESLint + @antfu/eslint-config, Drizzle over SQLite-first storage, OpenAPIHono on top of `Bun.serve()`, validated env config, Vite dev integration, standalone binary compilation with embedded assets and migrations, and CI quality gates.
