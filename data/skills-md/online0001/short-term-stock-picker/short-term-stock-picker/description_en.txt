Short-term stock screening tool, based on AKShare data. Screening criteria: circulating market cap ≤ 15 billion, has hit the daily limit up in the past 20 trading days, non‑ST, major A‑share/Shenzhen/ChiNext/STAR Market, estimated net inflow of main funds in the past 3 days, turnover rate ≤ 10%. Trigger scenarios: (1) user asks “help me pick short-term stocks,” “short-term trading,” “find strong stocks,” “screen stocks”; (2) need to screen A‑share short-term opportunities according to the criteria; (3) when executing the stock selection strategy.
