Parking Information Domain (park): parking lot list and codes, basic/system/area/channel/cloud parking equipment information, empty and remaining parking spaces, parking fee standard queries and billing calculations for other vehicle types, vehicle free‑parking information, screen‑display and voice content to be shown when searching for a specific vehicle, vehicle coupon records (read‑only). Used when a user needs to retrieve parking lot attributes, remaining spaces, and fee standards by parkCode. Boundaries: real‑time fee calculation/payments are handled in trade (openydt-billing); sending screen displays/broadcasts to devices is handled in device; coupon issuance/recovery is handled in coupon.
