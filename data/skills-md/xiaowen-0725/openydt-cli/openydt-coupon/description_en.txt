Electronic coupons and merchant domain (coupon): merchants (trader) add, modify, freeze, delete, and query; create electronic coupon templates; sell coupons to merchants; issue coupons to vehicles; and handle a complete closed loop of coupon checking and recycling. Used when users need to manage parking or discount coupons for merchants, or handle coupon issuance‑verification‑recycling. The actual fee/payment after coupon deduction is settled in the trade domain (openydt-billing).
