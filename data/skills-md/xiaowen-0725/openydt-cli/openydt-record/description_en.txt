Parking record domain (parking): query of on‑site vehicles / entry‑exit records, parking record details, payment records and overdue bill queries, supplemental vehicle entry recording, license‑plate correction, lock/unlock vehicles, interception policies, self‑service entry‑exit. Used when the user needs to check on‑site vehicles, entry‑exit records, overdue payments, lock vehicles, or perform supplemental correction. Note the boundary: for real‑time fee calculation/payment callbacks, use the trade domain (openydt-billing); this domain only queries historical payment records/bills.
