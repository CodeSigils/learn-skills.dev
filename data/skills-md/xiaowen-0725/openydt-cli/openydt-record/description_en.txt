Parking records domain (parking): query of on‑site vehicles and entry/exit records, parking record details, payment records and overdue bill queries, supplemental entry recording, license‑plate correction, lock/unlock, interception policies, and self‑service entry/exit. Used when a user needs to check on‑site vehicles, entry/exit records, overdue payments, lock a vehicle, or perform supplemental error correction. Note the boundary: for real‑time fee calculation/payment callbacks, use the trade domain (openydt‑billing); this domain only retrieves historical payment records/bills.
