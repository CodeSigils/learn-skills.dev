Parking payment transaction domain (trade): real-time/estimated fee lookup for temporary parking vehicles, payment information feedback, batch overdue payment, and preset prepayments with operational points. It is used when a user needs to calculate parking fees, return/synchronize payment orders, batch pay overdue amounts, or automatically deduct from a vehicle's prepaid balance. This domain belongs to “real-time fee lookup/payment”; for historical bill/payment record queries, please use the parking domain (openydt-record).
