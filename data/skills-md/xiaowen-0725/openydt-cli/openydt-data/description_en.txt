Data analysis domain (data): read‑only statistical reports such as payment bills and bill summaries, traffic‑flow curves, top license‑plate distribution, real‑time on‑site statistics, number of vehicle dispatches/transactions for the day, parking‑space usage (including eChart heat maps), parking‑duration distribution, etc. It is used when users need to generate parking‑lot operation reports, trend analyses, or data health checks (aggregated statistics rather than individual‑vehicle details). Note: the three statistical interfaces for parking‑space, duration, and eChart have a platform contract flag “write”; calls require “--yes”. For individual‑vehicle details and on‑site vehicles, see the parking domain (openydt‑record).
