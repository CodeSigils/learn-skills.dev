Bid document template filling engine. Used when the user explicitly requests “fill template,” “write bid,” “produce bid,” “generate tender documents,” “bid filling,” “apply template,” “create bid,” or provides a de‑identified DOCX template that needs to be populated. It scans placeholders and dummy text in the template, extracts tender information, gathers bidder data, builds a replacement map, performs the fill‑in, and outputs a finished bid ready for direct submission. It covers all sections of the commercial bid, technical bid, and pricing proposal.
