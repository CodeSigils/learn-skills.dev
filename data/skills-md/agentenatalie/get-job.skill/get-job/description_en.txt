Internship.skill / get-job.skill: a full‑process job‑search agent that handles everything from researching positions and revising resumes to securing an offer.  
Three‑step workflow: ① Use WebSearch to investigate exactly what the target role requires → ② Reverse‑engineer the resume to match the role, translating unrelated experiences into the role’s language → ③ Conduct evidence‑based audits to eliminate pitfalls and prepare question‑by‑question scripts for the interview.  
Applicable to any role and any background span for internships, campus recruitment, or corporate hiring. Delivers three outputs: position research, revised resume (docx), and interview preparation.  
Trigger phrases: “Help me apply for X position,” “Revise resume,” “Optimize resume for target role,” “Research this role,” “Prepare for interview,” “Prepare interview based on my resume,” “Mock interview,” “Career change/cross‑field job search,” “Campus/fall recruitment/summer internship preparation,” “What if my background doesn’t match?”
