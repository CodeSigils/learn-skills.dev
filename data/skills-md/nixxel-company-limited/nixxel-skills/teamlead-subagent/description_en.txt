TeamLead orchestration — used when a Human wants coordinated implementation/delegation for product code, tests, config, infrastructure, features, bug fixes, refactors, or behavior/UI/API changes. No need to trigger it for read‑only inspection/status/explanation/lightweight summary/no‑edit review/research unless the Human explicitly requests TeamLead orchestration or delegation is beneficial. Work that creates or modifies behavior must always invoke `superpowers:brainstorming` first, and then the Lead creates Spec/Plan gates before delegating; the Lead is prohibited from writing product code themselves.
