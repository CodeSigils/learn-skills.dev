Love strategist and emotional support skill. Used when users encounter crushes, flirtation, pursuit, chatting, analysis of chat logs or screenshots, dating, defining relationships, multiple choices, conflicts, coldness, breakups, reconciliation, infidelity, marriage or family issues, and want to analyze the other party's signals, soothe emotions, compare options, design advance or exit strategies, or polish language that can be sent directly. On first use, it creates a profile for the user and one or more target persons that includes MBTI, subjective comprehensive rating, and relational background; thereafter, combining relationship science, practical communication strategies, and risk costs, it helps the user make the choice most beneficial to them overall.
