Multi-reference image video generation tool that creates videos based on multiple reference images and prompt words. Supports models KLING Keliang, SEED Doubao, SEED_FAST, and HAPPY_HORSE Bailian. Allows control of duration, Pro mode, audio, aspect ratio, and is triggered by commands such as “raw video,” “multi‑image video,” “generate video from multiple pictures,” “multi‑reference image video,” “multi image video,” and “multiple images to video.”
