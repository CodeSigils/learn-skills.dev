LinkFox packaged Lingxing (Lingxing) ERP skill “linkfox-lingxing-erp”: collected and distributed by the LinkFox Skills repository, encapsulating the official Lingxing OpenAPI documentation and script usage; underneath it still connects directly to openapi.lingxing.com (not a LinkFox-built gateway). It covers roughly 373 interface scenarios, including advertising reports SP/SB/SD, orders and listings, inventory warehouses, finance and FBA, source tables, procurement, customer service, multi‑platform advertising and orders, etc. It is triggered when the user mentions Lingxing, Lingxing, Lingxing open interface, Lingxing ERP data, Lingxing advertising reports, Lingxing orders/inventory/profit, Lingxing SID, Lingxing OpenAPI, Lingxing ERP data, LinkFox Lingxing, or linkfox-lingxing-erp. Module‑specific parameters are in references/api.md and the various references/*.md; LINGXING_APP_ID and LINGXING_APP_SECRET are required.
