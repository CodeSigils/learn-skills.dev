Shopee (XiaPi) store coupons Voucher (same series as linkfox-shopee-store-auth) are forwarded through /shopee/developerProxy to all six Shopee Open API Voucher module interfaces: add_voucher, get_voucher_list, get_voucher, update_voucher, end_voucher, and delete_voucher. When a user mentions Shopee coupons, Voucher, store coupons, add_voucher, voucher_id, discount coupons, or discount codes, it triggers. Even if "coupon" is not explicitly mentioned, any involvement in managing Voucher activities for an authorized Shopee store should also trigger.
