Amazon Ads basic entity query skill, covering six lists for Sponsored Products (SP): campaigns, ad groups, keywords, negative keywords, product ads, and targets, as well as three lists for Sponsored Brands (SB v4): campaigns, ad groups, and ads; it automatically handles pagination, tokens, and inconsistent filter condition formats across entities in Amazon’s native API. It is triggered when the user mentions querying Amazon ad campaigns, viewing ad groups, listing keywords, ad placements corresponding to ASINs, checking targets, listing SP campaigns/adGroups/keywords/productAds/targets, or SB campaigns/adGroups/ads. This skill depends on linkfox‑amazon‑ads‑auth. Sponsored Display (SD) / Sponsored Television (ST) / Amazon DSP are not yet covered.
