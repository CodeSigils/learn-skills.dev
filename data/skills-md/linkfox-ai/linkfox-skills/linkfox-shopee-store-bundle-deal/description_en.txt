Shopee (Shopee) bundle discount Bundle Deal (in the same series as linkfox-shopee-store-auth), forwarded through /shopee/developerProxy, includes all 10 interfaces of the Shopee Open API Bundle Deal module: add_bundle_deal, get_bundle_deal_list, add_bundle_deal_item, update_bundle_deal, end_bundle_deal, etc. It is triggered when a user mentions Shopee bundle discount, Bundle Deal, combo promotion, add_bundle_deal, bundle_deal_id, or full-item discount. Even if "bundle" is not explicitly mentioned, as long as it involves management of Bundle Deal activities for an authorized Shopee store, it should be triggered.
