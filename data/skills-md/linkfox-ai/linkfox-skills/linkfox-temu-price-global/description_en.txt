Temu Global site (non‑US/EU) price/supply‑price API, forwarded through the LinkFox gateway with five endpoints (pricing list, recommended price, SKU supply‑price list, batch price updates, etc.), defaulting to site=global. It is triggered when users refer to Temu Global price, global‑site pricing list, priceorder query, SKU list price, checking SKU supply price, changing supply price, recommendedprice, or baseprice recommend. Use linkfox‑temu‑price‑us for the US site, linkfox‑temu‑price‑eu for the European site, and linkfox‑temu‑manage‑product‑global for product management.
