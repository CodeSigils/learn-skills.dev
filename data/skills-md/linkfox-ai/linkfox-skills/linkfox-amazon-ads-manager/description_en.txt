Amazon Ads management skill, covering ad query (list) and create/update operations for the three product lines SP/SB/SD. Queries cover six lists for Sponsored Products (SP): campaigns, ad groups, keywords, negative keywords, product ads, and targeting; three lists for Sponsored Brands (SB v4): campaigns, ad groups, and ads; and six lists for Sponsored Display (SD v3): campaigns, ad groups, product ads, targeting, negative targeting, and creatives. Write operations cover all create/update actions (including bid adjustments, budget modifications, status changes, Budget Rules). The skill is triggered when the user mentions querying, creating, modifying, or adjusting prices of Amazon ads. This skill depends on linkfox‑amazon‑ads‑auth. Sponsored Television (ST) / Amazon DSP are not yet covered.
