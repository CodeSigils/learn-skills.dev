Temu Europe site product pricing management API, forwarded through the LinkFox gateway to the Partner EU pricing interface: price order list query (bg.local.goods.priceorder.query), batch change of SKU base price (bg.local.goods.priceorder.change.sku.price), recommended supply price/base price estimation, etc. It is triggered when users mention Temu EU price, Temu Europe site price order, priceorder query, priceAuditList, change supply price, batch price change, site=eu price change, recommendedprice query, or baseprice recommend. Product management uses linkfox-temu-manage-product-eu; orders, shipments, and other domains can use the US skill with site=eu.
