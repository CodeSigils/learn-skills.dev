Apparel image generation (model/clothing type). Supports single images or image sets: white‑background images (invisible model), model images, inspiration images, selling‑point images, A+ images, size charts. A single image of a single type is output directly; multiple images of multiple types are automatically arranged and planned. When a user uploads a model image or apparel image (top/bottom/dress/coat/shoes & hats, etc.) and says “make a set,” “make a model image,” “make an inspiration image,” “make a selling‑point image,” “make an A+ image,” “make a size chart,” or “make a white‑background apparel image,” it triggers. Pure image‑editing operations are handled via linkfox‑aigc‑imagegen.
