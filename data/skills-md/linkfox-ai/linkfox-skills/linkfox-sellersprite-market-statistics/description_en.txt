When the user mentions category market statistics, select market dashboard, market basic assessment, node market quality, top product statistics, SellerSprite market statistics, or category statistics, this skill is triggered. Even if the user does not explicitly mention “SellerSprite,” as long as the request is to view aggregated statistics by category node, this skill should be activated.
