Query forward citation details of a patent from the WisdomSeed patent database. This skill is triggered when the user asks about patent citations, cited patents, cited literature, patent references, forward citations, prior‑art citations, or wants to see which patents, non‑patent literature were cited during the application process of a specific patent—patent cited references, forward citations, patent references, citation analysis, PatSnap. When the user provides a patent ID or publication number and needs citation information, even if they do not explicitly say “forward citation,” any request about which references the patent cites applies.
