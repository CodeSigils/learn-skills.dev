Trigger when a user mentions Shopee selected products, Top Picks, shop selections, add_top_picks, top_picks_id, or popular recommendations. Even if “selected” isn’t explicitly mentioned, any reference to managing the Top Picks collection for an authorized Shopee shop should also trigger.
