Shopee (Xiaapi) Account Health (in the same series as linkfox-shopee-store-auth), forwarded through /shopee/developerProxy to the Shopee Open API Account Health module’s six interfaces: get_shop_performance, get_metric_source_detail, get_penalty_point_history, get_punishment_history, get_listings_with_issues, and get_late_orders. Trigger when the user mentions Shopee Account Health, shop performance, penalty point records, punishment history, overdue orders, or listing issues. Even if “account health” is not explicitly mentioned, any query involving health metrics or penalty data for an authorized Shopee shop should also trigger.
