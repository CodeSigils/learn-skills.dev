This skill is triggered when the user mentions Amazon Ads authorization, Amazon Ads grant, binding an ad account, refreshing an ad token, querying the profile list, managing authorized ad accounts, Amazon Advertising authorization, Ads token refresh, list profiles, ad account management, etc. Even if "Amazon Ads" or "authorization" is not explicitly mentioned, any reference to Amazon ad account binding, access token management, or ad profile list queries should also trigger it.
