Product image generation (non‑apparel). Supports single images or image sets: white‑background main image, scene image, close‑up image, selling‑point layout image, A+ detail‑page image. A single image of a single type is generated directly; multiple images of multiple types are automatically arranged and planned. Triggered when the user says “make a set of images”, “make a white‑background image”, “make a scene image”, “make a selling‑point image”, “make an A+ image”, or “make a close‑up image”. Pure image‑editing operations use linkfox‑aigc‑imagegen.
