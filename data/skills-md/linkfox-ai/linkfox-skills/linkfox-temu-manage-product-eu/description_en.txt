Temu Europe site product management (Manage Product) API, forwarded by the LinkFox gateway to Partner EU's 24 bg.local/temu.local interfaces: product list/detail/SKU query, partial/full edit, deletion, inventory, listing/unlisting, pre‑sale, category pre‑check, attribute templates, compliance, external codes, video covers, etc. It is triggered when the user mentions Temu EU Manage Product, Temu Europe site product management, bg.local.goods, Partner EU, site=eu product listing/unlisting, inventory changes, product deletion, or compliance editing. Use linkfox-temu-promotion-eu for promotions; linkfox-temu-ads-eu for ads; and linkfox-temu-price-eu for pricing.
