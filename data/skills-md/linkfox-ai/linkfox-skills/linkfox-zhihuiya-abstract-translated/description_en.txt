Obtain translated versions of patent titles and abstracts from the PatSnap (Zhi Hui Ya) patent database. This capability is activated when a user requests patent abstract translation, patent title translation, translated patent abstracts, patent content in other languages, Chinese/English/Japanese patent abstracts, or needs to retrieve a specific patent’s abstract or title by patent ID or publication number, as well as for queries involving patent abstract translation, patent title translation, PatSnap, patent translation, or abstract lookup. The skill should also be triggered whenever the user mentions PatSnap, Zhi Hui Ya, or patent abstract lookup, even if “translation” is not explicitly stated.
