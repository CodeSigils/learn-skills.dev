Temu US site product price management API, forwarded through the LinkFox gateway to the Partner US price interface: price order list query (bg.local.goods.priceorder.query), batch modify SKU base price (bg.local.goods.priceorder.change.sku.price), recommended supply price/base price estimation, etc. When users mention Temu US price, price order, priceorder query, priceAuditList, modify supply price, batch price change, priceorder change sku price, recommendedprice query, baseprice recommend, it is triggered. Use linkfox-temu-manage-product-us for product management; linkfox-temu-order-us for orders; linkfox-temu-add-product-us for product publishing.
