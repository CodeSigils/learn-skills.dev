Shopee (虾皮) store product management (in the same series as linkfox-shopee-store-auth), forwarded through /shopee/developerProxy, handles all 57 endpoints of the Shopee Open API Product module: get_category, get_item_list, add_item, update_item, update_price, update_stock, boost_item, search_item, etc. When a user mentions Shopee products, Shopee listings, listing, delisting, SKU, inventory, price, category, attributes, get_item_list, add_item, product reviews, boost pinning, it triggers. Even if "product" is not explicitly mentioned, any query, creation, or update of products in an authorized Shopee store should trigger.
