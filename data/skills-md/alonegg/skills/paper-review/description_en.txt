Conduct systematic, multi-round, in-depth academic review of undergraduate theses. Follow the five-stage process of “full-text comprehension → structural review → paragraph-level refinement → issue aggregation → re-review iteration,” identify paragraph types and route them to six major specialized rules (introduction, literature review, methodology, analysis, conclusion, facts), and output structural diagnostics, paragraph-level revision suggestions, revision task lists, and re‑review reports. When a user provides a paper, graduation thesis, or dissertation and requests review, evaluation, revision suggestions, peer review, or appraisal, this capability must be used. Even if the user only says “help me look at this paper,” “review it for me,” or “give me revision suggestions,” this capability should be triggered. The review includes deep theoretical analysis: identify the paper’s implicit premises, locate its theoretical coordinates, reassess the paper’s positioning from multiple academic frameworks, and translate theoretical insights into actionable revision suggestions. When a user provides a .docx‑format thesis and wishes to receive a reviewed result with annotations, this capability must also be used (in conjunction with the docx tool to manipulate the document).
