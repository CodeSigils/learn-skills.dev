WeChat Official Account article subscription and push. Automatically deploy the wechat-download-api service, periodically poll the followed accounts, summarize new articles, and push them to Feishu. Trigger conditions: user mentions “Official Account subscription,” “WeChat article push,” “wechat watch,” “Official Account updates,” etc., or triggered by a cron job. Prerequisites: the user must have a WeChat Official Account (subscription or service account) for scan-code login.
