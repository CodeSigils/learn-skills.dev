**Research Report Deep-Reading Assistant**: Systematically read, extract, and analyze investment research reports (brokerage research, industry reports, macro-strategy reports, etc.).
- Mandatory trigger: Automatically activates when a user uploads a PDF research report and requests reading, analysis, summary, deep-reading, or key-point extraction.
- Also applies when the user says “read this report for me,” “analyze these research reports,” “deep-read these reports,” “summarize these reports,” “what are the core views of these reports,” “are these reports worth reading,” or “take a look at these PDFs.”
- Even if the user does not explicitly say “research report,” the assistant should trigger whenever investment-research-like PDFs are uploaded and analysis or summary is requested.
- When multiple reports are uploaded, the assistant will analyze each one individually, perform cross-report comparisons, and recommend which reports deserve deep reading.
