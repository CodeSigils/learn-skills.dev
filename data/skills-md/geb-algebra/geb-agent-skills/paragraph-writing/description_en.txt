A set of structure and conciseness rules for natural‑language answers that require each paragraph to convey a single message, display the main point with a bolded sentence at the beginning, and restrict the following sentences to evidence, reasons, specific examples, or supplementary information. These rules are used for creating, editing, summarizing, and reviewing responses that demand paragraph writing, point‑first organization, paragraph structure, or texts where the overview can be grasped from the opening sentence alone. Tables and bullet lists are excluded from the paragraph‑level rules.
