**Slash command**: `/max-search <question>`
Activate on: /max-search, "深度搜索", "max search", "全面搜索", "最大化搜索", "做个调研", "帮我调查一下", "最新动态是什么", "最近有什么进展", "对比一下", "分析一下", "做个对比分析", or any question that is factual, multi-faceted, or benefits from multiple orthogonal search queries.
