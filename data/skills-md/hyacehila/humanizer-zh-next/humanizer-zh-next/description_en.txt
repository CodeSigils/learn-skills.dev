Remove traces of AI-generated content from Chinese text. Using this feature while editing or reviewing text makes the text more natural and closer to human-written work. This capability is based on op7418/humanizer-zh and blader/humanizer, incorporates detection patterns from the English version, and rewrites trigger words, examples, and remediation strategies according to Chinese writing conventions. It follows the principle of “rewriting rather than deleting, equal coverage” and works through a draft → self‑check → final version process.
