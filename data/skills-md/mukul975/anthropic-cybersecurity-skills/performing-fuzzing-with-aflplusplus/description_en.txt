Performs coverage-guided fuzzing of compiled binaries with AFL++, instrumenting targets via afl-cc/afl-clang-fast, minimizing corpora with afl-cmin and afl-tmin, running parallel campaigns with afl-fuzz, and triaging crashes with CASR or GDB scripts. Use for binary fuzzing, crash and memory-corruption discovery, coverage-guided testing, or running AFL++ fuzzing campaigns.
