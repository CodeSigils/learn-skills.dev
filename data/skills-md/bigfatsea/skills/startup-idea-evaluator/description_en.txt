Evaluate startup ideas and early-stage projects as a pragmatic angel investor + operator. Optimized for small AI-augmented teams with China/global context. Trigger when the user: asks to evaluate a startup/project/idea, shares a business concept for feedback, asks about commercial viability or monetization potential, wants a GTM strategy, requests market sizing, asks "should I build this", "is this worth pursuing", or compares two pivots. Works with raw one-liners, detailed pitches, and everything in between. Even when the user hasn't said "evaluate" — if they describe a product concept, business model, or market opportunity and seem to want strategic input, invoke this skill. When in doubt, use it. If the user has an actual written business plan or pitch deck document and wants investor-style critique/rewrite of that document, use `master-bp-review` instead — this skill is for raw ideas and concepts, not for reviewing an already-written document.
