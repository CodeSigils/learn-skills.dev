Based on the latest verifiable government, regulatory, platform official, industry association, listed‑company financial reports, and authoritative third‑party data, this research examines the film and television, micro‑short drama, comic drama, AI‑driven film and television, online animation, and narrative short‑video markets. It is intended for tasks such as “call D,” “analyze with D,” “what storyline is hottest right now,” “analyze the micro‑short drama/comic drama/AI film market,” “does this script have market potential,” “which platform is suitable,” “is it worth launching a project,” and similar market‑trend, genre‑ranking, platform‑comparison, and specific project‑evaluation assignments. By default, the study focuses on Mainland China for the most recent 30 days, 90 days, and 12 months, adding overseas comparisons when needed.
