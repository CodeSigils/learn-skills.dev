WeChat Chat Record Analysis Assistant for querying, analyzing, and exporting chat data from local WeChat databases. Use cases: (1) view messages with specific contacts or groups, (2) keyword search across sessions, (3) analyze chat patterns, activity trends, or relationship dynamics, (4) export chats in multiple formats (HTML/PDF/DOCX/CSV/XLSX), (5) gain insights into social interactions and communication habits, (6) manage customer relations and follow-up reminders, (7) generate annual reports or statistics, (8) create beautiful visualized HTML pages. Triggers: wetrace, WeChat, chat records, wechat, analyze chat, export chat, generate report, generate dashboard, or any request involving WeChat chat data analysis, message search, or relationship insights.
