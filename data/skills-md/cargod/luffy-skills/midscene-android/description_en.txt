Vision-based Android device automation using Midscene to control Android phones/emulators via ADB.  
Driven purely by screenshots, no DOM or accessibility labels needed; can interact with any visible element on screen.

Supports: tap, swipe, enter text, launch apps, take screenshots, and more.

Keywords: Android, 安卓, 手机自动化, ADB, 移动端测试, 安卓测试, 手机操作, android automation, mobile app, tap, swipe, adb, test android app
