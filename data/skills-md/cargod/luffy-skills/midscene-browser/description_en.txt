Vision-based browser automation, powered by Midscene driving a headless Puppeteer browser.  
Driven purely by screenshots—no DOM or accessibility tags needed—able to control any visible element on the page.  
Runs in a headless browser without taking over the user’s mouse or keyboard, so you can keep using your computer.

Use it for: browsing websites, scraping data, filling forms, clicking buttons, testing front-end UIs, taking screenshots, automating web workflows.
