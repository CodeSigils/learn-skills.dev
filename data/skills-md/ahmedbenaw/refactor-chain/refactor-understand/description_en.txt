Use this skill when someone points refactor-chain at a project and you first need to understand "what is this codebase?" before touching anything — reading manifests, lockfiles, CI, and folder structure to summarize the stack, entry points, and how it builds/tests. This is the understand-phase entry point of the refactor-chain pipeline; it runs read-only, has no prerequisite, and feeds the diagnostic engine (refactor-diagnose). Trigger phrases: "look at this repo", "what is this project", "can you refactor this" (before any lane is chosen), "get your bearings first".
