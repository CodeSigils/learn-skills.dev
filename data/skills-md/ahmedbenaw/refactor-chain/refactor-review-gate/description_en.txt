Use this skill at the very end of a refactor to run the read-only ship gate — it aggregates the correctness, security, and performance review of the whole accumulated diff into ONE calm, ranked "here are the N things worth your attention, most important first" report. Trigger phrases include "is this ready to ship", "do a final review", "review the whole change", "run the ship gate", "give me the go/no-go". This is the review-phase GATE of the refactor-chain pipeline — the harness appends it as a kind:"gate" step at the end of every lane, and the Stop hook blocks "done" until it passes. It coordinates refactor-security, refactor-performance, and refactor-red-team, then de-dupes and ranks everything into one list. Read-only: it reviews and reports, it never edits code.
