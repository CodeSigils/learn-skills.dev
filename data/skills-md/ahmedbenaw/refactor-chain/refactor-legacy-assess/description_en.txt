Use this skill to detect the anti-patterns and modernization-readiness gaps that decide which refactor lane and steps make sense — tight coupling, missing/loose types, deprecated or end-of-life APIs, dead code, no test seam, outdated language/runtime targets. Trigger phrases: "is this ready to modernize", "what's blocking an upgrade", "find the anti-patterns", "how bad is the tech debt", "can we safely migrate this", "what's deprecated in here". This runs in the diagnose phase of the refactor-chain pipeline, after refactor-understand, and its readiness verdict informs which lane/steps refactor-diagnose and the plan phase choose. Read-only and advisory — it grades and gates, it never edits code.
