Roblox TeleportService and multi-place architecture — the modern TeleportAsync API that replaces deprecated Teleport/TeleportPartyAsync/TeleportToPrivateServer variants. Covers TeleportOptions, reserved servers via ReserveServerAsync, the 50-player group limit, cross-experience restrictions, teleport data (and its client-spoofable security caveat), custom loading screens, DataStore handoff, and matchmaking with MessagingService/MemoryStoreService. Use whenever moving players between places, servers, or reserved servers.
