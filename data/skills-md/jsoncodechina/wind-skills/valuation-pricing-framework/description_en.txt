Used for valuation and pricing frameworks, focusing on valuation analysis, pricing logic, and investment decisions. This skill is primarily for answering user questions, writing reports, and drafting financial articles. The report generates extensive output and is not suited for simple conversational scenarios. Various information and data can be obtained using the wind.financial.data tool with appropriate keywords or keyword combinations. Users want to know how a company should be valued, what level its current valuation is at, why the market is willing to assign that valuation, and whether there is room for revaluation.
