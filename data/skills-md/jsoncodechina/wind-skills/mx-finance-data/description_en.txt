Based on the East Money database, it supports natural language queries of financial data, covering a variety of assets such as A‑shares, Hong Kong and US stocks, funds, and bonds, including real‑time quotes, company information, valuations, financial statements, etc. It can be used for investment research, trade backtesting, market monitoring, industry analysis, credit research, financial‑report auditing, asset allocation and other scenarios, catering to the diverse needs of institutions and individuals. The returned results include data descriptions and an XLSX file.
