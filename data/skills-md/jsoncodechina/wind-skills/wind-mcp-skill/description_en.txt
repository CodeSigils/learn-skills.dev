Wind WanDe MCP Data Bridge skill (v1.1.0, 6 servers / 19 tools). Routes by `server_type`: (1) `quote` market data (A-share/HK stock snapshots, daily/weekly/monthly K-lines, minute data); (2) `fund_data` fund data (profile/financials/holdings/performance/shareholders/company); (3) `stock_data` stock depth (profile/financial fundamentals/capital structure/events/technical indicators/risk); (4) `financial_docs` document RAG (announcements/financial news); (5) `economic_data` EDB macro + industry economic indicators; (6) `analytics_data` general NL → Wind data. Requires WIND_API_KEY (obtain from developer center at aimarket.wind.com.cn). Trigger scenarios: A-share/HK stock codes/K-lines/minute data, any fund dimension, stock financial reports/valuation, listed company announcements/financial news, macroeconomic data, cross-ticker comparison. **Excludes**: US/European/Japanese stocks, FX/futures order book, cryptocurrency, non-financial data.
