Used for post‑market review, focusing on daily recap, market research, and trading summary. This skill mainly serves scenarios such as answering user questions, writing reports, and drafting financial articles. The report output is extensive and not suited for simple conversational settings. Various information and data can be obtained using the wind.financial.data tool with appropriate keywords or keyword combinations. After market close, a rapid review of the entire day’s market is needed to understand what happened, which signals deserve attention, and how to act tomorrow.
