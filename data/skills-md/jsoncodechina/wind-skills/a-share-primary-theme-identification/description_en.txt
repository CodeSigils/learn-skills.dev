For identifying the main theme in the A‑share market, focusing on market structure, thematic cycles, and capital behavior. This skill is primarily used for answering user questions, writing reports, and drafting financial articles. The report generates a large amount of output and is not suitable for simple dialogue scenarios. Various information and data can be obtained using the wind.financial.data tool with appropriate keywords or keyword combinations. After the market opens, at midday, and after the close each day, users need to quickly know: what the market is actually trading today, what the true main theme is, where sentiment stands, and where to focus tomorrow.
