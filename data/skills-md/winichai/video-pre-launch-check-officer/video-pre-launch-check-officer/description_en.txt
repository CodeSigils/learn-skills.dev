Video Pre-Launch Check Officer. Used for preparing requirements, inventorying assets, confirming design specifications, breaking down storyboards, and conducting a pre-render review before video generation. It is used when a user wants to create a video, promotional clip, product demonstration, animated short, Douyin/YouTube content, or to modify the storyboard, pacing, shots, subtitles, voiceover, or transitions. Outputs a standardized video-spec.md and, before HyperFrames generates the video, creates a keyframe preview chart preview-board.md for user approval, reducing rework caused by direct rendering.
