A runtime‑independent design charter for designing, implementing, and procuring the harness of AI agents (agent loop, tool interface, context management, authorization, state, termination conditions, evaluation, observability). It is used in scenarios such as “design the agent platform/harness,” “choose between custom, SDK, or off‑the‑shelf solutions,” “determine how to handle compaction,” and “decide whether to make it multi‑agent.” Refer to the repository design in agent-native-project-design and the design on Mastra in mastra‑ai‑architecture‑rules.
