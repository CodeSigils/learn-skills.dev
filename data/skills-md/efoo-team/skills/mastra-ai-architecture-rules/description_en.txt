Used when designing, implementing, and refactoring AI services that use Mastra as the runtime. Define the separation of responsibilities for agent, workflow, tool, workspace, memory, RequestContext, workflow state, and persistent storage, and determine which parts can be delegated to the LLM and which should be rendered as definitive code. Apply the principles of single‑agent first, minimizing the workflow, keeping only references, IDs, and summaries in state, and introducing optional capabilities (workspace, memory, persistent storage, etc.) only after user approval when their necessity is confirmed. It is used in situations such as “design with Mastra,” “create a workflow,” “decide the boundary between agent and code,” “organize what should be stored in state,” and “determine whether memory or workspace is needed.”
