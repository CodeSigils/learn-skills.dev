Used when designing, implementing, and refactoring AI services that use Mastra as the runtime. Define the separation of responsibilities for agent, workflow, tool, workspace, memory, RequestContext, workflow state, and persistent storage, and determine which parts to entrust to the LLM and which to collapse into deterministic code. Apply the principles of single‑agent first, minimizing workflows, keeping only references, IDs, and summaries in state, and introducing optional capabilities (such as workspace, memory, persistent storage) only after user approval when their necessity is confirmed. It is used in situations like “design with Mastra,” “create a workflow,” “define the boundary between agent and code,” “organize what should be held in state,” and “determine whether memory or workspace is needed.”
