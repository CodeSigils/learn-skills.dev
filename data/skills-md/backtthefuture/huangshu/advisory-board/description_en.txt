Uncle Huang’s Private Board — a business-decision brain trust of 12 top-tier thinkers.
A structured private-board process lets differing mental models collide to forge the best decisions.

Trigger scenarios:
- User says “convene a private board,” “call in the brain trust,” or “help me decide.”
- User says “private board: [topic]” or “let the big shots weigh in on this.”
- User employs /private-board or /advisory-board command.
- User faces a major business decision needing multi-angle analysis.

Non-trigger scenarios:
- Simple operational questions (how to publish, how to format).
- Pure tech issues (code debugging).
- Everyday content creation (use article-writer / topic-partner).
- Personal emotional support (use life-coach).
