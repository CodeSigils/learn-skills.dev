Use for Git work in a workspace containing an `oms.yaml` (source repos vendored as submodules under `oms/`) whenever the repository scope is ambiguous — committing or pushing "everything" from the workspace root, interpreting an `oms status` pointer that has moved, debugging a push, or adding or removing a repo with `oms sync` or `oms unsync`. Establishes workspace state and root-versus-submodule scope before acting on Git.
