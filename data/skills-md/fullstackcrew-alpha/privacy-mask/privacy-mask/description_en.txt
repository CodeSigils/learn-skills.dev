Mask, redact, anonymize and censor sensitive information (PII) in screenshots and images — phone numbers, emails, IDs, API keys, crypto wallets, credit cards, passwords, and more. Uses OCR (Tesseract + RapidOCR) with 47 regex rules and optional NER (GLiNER) to detect private data and applies blur/fill redaction overlays. Supports GDPR and compliance workflows, secret detection, and data loss prevention (DLP). All processing runs 100% locally and offline — no data leaves your machine.
