Voxel-It — turn any photo into a high-quality Minecraft screenshot, keeping the HUMAN subject(s) fully photorealistic while the rest of the frame becomes detailed Minecraft block geometry, then cinematic-color-grade the whole frame to the photo's mood so the real person and the voxel world read as one image. A Step 0 router reads the photo and picks ONE of three input-driven modes: object-only (voxelize just the held/featured object + its tableware, keep the person AND background photoreal, float Minecraft loot-tag labels), background-only (voxelize the background/environment, keep the person and any incidental object photoreal, no tags), or object+background (voxelize everything except the person). Single still-image edit via one generate_image call. This skill absorbs the old minecraft-food and minecraft-world trends.
