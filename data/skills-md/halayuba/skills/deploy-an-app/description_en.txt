Take an Expo + Cloudflare Worker app that runs on the user's machine and put it into production on Cloudflare — provisioning D1, R2, KV, email, payments, secrets, custom domains; deploying once; proving the live site. Use when the user wants to deploy, ship, publish, launch or go live with an app; when they have finished building and ask how to put it online; or when an already-deployed app needs a database, a custom domain, an email domain, or an integration wired up properly. Covers preflight and stack detection, provisioning D1 and R2, wiring Clerk production keys and webhooks, Cloudflare Email Service and its DNS records, cron triggers and Queues, Stripe webhooks and keys, custom domains and DNS, secrets via wrangler, and a closing pass that checks the deployed site rather than taking the deploy's word for it.
