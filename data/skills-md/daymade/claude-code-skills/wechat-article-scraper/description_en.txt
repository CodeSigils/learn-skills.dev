Scrapes WeChat Official Account articles, extracts body text, images, and metadata, and outputs them as Markdown or JSON. Supports intelligent strategy routing (fast/adaptive/stable/reliable/zero_dep/jina_ai/history), bulk scraping of an account’s full historical posts, optional OG metadata, lazy-loaded image extraction, local image download, paragraph–image association, Sogou search discovery, interaction data extraction (reads, likes, “in-view” counts), and a modern web management interface. Use when you need to download/save WeChat articles, batch-archive an account’s content, extract WeChat article assets, or want a visual dashboard.
