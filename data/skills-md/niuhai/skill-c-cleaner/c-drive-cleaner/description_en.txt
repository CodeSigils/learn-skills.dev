AI-driven C‑drive space diagnostics and safe‑cleaning consultant. It uses NTFS actual allocation measurements, parent‑child directory deduplication, application‑level growth and post‑cleanup regeneration tracking, Windows update leftovers, seldom‑used software and fragmented space inventory to pinpoint why space is growing and why cleaning is ineffective. When a user reports insufficient C‑drive space, cleaning that doesn’t work, no increase in available space, cache regeneration, wants to locate rarely used programs/large files/hidden usage, migrate data, or review cleanup results, this skill is invoked.
