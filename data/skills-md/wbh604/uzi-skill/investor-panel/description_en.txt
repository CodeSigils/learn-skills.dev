A 50-investor jury of heavyweights. Given a stock’s dimensions.json and raw_data.json, each of the 50 investors scores it according to their own methodology and outputs a Pydantic Signal (signal/confidence/score/verdict/comment). Covers seven schools: classic value, growth investing, global macro/hedge, technical trend, Chinese value, A-share hot-money, and quantitative system. Triggered when the user asks “jury / what do the 50 big shots think / would so-and-so buy it / run a big-shot vote”.
