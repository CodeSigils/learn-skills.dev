50 investment expert jury panel. Given a stock's dimensions.json and raw_data.json, 50 investors each score according to their own methodology and output Pydantic Signal (signal/confidence/score/verdict/comment). Covers 7 major schools: Classic Value, Growth Investment, Macro Hedge, Technical Trend, China Value Investing, A-share Hot Money, and Quantitative System. Use when user requests "jury panel/what do 50 experts think/would someone buy/conduct an expert vote".
