Core workflow for in-depth individual stock analysis. Triggered when users request "deep analysis / comprehensive analysis / take a look / worth buying / DCF / institutional modeling / initiation of coverage / IC memo" and other stock research-related requests. Covers A-shares, Hong Kong stocks, and US stocks, producing 22-dimensional data + 51 quantitative reviews by industry experts + 6 types of institutional-grade valuation modeling (DCF/Comps/LBO/3-Stmt/Merger) + 7 types of research outputs (initiation of coverage/earnings interpretation/catalyst calendar/investment thesis tracking/morning call/quantitative screening/industry overview) + 6 decision-making methods (IC Memo/DD/Porter/Unit Economics/VCP/Rebalancing) + pig-butchering scam detection, ultimately generating Bloomberg-style HTML reports + social media shareable battle reports.
