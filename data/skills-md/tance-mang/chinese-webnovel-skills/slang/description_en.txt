Chinese internet buzzwords/memes, real-time lookup + usage that's not jarring. Used when a user wants to give a contemporary novel an online feel, wants to know which memes are trending on Douyin/Xiaohongshu (RedNote)/Weibo/Bilibili, asks whether a particular meme is still popular, or worries whether “adding a meme will be awkward/outdated.” It searches the web for current hot memes and, based on genre/character/platform, provides judgments on “whether to use it, which one to use, where to place it,” to avoid awkwardness and cheapness.
