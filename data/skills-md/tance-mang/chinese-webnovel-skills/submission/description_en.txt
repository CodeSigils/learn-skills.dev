Chinese online literature submission and manuscript acceptance diagnosis. Used when users want to submit or sign a contract, want to know whether their manuscript can pass editorial review, write a title, summary, and tags, conduct a self‑check of the “golden three chapters” for acceptance, record the reasons a particular submission was rejected, or ask “why wasn’t it signed / how can I improve the acceptance rate.” It follows platform signing standards for a health check, reads and writes submission records, and avoids repeating past reasons for rejection.
