Chinese web novel long-form continuity and logic check. Use this when a writer fears contradictions, collapse after many chapters, setting drift, wants to verify if the timeline is chaotic, character actions are reasonable, resources appear out of thin air, identities appear or disappear abruptly, injuries aren’t maintained, combat power is over‑leveled, or needs a logical health check for a volume or the whole book. It checks each chapter against six logical chains plus the timeline, identifies contradictions with previous text or records, and suggests corrections.
