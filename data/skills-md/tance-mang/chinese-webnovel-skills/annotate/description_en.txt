Chinese web novel text annotation and rhythm visualization. Used when users want to mark the positions of hooks, high points, climaxes, face‑slaps, or foreshadowing in the main text, view a chapter’s rhythm distribution, check the density of high points, or tag and suggest embellishing words (the intensifying terms favored in short, fast‑paced爽文). It outputs the annotated text together with a rhythm data table, allowing authors to view the data at a glance.
