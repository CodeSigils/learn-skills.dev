Chinese web novel co‑writing mode (Co‑Write). Use it when you want “human–AI collaboration” instead of handing entire chapters over to the AI, when you’re stuck on “what to write next sentence/paragraph,” when you want the AI to continue from what you’ve written, when you need several direction‑based versions (recommended + alternatives) to pick from, or when you only set the goal/conflict/emotion and let the AI generate the main text. Three tiers: Auto (automatic writing), Assist (assistive writing— I write a bit, AI fills in), Director (director mode— I set the direction, AI produces multiple versions).
