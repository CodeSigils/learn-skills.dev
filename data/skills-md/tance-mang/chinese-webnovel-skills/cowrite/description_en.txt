Co-Write mode for Chinese web novels. When users want "human‑AI co‑writing" instead of handing the whole chapter over to AI, get stuck on "what to write next sentence/paragraph," want AI to continue from what they wrote, need several version options at once (recommendations + alternatives) to pick from, or just set the goal/conflict/emotion and let AI generate the main text. Three levels: Auto (automatic writing), Assist (I write a bit, AI fills in), Director (I set the direction, AI produces multiple versions).
