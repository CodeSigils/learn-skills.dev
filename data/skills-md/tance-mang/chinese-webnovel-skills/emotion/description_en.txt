Chinese web novel emotional description (show, don’t tell). When a user wants to turn blunt statements like “I feel heartache,” “I’m in great pain,” or “He’s very angry” into palpable portrayals that let readers feel the emotion themselves—especially if the emotion feels too direct, empty, or unengaging, or when writing scenes of cruelty, moving moments, tension, and similar feelings—use facial expressions, actions, physiological reactions, and subtext to enact the emotion rather than stating it outright.
