Chinese online fiction emotional description (show, don’t tell). Use it when you need to turn blunt statements like “I feel heart‑ache,” “I’m in so much pain,” or “He’s furious” into vivid, sensory depictions that let readers experience the emotions themselves—especially if the feelings come across as too obvious, vague, or unimmersive, or when writing scenes of suffering, poignancy, tension, and similar moods. Convey the emotion through facial expressions, actions, physiological responses, and subtext rather than stating it outright.
