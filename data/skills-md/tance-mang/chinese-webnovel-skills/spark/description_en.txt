Chinese web novel inspiration recording and one-click manuscript creation. It’s used when a user comes up with an inspiration or idea they want to jot down quickly, wants to turn a specific idea from their inspiration notebook into an outline or draft, or says, “Just this one idea—help me write it out.” It supports fast note‑taking of inspirations, a full workflow from idea → outline → draft, and allows setting a total word count or per‑chapter word count with automatic chapter division.
