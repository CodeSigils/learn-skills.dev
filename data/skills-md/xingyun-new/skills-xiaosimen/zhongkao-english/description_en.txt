Junior high school English (grades 7 to 9) practice question generation and knowledge-point learning system. Based on the Foreign Language Teaching and Research Press (FLTRP) textbooks, it generates question types such as vocabulary discrimination, grammar selection, cloze passages, reading comprehension, sentence pattern transformation, and situational dialogues by grade and module, with each question accompanied by a knowledge-point card. Use when a user asks to generate middle school English practice questions, grammar exercises, reading comprehension, or cloze tests for high school entrance exam preparation.
