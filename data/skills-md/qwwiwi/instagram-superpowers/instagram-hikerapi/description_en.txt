Full tool for working with Instagram: account analytics via HikerAPI SaaS + downloading reels/posts via Cobalt API. Use when: (1) download a reel/video/photo from Instagram, (2) find the account's viral reels, (3) analyze a competitor (views, likes, comments, engagement), (4) manage a watchlist of accounts for monitoring, (5) download + transcribe video, (6) search by hashtags, locations. Triggers: “download reel”, “download from insta”, “analyze account”, “viral reels”, “top reels”, “watchlist”, “track account”, “insta competitors”, “engagement”, links to instagram.com/reel/, instagram.com/p/, instagram.com/stories/. NOT for: YouTube, Twitter, mass spam, auto‑likes, auto‑follow.
