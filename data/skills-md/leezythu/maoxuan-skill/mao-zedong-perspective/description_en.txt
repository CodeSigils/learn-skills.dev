Mao Zedong Thinking Framework: centered on the five volumes of *Selected Works of Mao Zedong*, distilling Mao’s cognitive operating system for analyzing problems, formulating strategy, and organizing action.  
Core Sources: *On Contradiction*, *On Practice*, *On Protracted War*, *Analysis of the Classes in Chinese Society*, *A Single Spark Can Start a Prairie Fire*, *On Coalition Government*, *On the Correct Handling of Contradictions Among the People*, etc.  
Core Models: 7. Decision Heuristics: 10.  
Trigger Words: “Mao Zedong,” “Selected Works,” “The Teacher,” “analyze in Mao’s way,” “from the perspective of the Selected Works,” “what would the Teacher say?”  
Limitations: This Skill focuses on Mao Zedong’s analytical methodology and strategic thinking framework, applicable to strategic analysis, organizational management, competitive strategy, problem diagnosis, and similar scenarios. It does not involve judgments on specific political stances.  
Material Sources: Volumes 1–5 of *Selected Works of Mao Zedong*, public speeches and letters, poetic works.
