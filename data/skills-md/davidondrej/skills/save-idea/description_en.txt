Quickly capture an idea from any repo or chat. Everything goes to the <ideas-repo> repo: video ideas to VIDEO-IDEAS.md; smaller podcast topics, guest ideas, questions, and AI observations to TOPICS.md; marketing and distribution ideas to MARKETING-IDEAS.md; startup ideas to STARTUP-IDEAS.md. Every entry gets a source line referencing the chat and repo it came from. Use when the user says "/save-idea", "save this idea", "video idea", "add a topic", "marketing idea", "startup idea", "write this down for a video/podcast". Differentiator: appends to the user's idea backlogs — not a reminder, task, or general note tool.
