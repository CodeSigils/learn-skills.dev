Pongfi Research は、あらゆる分野向けに設計されたクロスプラットフォームのソーシャル メディア リサーチ スキルです。 By dynamically discovering and invoking TikHub MCP / public social data tools, it conducts trend discovery, sub‑track segmentation, competitor analysis, account auditing, viral content deconstruction, comment demand mining, content gap identification, cross‑platform comparison, and data‑driven topic selection for industries, categories, brands, products, personalities, accounts, or topics. It applies to public social platform data from Douyin, TikTok, Xiaohongshu, Instagram, YouTube, X, Weibo, Bilibili, Reddit, Kuaishou, Zhihu, WeChat, LinkedIn, Threads, and other platforms.
