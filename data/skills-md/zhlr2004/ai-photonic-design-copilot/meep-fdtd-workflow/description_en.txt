Guides end-to-end Meep FDTD simulations with PyMeep on Linux or WSL using Conda. Covers requirements, units, geometry, materials, sources, PML, periodicity, monitors, run control, normalization, convergence, HDF5/NumPy output, MPI, and diagnosis. Use when asked to design, generate, run, validate, or troubleshoot a Meep or PyMeep electromagnetic simulation.
