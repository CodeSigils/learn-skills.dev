Skill for customizing a resume based on the job description (JD). Use it when the user mentions “JD,” “job description,” “position matching,” “modify resume for…,” “submit,” “job search,” “recruitment requirements,” “Job Description,” or provides a specific job link/text requesting resume changes. Supports uploading or pasting JD text, automatically performs keyword matching and content optimization, and generates a tailored HTML and PDF version of the resume for that role based on the existing resume.
