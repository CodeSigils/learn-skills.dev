Skills for customizing a résumé based on a job description (JD). Use it when the user mentions “JD,” “job description,” “position match,” “revise résumé for…,” “submission,” “job hunting,” “recruitment requirements,” “Job Description,” or provides a specific job link/text requesting résumé edits. Supports uploading or pasting JD text, automatically performs keyword matching and content optimization, and generates a customized HTML and PDF version of the résumé for the role based on the existing résumé.
