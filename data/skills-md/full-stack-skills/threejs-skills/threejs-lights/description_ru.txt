освещение Three.js: AmbientLight, HemisphereLight, DirectionalLight, PointLight, SpotLight, RectAreaLight, LightProbe, IESSpotLight, ProjectorLight, подтипы теней и дополнительные помощники, такие как RectAreaLightUniformsLib, RectAreaLightTexturesLib, LightProbeGenerator, TiledLighting.
