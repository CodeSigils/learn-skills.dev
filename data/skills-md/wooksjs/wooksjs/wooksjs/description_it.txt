Usa questa abilità quando lavori con wooksjs monorepo o qualsiasi pacchetto @wooksjs. Copre @wooksjs/event-core (EventContext, slot key/cached/cachedBy, defineWook composables, defineEventKind, propagazione AsyncLocalStorage, useRouteParams, useEventId, useLogger), @wooksjs/event-http (createHttpApp, routing HTTP, useRequest, useHeaders, useCookies, useUrlParams, useAuthorization, useAccept, useResponse, HttpResponse, HttpError, prepareTestHttpContext), @wooksjs/event-cli (createCliApp, routing dei comandi, useCliOptions, useCliOption, useCliHelp, useAutoHelp), @wooksjs/event-ws (WooksWs, onMessage, onConnect, onDisconnect, useWsConnection, useWsMessage, useWsRooms, useWsServer, WsRoomManager, WsBroadcastTransport, WsError), @wooksjs/event-wf (createWfApp, passaggi e flussi del flusso di lavoro, useWfState, pausa/riprendi, condizioni, loop, gestori di stringhe, StepRetriableError, outlet), @wooksjs/ws-client (createWsClient, WsClient, invia, chiama, iscriviti, attivo, riconnessione, RPC, push listener, WsClientError).
