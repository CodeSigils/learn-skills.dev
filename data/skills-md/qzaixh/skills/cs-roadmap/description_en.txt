Turn the "too large to fit into a single feature" requirement into a complete pre‑planning: concept design + interface contract + sub‑feature breakdown list, stored in `.codestable/roadmap/{slug}/`. There are two modes: new and update. Triggers: the user says “I want an X system,” “Help me break down this requirement,” “Create a roadmap,” or discovers during the feature‑design phase that the requirement is too big.
