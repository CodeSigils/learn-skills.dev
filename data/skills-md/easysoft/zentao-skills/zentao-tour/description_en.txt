Use a relaxed, chat-style approach to get users started with ZenTao and zentao-cli. Let them follow their own role—product manager, project manager, tester, developer, or executive—while talking and actually working in a real ZenTao environment, so they get familiar with creating, reading, updating, and deleting items in modules like Product, Story, Plan, Task, Bug, and Test Case, as well as the status transitions. Activate this skill when a user first meets ZenTao, wants to try zentao-cli, wonders what ZenTao can do, or explicitly says “show me ZenTao / give me a ZenTao tour / let me experience ZenTao.”
