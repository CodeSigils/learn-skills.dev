Embedded bootloader management and firmware upgrade tool. Supports STM32 DfuSe (USB DFU), UF2, OpenBLT, MCUboot, and custom bootloader protocols. Performs firmware flashing via dfu-util, uf2conv, stm32flash, serial YMODEM/XMODEM, etc. Automatically triggers when the user mentions bootloader, DFU, DfuSe, UF2, firmware upgrade, online upgrade, dfu-util, stm32flash, OpenBLT, MCUboot, YMODEM, or IAP. Also compatible with explicit /bootloader calls.
