An orchestrator skill that refines Korean text written by AI (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, etc.) to make it read like it was written by a person. It detects and classifies over 40 AI “t” patterns across ten major categories—such as translationese, excessive English quotations, mechanical parallel structures, idioms, overuse of passive voice, overuse of conjunctions, rhythmic uniformity, overuse of emojis/bullets, etc.—and rewrites the style, rhythm, and expressions into natural Korean without altering a single character of the content. Triggers — “remove AI t”, “make AI-like writing natural”, “GPT/ChatGPT style”, “fix AI translationese”, “refine as if written by a human”, “AI refinement”, “remove ChatGPT t”, “Korean AI detection & refinement”, “make AI writing sound human”, “remove translationese”, “refine text with many English quotes”, “eliminate AI t”, “humanizer”, “humanize Korean”, “AI detector bypass Korean”. Follow‑up tasks — “redo only specific categories”, “adjust refinement intensity”, “change genre”, “only this paragraph”, “second‑pass refinement” are all handled by this skill. Simple spelling and typo corrections are done directly, translation uses a translation skill, and rewrites that involve adding or deleting content require a separate authoring skill.
