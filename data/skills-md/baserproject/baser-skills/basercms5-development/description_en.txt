Base skill that defines the common rules (environment, directory structure, naming conventions, log/error handling, prohibition of core hacking, server constraints) for baserCMS 5 series (CakePHP 5.0.5 series) + jQuery projects. Refer to it when checking the common policies and constraints of 5‑series projects such as “development with baserCMS 5,” “verify the configuration and conventions of a 5‑series project,” “placement under htdocs/plugins,” “log output with CakeLog,” “core hacking is prohibited in htdocs/vendor/baserproject and cakephp,” etc. The implementation patterns for plugin development/modification (Table/Entity, controller, admin‑screen forms, events, Vue/JS, etc.) are documented in **basercms5-plugin-development**, and theme development/modification (layout/element, widgets, mail‑form templates, assets) are documented in **basercms5-theme-development** as the official references. For baserCMS 4 series development refer to **basercms4-development**, and for upgrades from 4 to 5 refer to the **basercms4-to-5-upgrade** skill.
