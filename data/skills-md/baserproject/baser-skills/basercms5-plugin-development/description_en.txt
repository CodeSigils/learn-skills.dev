A collection of canonical patterns for developing or modifying plugins for baserCMS 5 (based on CakePHP 5). Refer to items such as “Create plugin,” “bake with bc_plugin,” “Add admin screen,” “Writing Table/Entity,” “Declare associations in initialize,” “validationDefault,” “Distinguishing fetchTable and TableRegistry usage,” “Writing BcAdminForm / control(),” “Search form element,” “Control id/name generation rules and JS integration,” “Event listeners (e.g., BcModelEventListener),” “Registering adminNavigation,” “setting.php / config.php,” “Registering BcShortCode,” “paginate($query),” “Redirect as return,” “Saving belongsToMany with _ids,” “BcKeyValue,” “Re‑entrancy guard in afterSave,” “$.bcUtil.adminBaseUrl,” “ICU patterns for Time::format,” “RouteBuilder in routes.php,” etc. The common rules regarding environment, directories, naming, prohibition of core hacks, and so on are documented in basercms5-development; theme development is in basercms5-theme-development; migration from 4‑series to 5‑series is in basercms-plugin-4-to-5-upgrade; and test execution and test infrastructure setup are in basercms-unittest.
