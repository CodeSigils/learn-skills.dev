A collection of development rules for baserCMS version 4 (based on CakePHP 2.10) + jQuery projects. It is consulted when checking coding policies, directory structure, naming conventions, log/error handling, handling of read‑only files, and server constraints for baserCMS 4 projects, covering topics such as “develop with baserCMS 4,” “modify version 4 plugins,” “write CakePHP 2 code,” “Controllers/Models/Views under app/Plugin,” “theme Elements/Layouts,” “use BcBaserHelper/BcFormHelper/BcHtmlHelper,” “output logs with CakeLog,” and “core hacks prohibited in lib/Baser and lib/Cake.” For baserCMS 5 development refer to basercms5-development, and for upgrading from 4 to 5 refer to the basercms4-to-5-upgrade skill.
