Use Linear as the source of truth for engineering work in projects that explicitly expose a `linear` MCP server. Use whenever a task names a Linear issue, asks to plan or track work in Linear, needs a durable planning or review artifact, or needs to create, claim, update, review, or close tracked work. Confirm the authenticated actor before writes, preserve the separation between Orchestrator, Planner, Builder, Reviewer, and Owner, and never introduce Linear into a project that has not opted in.
