Batch fetch a list of URLs and generate a visual board. When users need to: (1) paste a set of URLs for AI to browse and organize, (2) collect design inspiration/reference images/product screenshots, (3) compile titles, images, and excerpts from multiple pages into an HTML waterfall board, (4) use it for competitor research or data investigation. Supports both agent-browser real-browser fetching (able to render SPAs) and WebFetch lightweight fetching modes, automatically detecting available tools and selecting the optimal solution. Generates a card-style board and opens it automatically in the browser. Websites that cannot be fetched will be marked with the reason for failure.
