Nucleo CommunityToolkit.Mvvm (MVVM Toolkit): generatori di sorgenti ([ObservableProperty], [RelayCommand], [NotifyPropertyChangedFor], [NotifyCanExecuteChangedFor], [NotifyDataErrorInfo]), classi di base (ObservableObject / ObservableValidator / ObservableRecipient), comandi (RelayCommand / AsyncRelayCommand) e convalida. Competenze complementari: mvvm-toolkit-messenger per pub/sub, mvvm-toolkit-di per il cablaggio Microsoft.Extensions.DependencyInjection. Funziona su WPF, WinUI 3, MAUI, Uno e Avalonia.
