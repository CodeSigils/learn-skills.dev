Yichen's personal unified web and social platform search orchestrator. It is used for keyword-driven real-time public web retrieval, parallel batch searching, vertical searches such as finance, academia, law, and security, as well as public content discovery on platforms like GitHub, WeChat public accounts, Xiaohongshu, Douyin, Toutiao, Twitter/X, Bilibili, YouTube, Xiao Universe, and others; it can verify the original text and perform lightweight enrichment on the candidates obtained in a search, and unify results from different back‑ends into candidate records. AnySearch handles public web, batch, and vertical searches, while the platform‑native/OpenCLI serves only as the corresponding platform adapter. It is not used to read or download user‑provided known URLs, URL files, or confirmed candidates; such tasks use yichen‑content‑archive.
