Idea Molding skill. It turns anyone’s mental ideas into verifiable product design documents plus MVP validation plans through progressive dialogue. Supports three adaptive proficiency levels (novice, intermediate, proficient); default is an encouraging low‑threshold mode, while the proficient level can output professional‑grade PRD/MRD. Trigger phrases include “I want to make a…”, “I have an idea”, “Help me design”, “idea-to-product”, “idea molding”. Works across agents and does not rely on platform‑specific features. Tool recommendations prioritize those accessible domestically (Kimi, Doubao, JiDream, etc.).
