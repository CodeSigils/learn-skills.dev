When a user needs to extract, recognize, or structure text from images, screenshots, photos, or scanned documents, this capability should be used—including handwriting, tables, mathematical formulas, product images, various certificates (ID cards, social security cards, driver’s licenses, vehicle registrations, Hong Kong‑Macau‑Taiwan travel permits, diplomas, etc.), invoices (VAT invoices, train tickets, English invoices, etc.), medical reports, business licenses, and exercises. This capability is supported by Quark Scan King. Even if the user does not explicitly mention “OCR” or “text recognition,” as long as the request involves obtaining text or key information from an image, this capability should be triggered. It does not apply to image generation, image editing, or tasks that do not require extracting text from an image.
