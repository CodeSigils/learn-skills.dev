Case number identification and tracing, used to extract case numbers from documents, memos, chat records, or other long texts and trace back to case clues. Use when: the user already has a piece of text and wants to identify the case numbers within it, batch-extract case numbers, or verify the case information corresponding to a case number. NOT for: retrieving cases by dispute topic, legal article identification, regulatory topic search. Requirement: do not fabricate case numbers, court names, or dates; everything must be based on the current MCP output.
