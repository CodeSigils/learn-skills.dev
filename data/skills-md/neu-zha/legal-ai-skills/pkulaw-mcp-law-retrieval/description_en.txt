Peking University Law Treasure's native MCP regulation/precise article search skill. This machine is currently configured with `law-keyword` (search laws and regulations—keyword) and `fatiao` (precise article lookup—keyword); do not assume that older MCPs such as `law-semantic`, `law-recognition`, etc., are available. Use when: the user explicitly wants to find statutes, normative documents, judicial interpretations, supporting provisions, or wishes to locate relevant regulatory materials around a legal issue; if the statute name and article number are known, the precise article search should be prioritized. NOT for: case search, case number identification, web login, clicking on web detail pages, copying web URLs; web fallback should be redirected to the user's own `pkulaw-legal-search`. The Law Treasure MCP must be configured and subscribed; fabricating statute names, document numbers, article numbers, or legal status without a search basis is prohibited.
