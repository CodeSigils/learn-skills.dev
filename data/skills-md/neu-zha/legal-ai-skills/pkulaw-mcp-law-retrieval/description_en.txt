PKU Law Treasure’s native MCP regulation/precise article retrieval skill. This machine currently has `law-keyword` (search laws and regulations – keyword) and `fatiao` (precise article lookup – keyword) configured, and you must not assume that older MCPs such as `law-semantic`, `law-recognition`, etc., are available.  

Use when: the user explicitly wants to find regulations, normative documents, judicial interpretations, supporting bases, or wishes to first locate relevant regulatory materials around a legal issue; if the regulation name and article number are known, the precise article search should be prioritized.  

NOT for: case search, case‑number identification, web‑login, clicking on web detail pages, copying web URLs; web fallback should be redirected to the user’s own `pkulaw-legal-search`.  

Must have configured and subscribed to the Law Treasure MCP; prohibited from fabricating regulation names, document numbers, article numbers, or validity status without a search basis.
