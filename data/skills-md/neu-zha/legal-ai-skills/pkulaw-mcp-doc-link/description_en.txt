Legal Treasure Hyperlink Enhancement, used to convert references to statutes and cases in legal documents, replies, memoranda, or research materials into traceable links. Use when: the user already has existing text and wants to enhance it with clickable, traceable delivery versions. NOT for: retrieving new statutes, retrieving new cases, precise statutory queries, verification of statutory citations. The links and referenced entities must come from the current tool's output and must not be manually assembled fake links.
