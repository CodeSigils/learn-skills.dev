Legal Treasure Hyperlink Enhancement, used to convert statutory and case citations in legal documents, replies, memoranda, or research materials into traceable links.  
Use when: the user already has existing text and wants to enhance it into a clickable, traceable deliverable version.  
NOT for: searching for new statutes, searching for new cases, precise statute queries, or statute citation verification.  
The links and citation entities must come from the current tool's output; manual assembly of fake links is not allowed.
