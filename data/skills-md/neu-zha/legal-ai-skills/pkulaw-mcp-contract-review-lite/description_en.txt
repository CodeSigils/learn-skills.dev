Single-contract clause compliance initial screening workflow, used to quickly organize a contract into an actionable risk ledger, covering legal provision identification, clause source tracing, and citation verification. Use when: lawyers or legal staff need to conduct a first-round review of an individual contract, identify risks before approval, or perform an initial screening before template iteration. NOT for: bulk contract uniform screening, comprehensive legal due diligence, formal legal opinions, or litigation strategy recommendations. Require a clear label of “Initial Screening Result,” and high‑risk and uncertain items must be manually reviewed.
