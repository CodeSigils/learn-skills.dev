A compliance preliminary screening workflow for a single contract clause, used to quickly organize a contract into an actionable risk ledger, covering legal provision identification, clause traceability, and citation verification.  
Use when: lawyers or legal staff need to conduct the first-round review of a single contract, identify risks before approval, or perform an initial screening before template iteration.  
Not for: batch contract uniform screening, comprehensive legal due diligence, formal legal opinions, or litigation strategy recommendations.  
Require clear labeling of “Preliminary Screening Results,” and high‑risk and uncertain items must be manually reviewed.
