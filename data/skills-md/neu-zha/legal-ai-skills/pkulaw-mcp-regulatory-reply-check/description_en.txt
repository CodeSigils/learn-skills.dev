Regulatory response letters and explanatory material source verification workflow, used to organize the legal citations in response letters, inquiry replies, and explanatory materials into verifiable, traceable, and transferable verification drafts. Use when: lawyers, legal affairs, compliance, board office, or regulatory response support teams need to check before external release or approval whether citations are accurate, complete, and traceable. NOT for: pure statutory research, drafting complete specialized legal opinions, and lightweight scenarios that do not require delivery‑level citation verification. Requirement: first lock the verification scope, then trace and verify each citation individually; unverified citations must not be written as definitive bases.
