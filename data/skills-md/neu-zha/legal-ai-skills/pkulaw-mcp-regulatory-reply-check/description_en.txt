Regulatory response letter and explanatory material citation verification workflow, used to organize the regulatory citations in response letters, inquiry replies, and explanatory materials into verifiable, traceable, and transferable verification drafts. Use when: lawyers, legal counsel, compliance, board office, or regulatory response support teams need to check whether citations are accurate, complete, and traceable before external communication or approval. NOT for: pure statutory subject research, drafting full special legal opinions, or lightweight scenarios that do not require deliverable‑level citation verification. Requirements: first lock in the verification scope, then trace and verify each citation; citations that have not been verified must not be written as definitive bases.
