Statute identification and tracing, used to recognize potential legal citations from contracts, letters, replies, memoranda, or other long texts, and provide source clues for further verification. Use when: the user already has a piece of text and wants to automatically identify the statutes, article numbers, or citation expressions mentioned. NOT for: legal topic search, precise full‑text queries for known statutes and article numbers, case search. It is required to prohibit fabricating statute names, article numbers, and tracing links; the identification results must be based on the current MCP response.
