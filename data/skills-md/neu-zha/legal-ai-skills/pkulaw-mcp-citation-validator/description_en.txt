Legal provision citation verification and correction, used to check whether the legal citations already written in drafts, Q&A, memoranda, or contract review records are accurate, consistent, and traceable. Use when: the user already has a piece of text and wants to confirm whether the names of statutes, article numbers, or citation expressions are reliable. NOT for: thematic retrieval of statutes, case retrieval, case number identification, and pure information exploration before forming citation text. Requirement: based only on the current verification, return a conclusion of “hit / miss / needs review”, and do not write unverified content as confirmed evidence.
