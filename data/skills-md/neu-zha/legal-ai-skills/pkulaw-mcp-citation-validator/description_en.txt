Statutory citation verification and correction, used to check whether the statutory citations already written in drafts, Q&A, memos, or contract review records are accurate, consistent, and traceable. Use when: the user already has a piece of text and wants to confirm whether the regulation names, article numbers, or citation expressions are reliable. NOT for: thematic retrieval of statutes, case retrieval, case number identification, and pure information exploration before a citation text is formed. The requirement is to provide a conclusion of “Hit / Miss / Needs Review” based solely on the current verification, and not to present unverified content as confirmed evidence.
