Class-case retrieval report and case-handling memorandum workflow, used to compress case searches into a memorandum text that can be discussed, forwarded, and audited, including case samples, case-number verification, and link enrichment. Use when: lawyers, dispute‑resolution teams, or compliance‑investigation teams need to quickly produce a standard deliverable of “key points of similar cases + verifiable clues + delivery‑version memorandum”. NOT for: situations where only a single case’s original text is needed, only a case list is compiled, or no comparative analysis is required. Key information must be traceable to the current MCP response, to avoid unfounded judgments based on presumed bias.
