Case similarity search report and case memorandum workflow, used to compress case searches into discussion‑ready, shareable, and verifiable memorandum texts, including case samples, case‑number verification, and link enrichment. Use when: lawyers, dispute‑resolution teams, or compliance investigation teams need to quickly produce a standard deliverable of “key points of similar cases + verifiable clues + delivery‑ready memorandum.” Not for: situations where only a single case’s original text is needed, only a case list is compiled, or no comparative analysis is required. Requirement: key information must be traceable to the current MCP output, avoiding unfounded inferences about judicial tendencies.
