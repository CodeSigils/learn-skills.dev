Batch contract compliance preliminary screening workflow, used to quickly and uniformly screen contracts of the same template or type, producing an actionable batch risk ledger. Use when: corporate legal, procurement compliance, and risk control operations need to handle multiple contracts simultaneously, identifying high‑risk clauses, citation errors, and missing bases. NOT for: detailed clause‑by‑clause review of a single contract, full legal opinions, litigation strategy analysis. Requirement: clearly label the “Batch Preliminary Screening Results”; high‑risk items and items not flagged by the system must be manually reviewed.
