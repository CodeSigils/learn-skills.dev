Batch contract compliance preliminary screening workflow, used for rapid unified screening of contracts with the same template or same type, outputting an executable batch risk ledger. Use when: corporate legal, procurement compliance, risk control operations need to process multiple contracts simultaneously, identifying high‑risk clauses, citation errors, and missing bases. NOT for: detailed line‑by‑line review of a single contract, complete legal opinions, litigation strategy analysis. Requires clearly marking “Batch Preliminary Screening Results”; high‑risk items and items not flagged by the system must be manually reviewed.
