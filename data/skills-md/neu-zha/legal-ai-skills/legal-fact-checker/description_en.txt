Legal Output Fact-Checking Skill. This skill is triggered when the user needs to write, edit, or analyze any legal document (legal papers, case analysis, statutory interpretation, contract review, legal opinion, law school assignments, etc.). Core principle: All legal output must be based on factual evidence; the compilation of cases, statutes, or facts is prohibited. Trigger words: legal document, case analysis, statutory analysis, contract review, legal opinion, law school assignment, case retrieval, statutory verification, fact-checking, haunted house case, contract dispute, civil law assignment, legal writing, legal writing, fact check, legal output.
