Retrieve judicial case and judgment document data. Currently, this system only subscribes to `case-keyword` (judicial case retrieval – keyword) and must not assume that `case-semantic`, `case-number`, and other legacy MCPs are available. Use when: the user explicitly wants to find similar cases, judgment samples, or leads in judgment documents, or wishes to first see how a court has handled a particular dispute issue. NOT for: regulation retrieval, precise full‑text queries of known statutes, case‑number identification and tracing, or tasks that directly summarize “how courts usually rule” without prior searching. The Fangbao MCP must be configured and subscribed; fabricating case numbers, court names, judgment dates, or key rulings without a search basis is prohibited.
