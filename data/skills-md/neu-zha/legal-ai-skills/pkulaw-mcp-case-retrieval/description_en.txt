Search judicial cases and judgment documents. Currently this system is subscribed only to `case-keyword` (keyword-based judicial case search) and must not assume that `case-semantic`, `case-number`, or other older MCPs are available. Use when: the user explicitly wants to find similar cases, judgment samples, clues from judgment documents, or wishes to see how the court handled a particular dispute issue first. NOT for: statutory searches, precise full‑text queries of known legal provisions, case‑number identification and tracing, or tasks that directly summarize “how courts usually rule” without performing a search. The FaBao MCP must be configured and subscribed; fabricating case numbers, court names, judgment dates, or judgment points without a search basis is prohibited.
