Labor employment response draft workflow, used to organize labor law and employment management issues into short answer texts that are verifiable, traceable, and shareable. Use when: lawyers, legal counsel, HRBPs, or compliance staff need to reply to business units, management, or clients about labor employment issues and want to output “conclusion + basis + risk warning + points for review”. NOT for: complete labor‑specific due diligence, full‑case arbitration or litigation strategy design, and other matters better suited for general legal responses or non‑labor issues. Requirement: first supplement the factual premises, then conduct research and reach a conclusion; unverified specific statutes must not be written as definitive basis.
