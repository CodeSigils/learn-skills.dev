General legal response draft workflow, used to organize retrieved statutes, cases, and citation verification results into a response text that can be reviewed, forwarded, and further edited. Use when: lawyers, legal counsel, compliance, or legal operations need to reply to clients, business units, leaders, or internal groups, and the question does not clearly belong to an already separately modeled vertical scenario. NOT for: pure information exploration, tasks that only require a retrieval list and do not need to produce a response draft; or when a more suitable vertical skill already exists (e.g., labor employment response). Requirements: first supplement key facts, then retrieve conclusions; unverified specific statutes must not be written as definitive bases.
