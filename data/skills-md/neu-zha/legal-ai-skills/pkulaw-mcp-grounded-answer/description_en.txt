General legal response draft workflow, used to organize the retrieved statutes, cases, and citation verification results into a response text that can be reviewed, forwarded, and further edited. Use when: lawyers, legal counsel, compliance, or legal operations need to reply to clients, business units, leaders, or internal groups, and the question does not clearly belong to a specific vertical scenario that has been modeled separately. NOT for: pure information exploration, tasks that only require a list of references, or tasks that do not need to produce a draft response; and when a more suitable vertical skill already exists (e.g., labor employment response). Requirement: first fill in the key facts, then retrieve the conclusions; unverified specific legal provisions must not be written as definitive bases.
