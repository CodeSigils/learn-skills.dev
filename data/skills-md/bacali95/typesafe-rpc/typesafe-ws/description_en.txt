Type-safe WebSocket subscriptions library for Node/TS, companion to typesafe-rpc. Defines WsSchema (entity/operation/subscription handler), createWsHandler (server), createWsServer (typed cross-connection push handle for other backend code), createWsClient (client), Route and middlewares for subscriptions. Use when editing typesafe-ws, adding subscription operations, wiring a WebSocket server or client, pushing data into subscriptions from outside a handler, or implementing subscription handlers and middlewares in this repo.
