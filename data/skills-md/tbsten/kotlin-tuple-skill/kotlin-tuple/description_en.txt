Generates type-safe Tuple utilities (Tuple0–Tuple20) for Kotlin and Kotlin Multiplatform projects. Creates Tuple data classes, tupleOf factories, toList conversion, KSerializer for kotlinx.serialization, type-safe awaitAll, and allNotNullOrNull in one go. Use when requested: "add Tuple", "generate tupleOf", "type-safe Tuple", "type-safe awaitAll", "add allNotNullOrNull", "await multiple Deferred with type safety", "null-check multiple nullable values at once". For Kotlin/KMP projects where Pair/Triple is not enough for multi-element grouping.
