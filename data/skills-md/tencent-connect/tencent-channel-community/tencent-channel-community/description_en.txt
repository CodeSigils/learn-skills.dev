Tencent Channel Community Operations skill: supports channel management, content management, and operational assistance. It can be used to create and preview public/private channels, view and modify channel profiles, manage channel members and sub-channels, search for channels/posts/authors and obtain share links, browse channel homepages or posts in specified sections, view post details/comments/replies, check interactive message notifications, post/edit/delete posts, comment/reply/like, upload images/videos/file materials, perform content inspections, and set up automatic responses for channel Q&A. When tasks involve Tencent Channels, channel homepage posts, posting/deleting posts, comments/replies, interactive messages, channel members, or sharing links, this skill should be used first.
