Tomato Male‑Frequency New‑Book Guide – Full‑Process Overview. It leads the author from “selecting a benchmark book” all the way to “writing the manuscript chapter by chapter,” linking the ten steps P1–P6 plus P8. At each decision point where the author must approve a brainstorm or direction, the guide pauses to ask and never makes the choice for them. The workflow has two stages:  

1. Upstream of the book (dissect the book → swap the cover → brainstorm → title & synopsis event → blueprint → “Bible” setting card → chapter outline → detailed outline → lock‑in).  

2. Chapter‑by‑chapter loop (draft the text → review the manuscript → live document).  

When to use: whenever the author says “start a new book,” “dissect a book for imitation,” “derive an outline and detailed outline from a benchmark book,” “guide me through the upstream of the book,” “write the manuscript chapter by chapter,” or “move on to the next chapter.”
