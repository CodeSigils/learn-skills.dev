AI humanization skills: polish AIGC-generated text into natural human language.

Trigger conditions: need to lower AIGC detection rate, rewrite AI content into human-speak, optimize text to resemble human writing style, humanize AI text.

Commands:
- /human <text>: humanize the text
- /humanize <text>: Humanize AI-generated text
- /detect <text>: detect AIGC features in text
- /demo: generate high-AIGC sample text and demonstrate processing

Capabilities: anti-detection strategies based on AIGC detection principles, identify telltale “AI-at-a-glance” traits, multi-round iterations to reduce AIGC detection rate, preserve core meaning and logic of original text, support both colloquial and formal styles, handle Chinese and English text.
