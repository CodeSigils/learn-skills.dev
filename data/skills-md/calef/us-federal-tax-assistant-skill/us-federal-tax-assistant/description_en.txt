Use this skill when helping users prepare US federal tax returns. Triggers include: any mention of 'tax return', '1040', 'tax filing', 'IRS forms', W-2, 1099, or requests to help with taxes. Use for analyzing tax documents, identifying required forms, filling out tax form PDFs, creating document checklists from prior year returns, and auditing completed returns. Do NOT use for tax advice, tax planning, or state tax returns (which vary significantly by jurisdiction).
