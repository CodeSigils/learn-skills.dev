使用事件风暴和 CQRS 思想进行纯 DDD 领域建模。Use this skill when Codex needs to analyze business requirements, evolve a persistent domain model, identify actors and multi-role collaboration, domain events, commands, policies, aggregates, domain services, read models, produce structured Markdown or optional Mermaid/PlantUML diagrams, and review designs that may be CRUD, database, package-structure, or DDD-terminology driven instead of problem-domain driven. Especially use for CRUD-looking admin requirements such as company, department, position, employee, account, role, or permission management that need behavior-first modeling instead of flat noun aggregates.
