OpenSpec-flavored end-to-end Jira bug-fix workflow that persists root cause, behavior change, fix plan, verification, and archive into OpenSpec artifacts (openspec/changes/<name>/, archived into openspec/specs/) instead of leaving them only in chat context or Jira comments. Use when a Jira issue needs long-term behavioral-contract traceability, team review, or auditability. Do NOT use for a quick fix needing no traceability — use jira-fix-workflow instead.
