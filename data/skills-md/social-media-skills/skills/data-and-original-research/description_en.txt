The original-research / data-study content type — turn proprietary data, a survey, a public-dataset analysis, or an experiment into the most linkable and AI-citable asset you can publish. Use when someone wants to build authority with original data, run a "state of X" survey or industry study, turn proprietary/customer data into a publishable stat, or get cited by journalists and AI search (GEO). Uses the PROVE framework. Reads brand-profile + audience-research first. The agent designs the study (question, method, analysis plan) and frames the findings (headline stat, report, social cuts); the human/tool gathers the real data; WoopSocial publishes the finished cuts. Feeds ai-search-optimization + social-seo, the format writers, and infographic-and-data-viz. NEVER fabricates data, stats, or methodology; discloses method + limits. Distinct from educational-content-and-how-to (existing knowledge), analytics-and-reporting (internal performance), competitor-analysis, and trend-jacking.
