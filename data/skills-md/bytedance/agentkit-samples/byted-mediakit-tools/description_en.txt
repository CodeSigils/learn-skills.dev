Volcano Engine AI MediaKit is an audio and video processing toolkit that provides capabilities including video understanding, audio extraction, video editing, audio and video splicing, image quality enhancement, text-to-video generation, audio and video synthesis, as well as local flipping, speed adjustment, subtitles, watermarks, and transcoding. This Skill must be invoked when users mention needs such as audio editing, video editing, audio and video splicing, text-to-video generation, audio extraction, image quality enhancement, video understanding, audio and video synthesis, media cropping, video flipping, speed adjustment, adding subtitles, adding watermarks, or transcoding. When users require video understanding, the host agent must automatically parse the user's specific requirements as the prompt parameter, while also passing in the video URL and fps parameters; max_frames is an optional parameter.
