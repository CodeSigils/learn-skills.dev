Synthesize text into speech (TTS). Uses the Volcano Engine Doubao Speech Synthesis API, supporting streaming synthesis, multiple voices, speed/pitch/volume adjustment, Markdown filtering, and LaTeX formula reading. Use this skill when users need to convert text to speech, generate audio readings, voice-overs, narrations, broadcasts, or mention “text-to-speech,” “TTS,” “speech synthesis,” “reading aloud,” or “voice-over.”
