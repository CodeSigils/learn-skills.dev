Volcano Engine Market Insights Assistant. Helps users obtain publicly available content data about brands across major social platforms and media channels, uses AI to filter out the information truly worth attention, and uncovers potential business opportunity leads. Activate this skill whenever the user mentions any of the following scenarios: wanting to know if anyone online has recently discussed their brand or product, wishing to check competitors’ latest social-media activities, seeking to understand the buzz and trends around a certain topic or event online, wanting regular industry news and hot content related to their business, planning to feed filtered content data into their own systems for deeper analysis, needing to view monitoring tasks under their account or pull data for a specific task, wanting to follow online discussions on hot topics such as the latest Middle-East situation, tariffs, trade wars, or large AI models, looking to query business-lead information to obtain ACOR assessments, competitive analyses, or company profiles, or mentioning Volcano Engine Market Insight, Volcengine Insight, PullPost, ListCustomSubsTask, or QueryClueInfo. Even if the user does not explicitly say “market insights,” trigger this skill whenever the need involves brand-mention tracking, industry-trend awareness, hot-event online discussion, public content data acquisition, or lead query and analysis.
