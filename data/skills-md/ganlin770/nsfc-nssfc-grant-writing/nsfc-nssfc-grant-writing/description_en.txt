Assist applicants in writing their own complete guidance skill for project proposals/applications for the National Social Science Fund (NSSFC) and the National Natural Science Fund (NSFC). Covers topic selection and assignment of application codes, justification for project approval and review of current research status, concise formulation of research content/objectives/key scientific questions, research plan and technical route, innovative points, research foundation and team, budget, expected results, annual plan, anonymous leaflets, review scoring logic and reasons for rejection, research integrity red lines and generative AI usage guidelines, application process timeline, and includes a built-in chart‑making tool to generate technical route maps, research framework diagrams, mechanism model diagrams, and Gantt charts with one click (matplotlib, export printable PNG/PDF/SVG). Use this whenever the user mentions NSSFC/National Social Science Fund/Social Science Fund/NSFC/National Natural Science Fund/Science Fund/project proposal/application/leaflet/project argumentation/justification for project approval/technical route map/application form/general project/youth fund/major project/key project/Chinese academic translation/post‑grant, or says “write fund/modify proposal/polish justification/create technical route map/refine scientific questions/fund application/project application/how to write a proposal/will the review pass”. This is a tool to assist in writing “your own” proposal, not to write or buy/sell proposals. NOT for journal paper writing, thesis/dissertation/opening report (use thesis‑related skill), pure literature reviews, or writing unrelated to fund applications.
