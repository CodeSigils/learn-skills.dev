Short online fiction analysis. Dissect the story core, structure, emotional line, twist design, writing techniques, and resonance layers of popular short novels (such as Tomato Shorts, Story Club, Zhihu Salt Selection, Pursuing the Wife, Social Scenes, Rebirth, Villain Abuse, and other mass‑appeal topics). A single comprehensive analysis pipeline: after completing Stages 2‑6, generate a complete analysis report and save it to AnalysisLibrary/{book title}/. The downstream story‑short‑write process then reads the analysis report, plot nodes, writing techniques, original text, and _meta.json to produce a new piece. Trigger commands: /story-short-analyze, /短篇拆文, “拆短篇”, “拆这篇短文”, “短篇拆文”, “精细拆解短篇”, “8000 字短篇拆解”, “番茄短篇拆文”, “故事会拆解”, “盐言故事拆解”, “分析这篇短篇” — all feed into the same pipeline.
