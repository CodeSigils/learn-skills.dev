Content publishing risk check for creators. It examines, sentence by sentence, the titles, body text, image captions, subtitles, spoken narration, comments, account profiles, and video frames on self‑media, social media, and content platforms, determining what the platform's algorithms might detect and what substantive issues the content itself may have, and provides specific locations and minimal corrective actions; when the video context boundaries need clarification, it additionally generates three lines of accurate small overlay text. It is used when users ask “check for sensitive words,” “pre‑publish risk assessment,” “where might this content violate rules,” “can this Xiaohongshu post be published,” or “what statement to put on the video.” It retains strong viewpoints, emotional tension, personal style, and viral hooks.
