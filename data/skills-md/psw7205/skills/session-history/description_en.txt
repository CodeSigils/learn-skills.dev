A skill that summarizes session conversations and saves them as a history file focused on decision-making rationale. Triggered by requests like "Summarize the session," "That's it for today," "Wrap up," "End," "Save session history," "Write a handoff note," "Organize work history," "Record today's work," "done," "LGTM, that's it," "Good, let's finish," "Continue next time," "save session," "wrap up," "Save session," or when signaling the end of a major work unit. Also triggered if there is a decision record in a session that only involved design discussion.
