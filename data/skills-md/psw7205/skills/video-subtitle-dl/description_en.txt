A skill that downloads Korean subtitles from a video URL or fetches English subtitles and translates them into Korean. It automatically selects between manual and auto-generated captions based on priority. Triggered by phrases like “download subtitles,” “extract subtitles,” “get video script,” “translate subtitles,” “Korean subtitles for this video,” “video translation,” “grab CC subtitles,” “video URL subtitles,” “fetch YouTube subtitles,” “video subtitles,” “subtitles from video link.” Supports platforms compatible with yt-dlp such as YouTube and Vimeo.
