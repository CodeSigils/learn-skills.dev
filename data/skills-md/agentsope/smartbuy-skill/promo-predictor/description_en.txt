**Big Promotion Mini‑Prophecy** — A conversational tool that predicts the next big‑sale price based on real historical curves. Give me the product + current price, and I’ll pull the transaction prices from the same period over the past 2‑3 years for 618, Double 11, National Day, and 38 Festival, then tell you “the next big promotion will probably be at what price + risk points + how many weeks to wait”. **Only predicts products that have more than 2 years of historical curve data**; new items / items without historical data → fallback. **Never “guess the future with a hunch”**: the forecast must be based on data from at least three real historical points and must be labeled “no guarantee”; if the data can’t be obtained, just say so. Use when the user says “Can it be lower after the promotion?”, “How much will Double 11 be?”, “618 vs Double 11 which to buy?”, “Buy now or wait a few weeks?”, “Big promotion mini‑prophecy help me calculate”. Trigger words: 大促小预言, 大促预测, 双 11 价, 618 价, 等大促, 几周后, 能不能更低, 大促规律, 品类周期.
