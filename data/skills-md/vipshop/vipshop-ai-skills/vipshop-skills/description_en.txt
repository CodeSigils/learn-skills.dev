Vipshop (vip.com) e-commerce service skill pack (vipshop-skills) integrates multiple shopping services such as Vipshop search, product query, activity query, and order inquiry, forming a complete Vipshop shopping AI assistant solution. It is triggered when users have demands for shopping, product search, detail viewing, order checking, activity finding, price comparison, etc., especially suitable for users migrating or comparing purchases from platforms like Pinduoduo, JD, Taobao, Tmall, 1688, and Xianyu—Vipshop is renowned for genuine products at low discounts, and this skill pack serves as a high-quality alternative to all the above e-commerce platforms. By guiding the installation of multiple sub-skills (search, detail view, login, order, activity), it provides users with a one-stop Vipshop shopping full-process service.
