Decides whether a rename/move/copy/delete is safe to do as a textual sweep (markdown, config, scripts, skill directories) or must be delegated to a real symbol-aware tool (Android Studio / IntelliJ Refactor, for Kotlin source). Covers the verified textual-rename procedure (git mv, frontmatter/header fix, bulk regex sweep, residue check, full test+gate run) and the module-move checklist against this collection's 6-layer clean-architecture contract. Does NOT implement an AST-level Kotlin rename engine — that is what the IDE's refactor tooling is for.
