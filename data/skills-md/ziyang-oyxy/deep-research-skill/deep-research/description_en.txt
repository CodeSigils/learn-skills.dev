The local helper defaults to skeleton-only initialization: it mainly creates phase artifacts, state files, and a report skeleton rather than automatically finishing retrieval, writing, and artifact backfill. Triggers on "deep research", "comprehensive analysis", "research report", "compare X vs Y", "analyze trends", or "state of the art". Not for simple lookups, debugging, or questions answerable with 1-2 searches.
