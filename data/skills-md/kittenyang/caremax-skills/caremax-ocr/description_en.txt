Upload medical reports and run OCR recognition via CareMax Health API. Session-based: upload creates a session, OCR processes all files in the session, confirm saves all reports atomically. Also handles resuming incomplete sessions (stuck uploads, pending confirmations). Trigger terms: upload report, scan report, OCR, recognize report, extract indicators, health report image, check-up photo, upload, scan, extract, resume upload, continue upload, pending session, unfinished upload, 继续上传, 未完成.
