Systematic review and semantic examination skills for dissertations. From the perspective of a graduate supervisor or blind-review expert, conduct a comprehensive quality inspection of the dissertation (topic overview, theoretical methods, workload and results, logical structure, writing standards) as well as a semantic-level fine-grained review (semantic duplication, semantic redundancy, term conflicts, inconsistent expressions, cross‑chapter duplication, cohesion breaks); **when reviewing the entire text or at the chapter level, AI trace detection (humanizer‑zh mode) is performed by default**, and the results are output together with the semantic fine review and macro evaluation. The cross‑chapter checklist can be found in references/review‑checklist.md. The academic integrity agreement is in the repository root DISTRIBUTION.md.
