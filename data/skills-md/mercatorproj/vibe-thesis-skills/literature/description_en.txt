Full-chain literature skills. Prerequisite: multi-source export of bibliographic records, merging and deduplication (see references/multi-source-retrieval.md). Four modes: (1) Retrieval; (2) Reading and reviewing; (3) LaTeX insertion \cite{}; (4) Generating BibTeX. See also non-zotero-bibliography.md (EndNote/Word, etc.). Systematic review screening and PRISMA are covered in the systematic-review-lite skill.
