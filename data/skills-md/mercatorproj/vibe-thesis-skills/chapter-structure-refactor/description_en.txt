Intelligent chapter-structure reconstruction capability, which conducts deep analysis and systematic restructuring of the writing approach, logical architecture, and content organization of entire chapters in academic papers. By analyzing chapter intent, deconstructing sentence meanings, and re‑organizing the writing logic, it performs an academic‑level overall rewrite of the chapter. It is applicable to scenarios where a chapter’s logical main line is unclear, organization is chaotic, expression is non‑standard, or material utilization is insufficient; commonly needed for method chapters, model chapters, experiment/case chapters, and other sections that require comprehensive reshaping.
