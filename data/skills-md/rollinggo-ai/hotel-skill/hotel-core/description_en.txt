RollingGo hotel search and booking assistant, which implements the full hotel query process by calling the RollingGo hotel service API. Supported scenarios: 1) Search hotels by city, attraction, subway station, airport, etc.; 2) Filter by star rating, budget, tags (pool, breakfast included, family-friendly, pet‑friendly, etc.); 3) Query real‑time room types and prices for a specific hotel; 4) Compare multiple hotels; 5) Guide users to complete a booking. Trigger phrases: find a hotel, book a hotel, search hotels, hotel recommendation, hotel query, nearby hotels, five‑star hotels, homestay, resort, check room rates, view room types, check‑in, where to stay, accommodation, rollinggo, travel accommodation, business travel accommodation, family hotel, hotel with pool, hotel with breakfast.
