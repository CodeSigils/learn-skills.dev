RollingGo hotel search and booking assistant, which implements the full process from hotel inquiry to order placement by calling the RollingGo hotel service API. Supported scenarios: ① Search hotels by city, attraction, subway station, airport, etc.; ② Filter by star rating, budget, tags (pool, breakfast included, family-friendly, pet‑friendly, etc.); ③ Query real‑time room types and prices for a specific hotel; ④ Compare multiple hotels; ⑤ Guide users to complete a booking.
