Human‑flavored writing – when polishing, refining, or rewriting someone’s text, preserve the sense of the person behind the words.  
**Use when:** a user submits a passage they wrote and asks for polishing, refining, editing, condensing, rewriting, “help me edit,” “how does this look?” It also applies to scenarios where, after ghost‑writing, the user says “the AI flavor is too strong,” “there’s no human touch,” or “I can’t feel me,” prompting a rework.  
**Trigger words:** polish copy, refine, help me edit, rewrite, edit this passage, human flavor, AI flavor.  
**Built‑in two layers:** underlying principle (when to act, how to act) + post‑editing checklist (whether the edited parts still carry AI flavor, derived from Wikipedia’s “Signs of AI writing” / Blade Runner humanizer).
