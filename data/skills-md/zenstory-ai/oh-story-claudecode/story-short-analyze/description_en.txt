Short web‑fiction analysis. Deconstruct the story core, structure, emotional line, twist design, writing techniques, and resonance layers of popular short novels (Tomato short stories, Story Club, Zhihu Salt Selection, wife‑chasing, social life, rebirth, villain‑abuse, and other mainstream themes). A single, full‑stage analysis pipeline: after completing Stages 2‑6, produce a complete analysis report, save it to AnalysisLibrary/{book title}/, and downstream story‑short‑write reads the analysis report together with plot nodes, writing techniques, the original text, and _meta.json to write a new piece. Trigger commands: /story-short-analyze, /短篇拆文, “拆短篇”, “拆这篇短文”, “短篇拆文”, “精细拆解短篇”, “8000 字短篇拆解”, “番茄短篇拆文”, “故事会拆解”, “盐言故事拆解”, “分析这篇短篇” — all go through the same pipeline.
