Long-form web novel dissection. Deeply analyze the golden three chapters of a hit long novel, character architecture, pleasure‑point design, and pacing control. Single deep‑dissection pipeline: after completing the golden three chapters (Stage 1), a quick preview report is generated and the user is asked whether to continue with a full dissection; upon confirmation, Stage 2 proceeds with per‑chapter summaries, aggregated analysis, relationship mapping, and a consolidated report, with all outputs saved to DissectionLibrary/{BookTitle}/. Trigger methods: /story-long-analyze, /长篇拆文, “帮我拆这本书”, “拆这本书”, “分析黄金三章”, “深度拆解”, “完整拆解”, “系统拆解”, or providing the path to the novel’s text file — all enter the same pipeline.
