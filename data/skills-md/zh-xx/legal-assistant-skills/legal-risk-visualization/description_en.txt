Structured Analysis and Visualization of Legal Risks. Based on legal analysis text, a five-step risk extraction model is executed to generate a four-layer visual output (radar chart data, risk matrix, impact path diagram, decision tree). Applicable to: (1) users requesting risk visualization from legal analysis reports, (2) requests for structural or transmission risk analysis, (3) requests for generating risk decision recommendations or risk assessment reports, (4) mentions of keywords such as "risk radar chart," "risk matrix," "impact path diagram," or "decision tree."
