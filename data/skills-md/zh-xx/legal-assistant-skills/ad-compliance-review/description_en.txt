Ad compliance review skill, used to verify whether ad materials comply with the PRC Advertising Law and related regulations. Applicable scenarios: (1) when a user submits ad copy or ad materials requesting compliance review; (2) when a user mentions keywords such as "ad review," "ad compliance," or "advertising law review"; (3) when a user asks to check whether ad content carries illegal or non-compliant risks; (4) when a user submits ads for real estate, food, medical, pharmaceutical, internet, or other industries requiring special review. Review basis covers the Advertising Law, Anti-Unfair Competition Law, and industry-specific regulations.
