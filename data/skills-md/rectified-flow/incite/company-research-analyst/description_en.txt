Conduct systematic, in-depth research and analysis of A‑share, Hong Kong, and US listed companies, and produce structured research reports. This is triggered when a user provides a company name and requests analysis, research, valuation, revenue breakdown, profit forecasting, or assessment of a moat. Includes, but is not limited to: “Help me research XX company,” “Analyze XX’s business,” “What is the valuation of XX,” “How will XX’s revenue be broken down over the next two years,” “Help me look at XX’s competitive advantage.” Whenever deep analysis of a specific listed company is involved (rather than simply checking a stock price), this skill should be used.
