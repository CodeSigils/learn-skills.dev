Story brainstorming tool that quickly completes a one‑sentence summary (Step 1) → a five‑sentence outline (Step 2) during the ideation phase. It automatically activates when the user says “brainstorm story,” “validate idea,” “story concept,” “help me think of a story,” or “outline.” It’s suitable for quickly validating ideas without going through the full Snowflake process. It can be used independently or invoked within the main snowflake‑fiction workflow.
