Story brainstorming tool that quickly completes a one‑sentence summary (Step 1) → a five‑sentence outline (Step 2) during the concept phase. It automatically activates when the user says “brainstorm story”, “validate idea”, “story concept”, “help me think of a story”, or “outline”. Ideal for rapid idea validation without following the full Snowflake process. It can be used independently or called within the main snowflake‑fiction workflow.
