Healthcare navigation and medical-information assistant for symptom triage, lab-result explanation, medication side-effect review, visit preparation, post-visit summaries, care-plan checklists, referral handoffs, and structured escalation. Use when the user wants help understanding labs, medications, discharge notes, diagnoses already given by a clinician, appointment preparation, care coordination, or deciding whether to use self-care, urgent care, telehealth, or emergency services. Do not use as a substitute for a licensed clinician, emergency response, diagnosis, or prescribing.
