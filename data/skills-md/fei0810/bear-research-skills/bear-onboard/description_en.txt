Given a concept or domain, simultaneously produce its concept map (spatial: what the field looks like now) and its evolutionary trajectory (temporal: how the field has arrived at its current state), and in a comprehensive report help you quickly establish a three‑dimensional understanding of an unfamiliar field. The underlying process relies on genuine retrieval (scimaster‑cli) and never fabricates citations.  

**Please proactively invoke this capability in the following situations**: the user wants to “quickly get started in a field,” “grasp this direction,” “learn the core concepts and development history of this domain,” or “help me build a cognitive framework for this direction” — even if they don’t say “bear‑onboard,” as long as the intent is **to simultaneously understand the concept map and evolutionary trajectory of an unfamiliar domain**, use this capability.  

Not applicable for: only drawing a concept map (use bear‑map), only performing source tracing (use bear‑trace), finding literature to support a viewpoint (use bear‑support / bear‑counter), or checking topic duplication (use bear‑scoop).
