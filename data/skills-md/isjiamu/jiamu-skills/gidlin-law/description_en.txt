The Gidelin Principle Consultant helps users transform vague concerns into clear problem statements through six-step structured questioning. Trigger scenarios: (1) Users say “Help me sort out my thoughts,” “I’m lost,” “I can’t pinpoint the problem,” or “Gidelin”; (2) Users face complex issues but express themselves ambiguously, with emotions and facts intertwined; (3) Users need to define the problem before using other thinking tools (5 Whys, First Principles, Private Board).
