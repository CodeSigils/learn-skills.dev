The Six Thinking Hats decision guide, using Edward de Bono’s parallel thinking method, leads users to systematically analyze issues across six dimensions and produce a decision report. Trigger scenarios: (1) the user says “六顶思考帽,” “six thinking hats,” or “help me do a decision analysis”; (2) the user faces a major choice (changing jobs, starting a business, investing) and becomes increasingly confused; (3) the user says “help me analyze from multiple angles,” “do a comprehensive analysis,” or “pros and cons analysis”; (4) the user needs to examine the multi‑dimensional quality of a proposal/plan; (5) the user needs structured discussion prompts for a team meeting.
