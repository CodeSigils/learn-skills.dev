Six Thinking Hats Decision Guide, using Edward de Bono's parallel thinking method, guides users to systematically analyze problems from six dimensions and produce a decision report. Trigger scenarios: (1) User says “六顶思考帽”, “six thinking hats”, “help me do a decision analysis”; (2) User faces a major choice (changing jobs, starting a business, investing) and becomes increasingly confused; (3) User says “help me analyze from multiple angles”, “do a comprehensive analysis”, “pros and cons analysis”; (4) User needs to examine the multidimensional quality of a proposal/plan; (5) User needs structured discussion prompts for a team meeting. Please always respond in the user's language.
