Orchestrates deep research sessions and writes comprehensive, detailed notes directly into an Obsidian vault. Use this skill whenever the user wants to research a topic, create Obsidian notes from sources (URLs, PDFs, YouTube videos, raw text), build a Map of Content (MOC), generate mindmaps, or organize knowledge into their vault. Trigger even if the user just says "research this", "make notes about", "add to my Obsidian", or "help me learn about X". This skill handles the full pipeline: source ingestion → deep synthesis → iterative per-concept writing → vault files with proper citations. Always use this skill for any multi-source research or Obsidian note creation task, even if the user doesn't say "skill" or "orchestrate".
