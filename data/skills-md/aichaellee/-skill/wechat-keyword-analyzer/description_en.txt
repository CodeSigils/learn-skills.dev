Public Account keyword article search and deep analysis tool. Based on the keyword entered by the user, it searches public account articles from the past week (default retrieves 3 pages, about 30 articles), conducts multi-dimensional deep analysis, and generates a Markdown report.

Analysis dimensions include: (1) Title analysis – analyzing title features, keywords, attraction elements; (2) Topic categorization – classifying articles by theme/subfield; (3) Writing style breakdown – analyzing writing techniques, article structure, hook points; (4) Interaction data analysis – analyzing view count, likes, comments, etc.

When users need: (1) search public account articles by keyword, (2) analyze popular articles on a specific topic, (3) find content creation inspiration and topic references, (4) learn writing methods of high‑read articles, (5) understand content trends in a certain field, they use this skill.
