Horizontal‑Vertical Analysis Method Deep Research Skill. Used when the user wants to systematically study a product, company, concept, technology, or individual.  
The core is dual‑axis analysis: the vertical axis tracks the complete life‑cycle from inception to the present, while the horizontal axis conducts a systematic comparison with competing or similar items at the current time slice. The intersection of the two axes yields insights. The final output is a Markdown research report.  
Trigger phrases include but are not limited to: “research this,” “help me analyze,” “deep research,” “do a survey,” “competitor analysis,” “help me understand,” “what’s this thing.”  
Not for simple term definitions, not for public‑account writing, and not for title or summary generation.
