Paper Reverse-Reading Method: Given a paper, recursively identify its preceding papers that critique and improve upon it (up to five layers), then locate the most recent advances that follow it, and narrate the evolution of the problem forward from its origin. Using the problem as the axis, explain in a Feynman style the issues observed in each paper and the innovative solutions. Use when the user shares a paper and wants to understand its intellectual lineage, citation chain, problem evolution, or says “倒读”, “论文溯源”, “论文脉络”, “paper river”, “paper connects”, “trace back”, “这篇论文的来龙去脉”, “论文演化”. Also trigger when the user wants to understand how a research problem evolved across multiple papers.
