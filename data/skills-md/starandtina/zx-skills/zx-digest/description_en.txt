Article distiller. Given an article, steam away the water and leave the bones. It’s not an abbreviation; it’s a re‑interpretation—expressing in your own words what the author is truly saying. Use this when the user says “summary,” “abstract,” “distill,” “state the main points,” “digest,” “summarize,” “tldr,” “tl;dr,” “help me read,” “what does this talk about,” “too long, didn’t read,” or when the user shares an article/blog/essay URL or text and wants the core extracted. Also trigger when the user pastes a long text and asks “what does this say” or “what’s it about.”
