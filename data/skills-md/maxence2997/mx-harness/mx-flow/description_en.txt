Full development workflow orchestrator. Runs mx-brainstorm → mx-plan → mx-worktree → convergent loop (mx-tdd → mx-commit → mx-team-review → mx-review-triage) → mx-verify. Pauses at human decision gates. Default: 1 hard gate (spec approval), all others auto-proceed. Gated mode (--gated): 4 human gates for full control. Use when starting a new feature or significant change from scratch.
