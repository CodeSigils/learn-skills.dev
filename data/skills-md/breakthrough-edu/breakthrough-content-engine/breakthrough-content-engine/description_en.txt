Content Planning Engine: Given a business goal, it generates an entire content plan; humans only review a single seed sheet while the rest is derived mechanically. It covers FB, IG, short videos (TikTok/Reels), and Xiaohongshu, aimed at small Chinese-Malaysian business owners. Five entry points: plan a batch of content (“Help me plan this month’s content,” “How many pieces should this batch have,” “What should I post,” “plan my content”); diagnose first without planning (“Why does my content get no views”); collect customers’ exact words into a material library (“I captured a few customer quotes,” “The customer said something”); record performance (“Log the results,” “Data for this post”); review (“Do a review,” “How did the last few weeks go”). NOT for: single-copywriting (that doesn’t require the whole pipeline, just write it); paid ad creative testing (this engine only handles organic, the allocation logic is completely different); brand strategy itself (this engine consumes the outputs of brand strategy, it does not produce brand strategy).
