Content Planning Engine: Given a business goal, generate an entire content plan; humans only review a single seed sheet while the rest is mechanically derived. It covers Facebook, Instagram, short‑form video (TikTok / Reels), and Xiaohongshu, aimed at Malaysian Chinese small‑business owners. Five entry points: plan a batch of content ("help me plan this month’s content", "how many pieces should this batch have", "what should I post", "plan my content"); diagnose first without planning ("why is nobody watching my content"); collect customers’ exact words into a material library ("I captured a few customer quotes", "the customer said something"); record results ("log the results", "the data for this post"); review ("do a review", "how have the past weeks performed"). NOT for: single‑piece copywriting (that doesn’t need the whole pipeline, just write it directly); paid‑media creative testing (this engine only handles organic, the allocation logic is completely different); brand strategy itself (this engine consumes the output of brand strategy, it does not produce brand strategy).
