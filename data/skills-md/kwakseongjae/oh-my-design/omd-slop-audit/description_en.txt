It inspects the UI/UX copy of actual product routes and diagnoses, using rule IDs and line references, repeated generative basic patterns lacking product context, decorations without brand justification, overuse of cards, gradients, and icon tiles, as well as translated-sounding and abstract copy. It is used for requests such as “catch AI slop,” “looks like a template,” “why doesn’t it look like an AI‑generated screen?” and “anti‑slop audit.” Accessibility errors and taste differences are categorized into separate grades.
