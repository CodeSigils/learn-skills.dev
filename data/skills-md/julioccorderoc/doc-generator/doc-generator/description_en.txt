Generates professional PDF business documents (purchase orders, invoices, requests for quotation). Use this skill when the user asks to create, generate, draft, or send a PO, invoice, bill, or RFQ, even if phrased casually (e.g., 'make a PO for Acme', 'invoice a client for 10 hours', 'request a quote from supplier'). The skill handles data collection, payload generation, and CLI execution. Do not use for editing existing PDFs.
