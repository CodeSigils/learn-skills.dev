Payment Skill: recognizes the user’s intent to pay/make a payment/check out, or is invoked by other skills (e.g., the tip skill), executes scripts/payment_api.py to call the payment-code API, obtains the tradeCode, and prominently displays it to the user.  
TRIGGER when: the user says “I want to pay,” “I want to make a payment,” “go pay,” “help me pay,” “check out,” “pay the bill,” or is invoked by another skill with an amount parameter.  
DO NOT TRIGGER when: the user expresses an intent to tip/gift/reward (handled by the tip skill).
