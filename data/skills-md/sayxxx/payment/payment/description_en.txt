Payment Skill: Recognizes the user's intent to pay/check out, or receives calls from other skills (e.g., tip skill), executes scripts/payment_api.py to call the payment-code API, obtains tradeCode, and prominently displays it to the user.  
TRIGGER when: user says “I want to pay,” “I need to pay,” “go pay,” “help me pay,” “check out,” “pay the bill,” “foot the bill,” or is invoked by another skill with an amount parameter.  
DO NOT TRIGGER when: user expresses tipping/gift-giving/appreciation intent (handled by tip skill).
