Mall Skill: Recognize the user's shopping/buying/selecting-products intent, display the product catalog, guide the user to choose items and quantities, and after summarizing the order call the payment skill to complete the payment. TRIGGER when: the user says "buy things", "go shopping", "I want to buy", "what can I buy", "show me products", "buy a phone", "buy fruit", "buy groceries", "browse the mall", "place an order", "shop around", or other shopping-related intents. DO NOT TRIGGER when: the user only says "pay", "make a payment", "tip", or other intents without the meaning of selecting products.
