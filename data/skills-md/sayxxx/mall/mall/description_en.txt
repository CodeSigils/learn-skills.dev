Mall Skill: Recognizes user intentions related to shopping/buying/selecting products, displays the product catalog, guides the user to choose products and quantities, and after summarizing the order, calls the payment skill to complete the payment. TRIGGER when: user says things like "buy something," "shopping," "I want to buy," "what can I buy," "show me products," "buy a phone," "buy fruit," "buy groceries," "go to the mall," "place an order," "make a selection," and other shopping-related intentions. DO NOT TRIGGER when: user only says "pay," "make a payment," "tip," etc., without the intention of selecting products.
