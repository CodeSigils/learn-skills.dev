Academic-paper typesetting assistant based on a standard directory structure. Supports PDF/.doc/.docx/.md input formats, automatically selects the OCR pipeline, reflow pipeline, or direct MD conversion pipeline. Includes environment cleanup confirmation, breakpoint resumption, intelligent image cropping, per-unit incremental DOCX generation, dual-unit quality check, intermediate state saving, and BibTeX reference management. All intermediate files go to resources/, final outputs to outputs/.
