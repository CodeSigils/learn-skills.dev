Use when the user wants a complete profile of a single property — by street address or a portal listing URL. Pulls full details, price/tax history, valuation/estimate, comparables, and neighborhood context (schools, climate, walkability) and turns them into a property brief. Triggers on "tell me about this house", "look up this address", "is this listing a good deal", "what's this property worth", "pull the details for this Zillow/Redfin/Rightmove link".
