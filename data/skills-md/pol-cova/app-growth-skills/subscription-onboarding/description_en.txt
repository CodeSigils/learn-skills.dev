Designs, audits, and optimizes onboarding and onboarding-to-paywall flows for consumer subscription apps. Use when onboarding, first-run experience, activation handoff, onboarding paywalls, personalization, permissions, or onboarding experiments are the primary product decision. When an app codebase is available, inspect the implementation before recommending changes. Reasons from product facts, first-party evidence, time-to-value, user intent, monetization model, and platform constraints rather than applying fixed funnels or benchmarks.
