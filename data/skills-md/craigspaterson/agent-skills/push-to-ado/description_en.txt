Uploads a drafted User Story or Technical Story to Azure DevOps as a work item, populating Title, Description, Acceptance Criteria, Story Points, Priority, and Tags, and optionally linking a parent feature. Also supports creating a full Epic → Feature → Story hierarchy from a structured backlog, and updating existing work items in bulk. Use when the user says "push to ADO", "create in ADO", "upload to ADO", "add to ADO", "update ADO items", or similar after stories have been drafted in conversation.
