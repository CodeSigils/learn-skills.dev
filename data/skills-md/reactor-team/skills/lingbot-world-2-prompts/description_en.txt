Turn a loose scene idea (even a single short sentence, English or Chinese) into a production-ready LingBot-World 2 layered scene: a StructuredExample JSON — base / camera / movement layers with static+dynamic variants, stackable hold-key events (keys 1..9), and per-scene jump/crouch/stand prompts — that drops straight into the frontend's lib/lingbot-cases/, plus a derived seed-image prompt. Can also emit a single composed standalone prompt when the user wants just one prompt. Use whenever the user mentions LingBot 2, LingBot-World 2, lingbot-world-2, reactor/lingbot-world-2, layered scene, StructuredScene, composePrompt, isMoving, base/camera/ movement layers, hold keys, event keys, jumpPrompt/crouchPrompt, lib/lingbot-cases, a "playable character" video prompt, or gives a rough idea they want expanded into an interactive world-model prompt ("make a lingbot 2 scene for a knight in the desert"). Also handles requests phrased in the legacy actions.json / f-g slot export format (see references/legacy-sessions.md). The separate lingbot-world skill covers the V1 JSON state-machine format.
