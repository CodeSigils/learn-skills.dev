When a user wants to add or edit a post, manage categories, upload images, and publish to their Hugo static blog (e.g., maggiacito.com), they use a conversational back‑end—just say a phrase and it modifies the file, commits, pushes, and auto‑deploys, with no CMS or server required. Trigger phrases: “post a blog”, “add an article to Hugo”, “write a post to the blog”, “manage blog categories”, “upload an image to the blog”, “blog back‑end”, “/wjs-publishing-hugo”.
