Review logical inconsistencies in documents, articles, reports, and proposals, and verify the structure of issues, necessary information, reasoning, conclusions, and evidence. Detect leaps, contradictions, misaligned points, and insufficient evidence from the perspectives of Why So, So What, and MECE. Use it when checking the coherence of a text before publication or revision. It is not used for simple proofreading, style adjustment, fact-checking only, legal advice, or rigorous verification of mathematical proofs.
