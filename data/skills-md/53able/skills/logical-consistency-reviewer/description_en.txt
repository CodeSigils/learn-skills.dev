Review logical inconsistencies in documents, articles, reports, and proposals, and verify the structure of issues, required information, reasoning, conclusions, and supporting evidence. Detect leaps, contradictions, misaligned issues, and insufficient evidence from the perspectives of Why So, So What, and MECE. Use it to check the coherence of a text before publication or revision. It is not intended for simple proofreading, style adjustments, fact‑checking only, legal advice, or rigorous verification of mathematical proofs.
