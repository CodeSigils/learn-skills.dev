Enterprise WeChat CLI All-in-One Suite, covering six major business areas: Contacts, Messages, Documents (including Documents, Smart Sheets, and Smart Docs, formerly Smart Pages), Calendar, Meetings, and To-Dos. It supports searching contacts by name/alias, sending and receiving messages (text, images, files, voice, video), creating/reading/editing documents in Markdown, managing Smart Sheet structure and records, creating Smart Docs and exporting their content as Markdown, creating/modifying/canceling calendar events and checking availability, booking/managing meetings, and creating/tracking/assigning to-do tasks. Trigger this skill whenever scenarios involve messages, calendar, to-dos, etc., even if the user does not explicitly mention “Enterprise WeChat.”
