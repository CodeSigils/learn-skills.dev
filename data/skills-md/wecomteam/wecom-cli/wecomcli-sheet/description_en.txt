WeCom online spreadsheet (sheet) management skill. Provides creation of online spreadsheets, content reading, content modification, appending row data, and addition/deletion management of sub-sheets. Applicable scenarios: (1) Create a blank online spreadsheet (2) Read the full content of a spreadsheet (Markdown) (3) Read basic information and sub-sheet list (4) Read sub-sheet data of a spreadsheet (5) Modify content in a specified area (6) Append a row of data at the end (7) Add/delete sub-sheets. This skill is triggered when the user mentions “WeCom spreadsheet”, “WeCom online spreadsheet”, “WeCom Excel spreadsheet”, or a link in the form `https://doc.weixin.qq.com/sheet/xxx`. Note: For smart sheets (`/smartsheet/*`) use `wecomcli-smartsheet`; for regular documents (`/doc/*`) use `wecomcli-doc`; for smart documents/smart pages (`/smartpage/*`) use `wecomcli-smartpage`.
