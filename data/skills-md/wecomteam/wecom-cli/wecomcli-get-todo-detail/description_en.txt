Enterprise WeChat batch query skill for to-do details: retrieves full information (including content and assignee) based on a list of to-do IDs. Use when the user says things like “show me the details of this to-do,” “what’s the to-do content,” “who is this to-do assigned to,” or “tell me the specific info of the to-do.” Normally used together with wecomcli-get-todo-list—first get the list of to-do IDs, then use this skill for details.
