wecom-cli business skill's common pre-checks, obtaining robot and authorized real-person identity, and generic output constraints. Before any wecomcli-* skill attempts to execute a wecom-cli command, it must also read this skill to verify that the CLI is installed, that its version is at least 1.1.0, and that the corporate WeChat credentials have been authorized; installation or initialization is performed only if they are missing, the version is too low, or authorization is absent. This skill also defines a constraint that all ID-type fields common to skills must not be exposed. This skill does not handle specific business requests.
