wecom-cli business skill’s common pre‑checks, acquiring a robot and authorizing a real‑person identity, and general output constraints. Before any wecomcli‑* skill executes a wecom‑cli command, it must also load this skill, verify that the CLI is installed, that its version is at least 1.1.0, and that the Enterprise WeChat credentials have been authorized; installation or initialization is performed only when missing, the version is too low, or authorization is absent. This skill also defines a constraint that ID‑type fields shared by all skills must not be exposed. This skill does not handle specific business requests.
