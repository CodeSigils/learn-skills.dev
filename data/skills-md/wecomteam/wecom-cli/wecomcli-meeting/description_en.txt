Enterprise WeChat meeting functionality supports creating scheduled meetings, querying meeting lists, retrieving meeting details, canceling meetings, and updating meeting participants. It is triggered when users need to "create a meeting," "schedule a meeting," "arrange a meeting," "view meetings," "query the meeting list," "get meeting details," "when is the meeting," "what meetings are there," "find a meeting," "cancel a meeting," "delete a meeting," "modify meeting participants," "add meeting attendees," or "remove meeting members."
