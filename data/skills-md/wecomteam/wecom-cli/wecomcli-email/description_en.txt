Enterprise WeChat Mail: send/reply/forward emails, search email lists, retrieve email details (body, attachments, embedded image parsing), and support sending calendar invitations and meeting reservations via email. Use this when users have needs related to internal email sending/receiving, email queries, email management, etc. Note: calendars and meetings have separate skills; only when the user explicitly mentions “mailbox” or “email” (e.g., “send a meeting invitation via email,” “send a meeting email”) should this skill be used to handle meeting calendar emails.
