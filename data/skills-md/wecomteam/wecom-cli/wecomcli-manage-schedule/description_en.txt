Enterprise WeChat calendar management skill. Suitable for users' various calendar management needs in Enterprise WeChat. Use this skill when the user needs to: (1) query the schedule list within a specified time range or obtain detailed schedule information (title, time, location, participants, etc.), (2) create a new schedule and set reminders and participants, (3) modify the title, time, location and other information of an existing schedule or cancel the schedule, (4) add or remove schedule participants, (5) query the free/busy status of multiple members and analyze common free time slots to arrange meetings.
