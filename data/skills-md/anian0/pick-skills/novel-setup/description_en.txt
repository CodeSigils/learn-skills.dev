Novel Project Cold‑Start and Long‑Form Structure Management Assistant. Designed to create a novel project from scratch, plan the overall direction, maintain the story outline, track foreshadowing, keep the protagonist’s dossier and story‑state snapshots, and take over the promotion of pending world‑rule, foreshadowing, and outline entries within the setting.  

Trigger scenarios: when the user wants to start a new novel project, open a plot thread, plan an outline, design the world view, create a protagonist, adjust story direction, track foreshadowing, check story progress, view the current story status, plan volumes and acts, organize the timeline, promote pending world‑rule entries, tweak writing guidelines, or cooperate with a setting health‑check to revise macro‑level settings, etc.  

Works in conjunction with novel-lite (chapter writing) and novel-review (chapter reviewing), handling the “macro” layer.
