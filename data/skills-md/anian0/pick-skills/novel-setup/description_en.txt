Novel Project Cold-Start & Long-Form Structure Manager. Used to create a novel project from scratch, plan the overall direction, maintain the story outline, track foreshadowing, keep protagonist profiles and story-state snapshots, and promote items in pending settings—world rules, foreshadowing, outline entries—into canon. Triggered when the user asks to start a new novel project, open a new story pit, plan the outline, design the worldview, create the protagonist, adjust story direction, track foreshadowing, check story progress, view current story status, plan volumes and acts, organize the timeline, promote pending world rules, tweak writing notes, or coordinate with setting audits to revise macro settings. Works in tandem with novel-lite (write chapters) and novel-review (review chapters), handling the “macro layer.”
