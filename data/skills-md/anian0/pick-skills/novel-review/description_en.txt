Novel Chapter and Setting Professional Review Assistant. Three review modes: (1) Main‑text review – for the chapter text, combined with world setting, story outline, protagonist profile, story status, foreshadowing tracking, creative standards, etc., generate 10‑20 high‑quality questions covering seven dimensions: plot deepening, setting refinement, fun enhancement, logical rigor, perspective consistency, character development, and long‑term continuity; (2) New‑setting review – for drafts produced in the workspace, pending settings, or during the creative process, evaluate each item for promotion, adjustment, or discard; (3) Setting health check – perform a comprehensive examination of relevant or existing setting documents for consistency, outdated elements, conflicts, and overall health. Drives a “review‑revise‑re‑review” creation loop. Trigger scenarios: user requests chapter review, storyline inspection, setting loophole check, proposes improvement questions, evaluates chapter quality, chapter diagnosis, runs the review loop, chapter polishing, final pre‑final review, reviews pending settings, pre‑promotion evaluation, setting health check, setting consistency check, setting file health, etc. Used together with novel‑setup and novel‑lite.
