Novel Chapter and Setting Professional Review Assistant. Three review modes: (1) Main Text Review—targeting chapter content, integrating world-building, story outline, protagonist profile, story status, foreshadowing tracking, and creation standards to generate 10-20 high-quality questions covering seven dimensions: plot deepening, setting refinement, entertainment enhancement, logical rigor, perspective consistency, character development, and long-term continuity; (2) New Setting Review—evaluating drafts from workspace/pending settings/ongoing creation processes for promotion/adjustment/abandonment; (3) Setting Health Check—comprehensive examination of related settings/existing setting files for consistency, outdated items, conflicts, and overall health. Drives the "review—revise—re-review" creative cycle. Trigger scenarios: user requests chapter review, plot examination, setting loophole checks, improvement questions, chapter quality assessment, chapter diagnosis, review cycle execution, chapter polishing, final pre-publication review, pending setting review, pre-promotion evaluation, setting health check, setting consistency check, setting file health status, etc. Used in conjunction with novel-setup and novel-lite.
