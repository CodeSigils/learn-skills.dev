Write or update the project's external-facing guide documents—developer guide (dev-guide, for contributors/integrators/downstream developers) and user guide (user-guide, for end users). The deliverables go into the project's docs/ directory, are maintained alongside the code, and can be indexed by search tools. Difference from libdoc: guidedoc is task-oriented ("how to do Y with X"), libdoc is reference-oriented ("what every part of X looks like"). Trigger scenarios: user says "write docs," "developer guide," "user guide," or it's proactively pushed when feature-acceptance wraps up.
