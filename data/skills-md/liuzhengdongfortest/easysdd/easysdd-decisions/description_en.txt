Log the finalized technical choices, architectural decisions, long-term constraints, and coding conventions in the project as searchable, permanent documentation. Six months later, no one remembers why X was chosen, but with decision records, at least the context can be understood before the next change. Four types: tech-stack (what tools/libraries/frameworks to use), architecture (how the system is organized), constraint (what is not allowed), convention (what is standardized to be done this way). Trigger scenarios: proactively push after making important choices following feature-design or issue-analysis, or when the user says "record the decision," "archive technical selection," "ADR," "record this constraint," or "write down the convention." Only archive finalized decisions; proposals under discussion are not archived.
