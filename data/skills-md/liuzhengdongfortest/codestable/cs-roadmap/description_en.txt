Break a requirement that is "too big to be treated as a single feature" into a list of sub-features with dependencies and states, and place it in a dedicated `codestable/roadmap/{slug}/` directory—to serve as the seed and scheduling basis for subsequent feature iterations. Two modes: new (draft a fresh roadmap from a large requirement) and update (refresh an existing roadmap: add items, change dependencies, reorder, mark dropped). Division of labor with requirements/architecture—those two document "what the system is now," while the roadmap records "how we plan to proceed next." Trigger scenarios: user says "I want an X system," "help me split this requirement," "schedule this big requirement," "create a roadmap," or during feature-design the requirement is found to be too large for one feature.
