Break down a requirement that's "too big to be a single feature" into a sub-feature checklist with dependencies and states, placed in a dedicated `codestable/roadmap/{slug}/` directory—as seeds and scheduling basis for subsequent multiple feature workflows. Two modes: new (draft a new roadmap from a large requirement), update (refresh an existing roadmap: add items, change dependencies, reorder, mark drops). Division of labor with requirements/architecture—those two record "what the system is now", roadmap records "where we plan to go next". Trigger scenarios: user says "I want an X system", "help me break down this requirement", "schedule this big requirement", "create a roadmap", or during feature-design phase realizing the requirement is too large to fit into one feature.
