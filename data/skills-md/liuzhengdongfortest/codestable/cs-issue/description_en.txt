When fixing a bug, enter this sub-process—turn the verbal “problem found” into a closed loop of verification and fix, leaving behind three artifacts: the problem report, the root-cause analysis, and the fix record. This flow inserts a buffer between “seeing the problem” and “touching the code,” preventing the usual crashes: the mental description vanishes after the edit, surface-only patches made without root-cause analysis, uncontrolled scope creep that can’t be traced, or fixes shipped without verification so you never know if they worked. The skill only acts as a router, deciding which of report / analyze / fix to enter based on existing artifacts. Trivial issues located at a glance take the fast lane, skipping the middle two steps and leaving only a fix-note.
