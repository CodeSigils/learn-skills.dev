Record the finalized technology choices, architectural decisions, long-term constraints, and coding conventions of the project as searchable, permanent documents. Six months later, no one will remember why X was chosen, but with decision records, the background can at least be understood before the next change. Four types: tech-stack (which tools / libraries / frameworks), architecture (how the system is organized), constraint (what is not allowed), convention (what must be done uniformly). Trigger scenarios: actively push after an important choice is made following feature-design or issue-analyze, or when the user says “record the decision,” “archive the tech choice,” “ADR,” “record this constraint,” “write down the convention.” Only archive finalized decisions; options still under discussion are not archived.
