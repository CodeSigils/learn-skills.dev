Issue workflow stage 1—through conversation, turn the problem in the user's mind into a reproducible, traceable {slug}-report.md. The AI only asks "what do you see, how to reproduce, what should happen," without guessing the root cause (that's stage 2). This stage is also the sole official checkpoint to decide between the fast track and the standard path: based on the user's description, first skim the relevant code; if the spot can be located at a glance and the fix is trivial, directly tell the user to take the fast track. Trigger: user says "file an issue," "record this bug," "I found a problem." Entry point of the issue workflow, no prerequisites.
