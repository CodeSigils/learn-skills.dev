Ultra-lightweight lane for refactoring—used when the change is so trivial that it’s not worth going through the full scan → design → apply cycle. The AI spots 1–3 low-risk optimizations on its own, asks the user once for confirmation, applies the fix in-place with the classic method, and proves correctness with existing tests. No scan checklist, no design doc, no multi-step HUMAN sign-off.  
Trigger: user says “quick refactor”, “small refactor”, “just optimize function XX”, “change it directly”, “skip the steps”, and the scope is clearly a single function/component with tests to self-validate.
