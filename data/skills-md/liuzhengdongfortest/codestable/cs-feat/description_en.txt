When developing a new feature, enter this sub-process—turn the vague idea of "add capability X" into a closed-loop acceptance, with a design document archived so both AI and users can later look up what was thought at the time and why the decision was made. Triggering scenarios focus on new capabilities ("build a new feature", "add an X", "implement XX") and do not cover bugs in existing code. This skill only routes; based on existing artifacts it decides the next step among brainstorm / design / fastforward / implement / acceptance.
