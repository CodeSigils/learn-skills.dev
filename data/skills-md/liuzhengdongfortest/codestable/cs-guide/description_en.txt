Write or update the project's public-facing guide documents—developer guide (dev-guide, for contributors/integrators/downstream developers) and user guide (user-guide, for end-users). The deliverables go into the project's docs/ directory, are maintained alongside the code, and are searchable by indexing tools. Difference from libdoc: guidedoc is task-oriented ("how to do Y with X"), libdoc is reference-oriented ("what every part of X looks like"). Trigger scenarios: user says "write docs," "developer guide," "user guide," or proactively pushed when feature-acceptance wraps up.
