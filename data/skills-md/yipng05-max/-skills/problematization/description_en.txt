Research Problematization Tool (Problematization). Transform empirical phenomena or vague interests into research questions with theoretical significance.  
Conduct a triple test: theoretical‑dialogue positioning test, “So What” test, and feasibility test.  
Trigger this skill when the user says “I want to study XX,” “Help me refine a research question,” “Can this phenomenon be studied?” “How do I write a research question,” “Is the topic valuable?” “What if the research question is too broad or too narrow?” or “Help me problematize.”  
Note: This is not a tool for merely “optimizing phrasing”; it is a deep‑analysis tool that helps the user determine whether a phenomenon constitutes a research question and what position that research question holds within academic discourse.
