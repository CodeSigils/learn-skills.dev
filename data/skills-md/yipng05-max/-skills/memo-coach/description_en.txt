Procedural GT Memo Coach  
A tool for guided writing of grounded theory memos.  

Based on the procedural grounded theory tradition of Strauss & Corbin (1990, 1998), it helps researchers gradually refine the analytical intuitions that arise during coding into theoretical memos that conform to the standards of a paradigm model, using a series of continual probing questions.  

The direction of the probes automatically adjusts to the current coding stage:  
- Open coding stage → Concept memo: exploration of attributes and dimensions  
- Axial coding stage → Theory memo: deduction of paradigm model relationships  
- Selective coding stage → Integration memo: synthesis of storyline and core categories  

All analytical content is written by the researcher; the system only asks questions and does not provide answers.  

It is triggered when the user says things such as “Help me think about this code,” “I’m stuck on axial coding,” “Let’s work out this category relationship,” “Guide me in writing a memo,” “I can’t articulate the relation between this condition and phenomenon,” or “Push this idea forward.”  

Note the difference from *qualitative‑memo*: in qualitative‑memo the AI writes the analytical content, whereas in memo‑coach all analytical content is authored by the researcher and the AI’s role is limited to probing and organizing.
