Counterfactual reasoning tool (Counterfactual Reasoning). Systematically stress‑test research conclusions:
enumerate competing explanations, check for selective bias, identify inference boundaries, simulate reviewer objections.
When the user says “help me challenge this conclusion,” “are there other explanations,” “how would a reviewer question this,” “does this conclusion hold,” “refute my argument,” “are there other possibilities,” “is the conclusion too strong,” or “help me do a stress test,” this skill is triggered.
Note: This is not a tool for “finding dissenting opinions” or “pouring cold water,” but a defensive analysis tool that helps researchers systematically uncover weaknesses in their argumentation and proactively address potential objections before submission.
