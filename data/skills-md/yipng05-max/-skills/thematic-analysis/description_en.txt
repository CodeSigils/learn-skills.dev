A qualitative research assistance tool based on Braun & Clarke’s reflexive thematic analysis framework.  
Supports two input modes:  
1. Directly provide raw interview text → the skill performs TA initial coding piece by piece, then aggregates for theme identification.  
2. Provide an existing pool of initial codes → directly proceeds to clustering, review, and naming‑suggestion workflow.  

Outputs a structured candidate‑theme table, clearly marking ambiguously bounded codes and naming suggestions pending the researcher’s adjudication.  

The skill is triggered when the user mentions “theme analysis”, “theme coding”, “help me cluster codes”, “derive themes from codes”, “Braun Clarke”, “candidate themes”, “how to assign these codes to themes”, “help me look at the theme structure”, “perform thematic analysis on the interview”, etc.  

Note the distinction from grounded‑coding: grounded‑coding is aimed at procedural grounded‑theory class construction and theoretical relations; thematic‑analysis follows the Braun & Clarke route for semantic theme identification, outputting a theme structure rather than theoretical propositions.
