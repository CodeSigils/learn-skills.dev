PhD dissertation literature review (approximately 20,000 words) engineering writing assistance system (PhD-LitReview-Auto-Flow). Based on core assets provided by the user such as research topic, research questions, and core concepts, the system systematically retrieves authentic academic literature, verifies the authenticity of the sources, determines the review structure and classification criteria through a multi-stage workflow, and writes a complete literature review chapter of about 20,000 words according to PhD dissertation standards. Using thematic organization and a multi-stage single-step output mechanism, it covers full modules including concept definition, theoretical context, empirical research appraisal, and identification of research gaps, ultimately generating a Word document and providing cross‑chapter back‑filling guidance. Trigger condition: this skill is activated when the user mentions the need to write a PhD dissertation literature review / doctoral thesis literature review / PhD dissertation literature review, needs to write a 20,000‑word literature review, needs to write the second chapter literature review of a thesis, or needs a doctoral dissertation literature synthesis and appraisal. Note: This skill is dedicated to long‑form literature reviews at the doctoral dissertation level (approximately 20,000 words), distinct from literature-review-writer (short journal‑article reviews, 2–5 pages) and paper-analyzer (single‑paper dissection).
