C‑journal (CSSCI source journal) comprehensive paper analysis tool. When the user provides a specific C‑journal name (e.g., “Management World,” “Sociology Research,” “Economic Research,” etc.), it automatically queries CNKI for all issues of that journal over the past five years, retrieving article titles, authors, and abstracts, and generates a professional Word analysis report. The report includes: topic hotspot trends, high‑frequency keywords, research‑method preferences, core author groups, column‑theme evolution, identification of research gaps, suggestions for submission directions, and other full‑dimensional analyses. Trigger conditions: the user mentions a need to analyze the publishing trends, topic preferences, or submission directions of a particular C‑journal/CSSCI journal/core journal; or provides a Chinese academic journal name and requests to view recent thematic distribution and trend of published papers; or says “help me analyze XX journal.” Note: this skill is for macro‑level journal analysis, distinct from paper‑analyzer (single‑paper breakdown) and literature‑review‑writer (literature review writing).
