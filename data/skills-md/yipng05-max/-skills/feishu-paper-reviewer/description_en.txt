Feishu Document Paper Review Tool. Directly conduct academic paper reviews on Feishu Cloud Documents, supporting various revision marks such as highlighting, strikethrough, bold color changes, word-level comments, and inserting review comments. It is triggered when users mention reviewing, editing, evaluating, or annotating papers in Feishu Documents/Cloud Documents, or provide a Feishu document link requesting a review. Keywords: Feishu paper review, Feishu manuscript review, cloud document evaluation, Feishu paper annotation.
