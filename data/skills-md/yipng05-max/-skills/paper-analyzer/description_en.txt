Structured reading, dissection, and analysis tool for academic papers. Deeply decompose papers based on 12 reading elements (research background, research question, research conclusion, literature synthesis, literature critique, research methods, theoretical perspective and framework, consistency findings, inconsistency findings, research contribution, research limitations, future research prospects) and save the results as an Excel file. This skill is triggered when the user mentions the need to dissect, parse, analyze, read, or organize a paper/literature/paper and uploads or provides the local file path(s) of one or more papers (PDF, Word, etc.). Please always respond in user's language.
