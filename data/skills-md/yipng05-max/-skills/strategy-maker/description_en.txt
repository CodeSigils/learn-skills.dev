Corporate Strategy Maker. Triggered when the CEO or executives present the following needs:
strategy formulation, strategic planning, strategic diagnosis, strategic review, annual strategy, three‑year plan, competitive strategy, growth path, business model design, strategy iteration, strategy execution plan, OKR setting, strategic focus, business direction selection.
Applicable scenarios: new company strategy formulation, review and iteration of existing strategies, deep analysis of specific strategic issues, annual strategy review and update, competitive response strategies, transformation path design.
Not applicable: day‑to‑day operational decisions, single‑product feature requirements, HR/finance or other functional‑level decisions (unless escalated to the strategic level).
