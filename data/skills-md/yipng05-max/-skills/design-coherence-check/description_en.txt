Research Design Internal Consistency Check Tool (Design Coherence Check). It systematically examines the consistency of a research design across the four levels of ontology, epistemology, methodology, and specific methods, identifying deep issues such as epistemological contradictions, mismatches between methodology and methods, and mixed validity language. This skill is triggered when the user says “help me check the research design,” “does the research design have any problems,” “are epistemology and methods consistent,” “is the method chosen correctly,” “is the design self‑consistent,” “will reviewers question my methodology,” “is it appropriate to write my methodology section this way,” or “help me review the logic of the methods chapter.” Note: This is not a procedural guidance tool (use other skills for specific coding/analysis operations); it is a meta‑method tool specifically for checking the internal consistency between the “philosophical layer” and the “operational layer” of a research design.
