Academic paper title/abstract/keyword generation tool. Executed in three steps:
Step 1: Generate 4 candidate titles (different angles + applicable scenarios + pros and cons analysis),
Step 2: Generate 3 abstract versions (different structures and emphases),
Step 3: Generate 2 sets of keyword schemes (different retrieval strategies, with a rationale for each term).
Pause after each step to wait for the user to make a selection before proceeding to the next step.
Trigger when the user needs to generate a paper title/abstract/keywords, or at checkpoint 10 of ta‑research‑AFP.
