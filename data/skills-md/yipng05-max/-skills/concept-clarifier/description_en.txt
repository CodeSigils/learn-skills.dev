Concept Clarifier tool. It provides in‑depth analysis of the theoretical lineage, internal tensions, boundary conditions, and operationalization methods of concepts in sociology and related disciplines. It distinguishes substantive differences between similar concepts to prevent conceptual mixing. This skill is triggered when the user says “What does this concept mean?”, “What is the difference between XX and YY?”, “Can this concept be applied to my research?”, “What are the core concepts of this theory?”, “How is the concept operationalized?”, “Can these two concepts be used interchangeably?”, or “Help me map out this concept.” Note: This is not an encyclopedia‑style definition lookup tool, but a deep‑analysis instrument that helps researchers use concepts precisely in their work, understand conceptual limitations, and avoid labeling.
