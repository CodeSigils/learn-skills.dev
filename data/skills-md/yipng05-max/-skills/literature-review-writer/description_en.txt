Top‑journal literature review (Literature Review) writing tool. Based on the research topic or question provided by the user, it automatically retrieves authentic academic literature, verifies the literature’s authenticity, and composes the literature‑review section according to the writing standards of leading journals (e.g., AMJ, ASQ, SMJ, MISQ, OS, JCR, JMR, etc.). It employs a funnel‑shaped structure, emphasizing the contextual relationships, evolutionary trends, and research gaps among the sources. This skill is triggered when the user indicates a need to write a literature review, provides a research topic/direction, or requests a review in top‑journal style. Note: This skill is for writing literature reviews, not to be confused with paper‑analyzer (which dissects existing papers).
