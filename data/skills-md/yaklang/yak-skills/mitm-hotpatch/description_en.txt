Yakit MITM Hot Reload (Hot Patch) topic. It is used when users need to dynamically hijack or modify requests and responses with code in the MITM proxy, mirror and analyze traffic, tag/color data before storage, or mock dangerous operations with protective safeguards. It overrides all hooks and forward/drop semantics for hijackHTTPRequest, hijackHTTPResponseEx, the mirror series, hijackSaveHTTPFlow, and mockHTTPRequest.
