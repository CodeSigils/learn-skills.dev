From an idea or a piece of copy, create a complete short video in the “archive collage” style—full‑page ivory paper, monochrome black‑and‑white printing, a single dark brick‑red hue; the shots are driven by MiniMax H3 start‑and‑end frames, the voice‑over uses Fish Audio, and the final video is assembled with ffmpeg. It is used when the user wants to make a video with an archive‑collage/editing‑design/paper‑collage style, or to turn a piece of copy, a topic, or a news item into an explanatory short. The workflow includes environment installation checks, breaking down story, visuals, and rhythm, generating each shot, and speed‑adjusted splicing.
