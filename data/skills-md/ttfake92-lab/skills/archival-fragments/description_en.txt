From an idea or a piece of copy, create a complete “archive collage”‑style short video—full‑frame ivory paper, monochrome black‑and‑white printing, a single dark brick‑red tone, shots driven by MiniMax H3 start‑and‑end frames, voiceover using Fish Audio, and the final video assembled with ffmpeg. It is used when a user wants to produce a video in an archive‑collage/edit‑design/paper‑collage style, or to turn a piece of copy, a topic, or a news item into a narrated short. The workflow includes environment installation checking, breaking down story, visuals, and rhythm, per‑shot generation, and speed‑adjusted clipping.
