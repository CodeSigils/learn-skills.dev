Public account article pipeline · Take a written Markdown/HTML article through deterministic mechanical steps, finally storing it in your own public account’s **draft box** (only saved as a draft, never mass‑sent; later you go to the public account backend to confirm and manually mass‑send). It automates subsequent operations: load identity context → whoami verify target account → pre‑check → md→public‑account HTML rendering → local image pre‑upload → cover → save draft → report. Used when the user wants to write a public‑account article, convert public‑account formatting, push a public‑account draft, re‑push a draft, publish with a cover to the draft box, store an article in the public‑account draft box, or run the public‑account article pipeline. You must first bind your public account on doubaoya.com and have a DOUBAOYA_API_KEY. Trigger words: write public account / convert public account formatting / push public account draft / re‑push draft / publish with cover to draft box / store article in public account draft box / public account article pipeline / wechat-article-pipeline.
