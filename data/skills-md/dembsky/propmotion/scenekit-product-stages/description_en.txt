Build and debug SceneKit scenes where one 3D object (a product, badge, coin, wheel) performs on a transparent stage inside a SwiftUI app - studio lighting, real shadows, baked keyframe choreography, hand-rolled physics, gestures and haptics, multi-scene sequencing. Use when working with SCNView or SceneView in SwiftUI, UIViewRepresentable 3D scenes, product or hero-object animation, roll or spin entrances, a first-frame hitch when a scene appears, shadows missing or wrong, metal rendering black, or choreographed 3D motion that must stay interruptible. Not for RealityKit, ARKit, visionOS, or full game worlds.
