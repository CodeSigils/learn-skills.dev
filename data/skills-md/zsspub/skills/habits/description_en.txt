Manages daily check-in records stored locally in SQLite, supporting adding check-ins, viewing records, statistical analysis, consecutive check-in days, deletion, and modification. When a user mentions check-ins, sign-ins, logging exercise/reading/studying/fitness/meditation/running/cycling or other daily habits, or wants to see how many days they’ve kept up a habit, how much time they exercised this week, or what they checked in for today, this skill should be used proactively. Even if the user doesn’t explicitly say “check-in,” as long as it involves tracking daily habits or activity logging, it applies. English scenarios are also supported, such as “check in,” “log my workout,” “track my reading,” “how many days in a row,” “streak,” “habits.”
