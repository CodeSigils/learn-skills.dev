Manual image resource optimization task for Phaser 4 projects. It should be used only when the user explicitly invokes $phaser4-game-image-optimization, to inventory runtime bitmaps, generate compression candidates, verify visual quality and channel compatibility, and replace with smaller resources in a reversible manner; it is prohibited from being implicitly triggered by the master controller, build, test, release, A1 through A6, or G0 through G3.
