Short-drama and product-video development skill for planning drama projects through a bundled Busabase App-in-Skill UI, with each project linked to a specified HyperFrame project and each episode linked to a HyperFrame composition for final motion/video tuning. Use when the user asks to create, edit, organize, or review short-drama scripts, product explainer episodes, character consistency cards, series bibles, episode beat sheets, storyboard shots, HyperFrame project/composition links, production checklists, or a drama planning workbench.
