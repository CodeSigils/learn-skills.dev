Löst aus auf: Punkt, ObservablePoint, Matrix, Rechteck, Kreis, Polygon, Dreieck, RoundedRectangle, toGlobal, toLocal, hitArea, StrokeContains, Pad, Fit, Enlarge, Ceil, GetBounds, IncludesRect, Intersects, IsClockwise, Math-Extras, LineIntersection, SegmentIntersection, DEG_TO_RAD, PointData.
