Based on the target position JD, a Chinese résumé, and the user‑verified real experiences, it performs JD analysis, evidence mapping, gap diagnosis, material probing, customized résumé rewriting, ATS/HR/interviewer review, and generates submission communication materials for Mainland China job‑search scenarios. It is applicable to fresh graduates, corporate hires, career changers, and roles in internet, AI, product, operations, sales, technology, data, design, etc.; it is used when a user requests JD‑focused optimisation of a Chinese résumé, improved matching, creation of Boss Direct Hire or headhunter scripts, or validation of interview coherence.
