Triggers on: 'migrate from The Graph', 'move my subgraph to Goldsky', 'switch off The Graph', 'redeploy a TheGraph subgraph', 'my subgraph endpoint is gone', 'from-url', 'from-ipfs-hash', 'drop-in replacement for The Graph'.
