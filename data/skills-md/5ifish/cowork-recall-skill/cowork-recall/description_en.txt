A local session retrieval and recall hub for cross‑AI coding agents (ZCode, Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Cline, Roo Code, Continue, OpenCode, Qoder, WorkBuddy, CodeBuddy, Kimi Code, Trae). Search related sessions across apps by keyword or time range, paginate through aggregated session history, view the full dialogue and discussion conclusions of a specific session, and generate daily, weekly, or custom‑period work summaries based on this. Used when the user asks “Which previous session discussed X?”, “What did I chat with the AI about last week?”, “Summarize what was done today”, or “Generate a daily/weekly report”.
