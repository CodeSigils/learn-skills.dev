News aggregation and information acquisition skill. Based on pre-configured sources, invoke search-and-fetch to grab the latest news and output structured summaries. Triggered when the user needs a news briefing, wants to follow the latest developments on a topic, or check the newest content from a specific outlet. Trigger scenarios: today’s news, news briefing, information, briefing, latest updates, what’s new with X today, track for me, daily report, morning report, evening report. Even if the user doesn’t say “news,” this skill activates whenever the request involves obtaining the latest information, industry trends, or hot-topic tracking.
