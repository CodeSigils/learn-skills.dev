Unified pitch deck skill that sequences meta-pitch-preparation → meta-presentation-design in a single workflow. Takes a completed business plan (or section set) and produces a presentation-ready pitch deck plus delivery training programme. Covers investor pitches, bank/DFI loan presentations, client proposals, grant presentations, and board presentations. Call this skill when a client needs both the deck and the pitch — it orchestrates the two meta-skills so nothing falls through the gap between content strategy and slide design.
