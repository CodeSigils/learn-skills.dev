Dual-purpose pitch preparation meta-skill for (A) preparing yourself to pitch and (B) coaching clients to pitch. Covers investor pitches, bank/DFI loan presentations, client proposal pitches, grant presentations, and board presentations. Integrates Klaff's STRONG method and frame control (Pitch Anything), McGowan's Hook-Meat-Payoff and 7 Principles (Pitch Perfect), and the business plan skill suite's financial and market frameworks. Use before any live pitch, presentation, or proposal defence.
