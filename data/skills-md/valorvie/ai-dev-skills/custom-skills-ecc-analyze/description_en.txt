Review the ECC (everything-claude-code) distribution list, which includes two modes:
(A) Existing list slimming: Analyze the overlap and quality between distribution.yaml.enabled and the first‑party ai-dev-skills, and output enabled_remove recommendations;
(B) New item introduction evaluation: Based on ecc-catalog.yaml and the scope of the last update, introduce newly added upstream ECC skills, analyze applicable scenarios, and recommend whether to add them to enabled.
Use when: (1) assessing whether the ECC distribution list should be slimmed down, (2) deciding whether to adopt a newly added upstream ECC skill, (3) creating an ecc-profile.yaml starter for end users, (4) conducting a quarterly or semiannual regular review.
Triggers: "Analyze ECC skill", "ECC overlap", "ECC list slimming", "ECC new item", "ECC upstream addition", "Whether to adopt", "ecc analyze", "ecc new items review", "ecc profile starter", "ECC quality review".
