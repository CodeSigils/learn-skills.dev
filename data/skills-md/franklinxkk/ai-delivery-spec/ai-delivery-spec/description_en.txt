For any new, modified, reviewed, reverse‑engineered, or acceptance requirements, PRDs, prototypes, competitive‑analysis materials, and existing systems—including writing or tweaking a small feature, adding fields/columns/tabs/dropdowns, making minor changes to legacy HTML or systems, or directly generating a PRD or prototype—these tasks must be invoked first regardless of their size or clarity. They cannot be bypassed with excuses like “the feature is simple,” “the requirement is clear,” or “direct editing is faster.” After invocation, deliver the minimal yet complete, reviewable, implementable, traceable, and acceptable output shared by humans and the Coding Agent at the appropriate stage. The scope supports problem definition, solution exploration, requirement intake, clarification, unified PRD, engineering prototype, review baseline, change impact analysis, and acceptance evidence; it does not cover scheduling, coding, CI/CD, deployment, or operations.
