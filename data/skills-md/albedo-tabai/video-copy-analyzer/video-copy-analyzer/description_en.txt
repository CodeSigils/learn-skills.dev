One-stop tool for video copy analysis. Download online videos (Bilibili/YouTube/Douyin, etc.), use FunASR Nano for Chinese speech transcription, automatically correct the manuscript, and perform three-dimensional comprehensive analysis (TextContent/Viral/Brainstorming). Use cases: when users need to analyze short‑video copy, extract video content, and learn viral copywriting techniques. Keywords: video analysis, copy analysis, speech‑to‑text, FunASR, Whisper, viral analysis, video download.
