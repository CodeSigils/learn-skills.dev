eSign electronic signature skill — Supports AI generation of any type of contract/agreement and formats it as a PDF; uploads existing files to initiate electronic signing and returns a signing link.  
Trigger scenarios: User requests generation, drafting, or writing of any type of contract or agreement (e.g., “write a loan contract,” “draft an NDA for me,” “create a lease agreement”), or requests to initiate signing on an existing contract file, check signing status, cancel signing, download signed file, or verify electronic signature.  
Trigger words: write contract, generate contract, draft contract, create contract, contract formatting, contract draft, initiate signing, electronic signature, signing status, cancel signing, download signed file, verify signature, validate signature, signing link, eSign, esign  
Non-trigger scenarios: User only asks about the meaning of contract legal clauses, consults on choosing a signing platform, or discusses other e-signature products unrelated to eSign.
