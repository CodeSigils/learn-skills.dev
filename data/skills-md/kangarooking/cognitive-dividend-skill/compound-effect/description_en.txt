Design a growth model with compound-interest effects. Use it when you’ve worked on something for a long time with slow progress and feel like quitting, or when you want your efforts to create a “the more you do, the faster it gets” dynamic. Core steps: find the leverage point (validate cause-and-effect) → design a reinforcing loop (A strengthens B, B in turn strengthens A) → persist until a milestone. Not suitable for one-off tasks, short-term projects, or events with unclear causality. Key trigger signals: “Why hasn’t it worked after all this time?” “Should I keep going?” “How can I accelerate growth?”
