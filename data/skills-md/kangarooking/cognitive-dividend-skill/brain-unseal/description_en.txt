Unlock the two seals on the brain (negative words and negative emotions) to restore rational thinking ability. This applies to scenarios where the brain "freezes" and cannot think when facing pressure, setbacks, or conflicts, as well as long‑term optimization of thinking patterns. Use this skill when you fall into a self‑defeating loop of "I can't," "I’m not able to," or "There's no way," or when you are overwhelmed by emotions such as anger, anxiety, or fear. It is not suitable for mental health issues that require deep psychotherapy (e.g., depression, anxiety that need professional medical care), nor is it suitable for pure knowledge‑learning bottlenecks (those are method issues, not seal issues). Key trigger signals: “I can’t/I’m not able to,” “It makes me so angry/I’m frantic,” “My mind goes blank,” “The more I think, the more annoyed I get,” “I can’t control myself.”
