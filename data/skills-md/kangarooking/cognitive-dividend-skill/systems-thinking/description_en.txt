Analyze complex systems from elements to relationships, from parts to the whole, from static to dynamic. Use it when problems keep recurring, when a small change ripples through everything, when treating the symptom fails. Switch to systems thinking for a root-cause solution whenever “the harder you try, the worse it gets” or “the issue always comes back.” It is unsuited to simple single-cause problems or emergencies demanding immediate action. Key trigger signals: “Why does this problem keep returning?”, “Fixing A broke B,” “Feels like we’re stuck in a dead loop.”
