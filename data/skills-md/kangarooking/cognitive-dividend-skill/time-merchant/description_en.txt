Framework for assessing and upgrading personal time management models. Use it when you feel “I work hard but my income doesn’t increase” or “people with the same workload earn much more than me.” Core formula: Personal Gross Product = time unit price × time that generates value. Four models from lowest to highest: retail → wholesale → trade → taxation. Not applicable to those who already have a platform‑level business model. Key trigger signals: “I’m busy every day but my income doesn’t grow,” “Why do others earn more without working as hard?” “How to break through the income ceiling?”
