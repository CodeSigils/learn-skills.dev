Identify which zone of the mirror world the current event falls into—Domain of Control, Blind Spot, Probability Zone, or Risk Zone—and apply the corresponding survival strategy; avoid using the deterministic rules of the left-side world to confront the uncertain problems of the right-side world. Suitable for crafting strategies around investment decisions, entrepreneurial choices, and career transitions, as well as for post-mortems asking, “Why did the plan still fail?” Not applicable to pure skill-building or deliberate-practice scenarios, which naturally belong to the Domain of Control. Key trigger signals: “Why isn’t effort paying off?”, “Does this depend on skill or luck?”, “How should I deal with uncertainty?”, “How can I raise the odds of success?”
