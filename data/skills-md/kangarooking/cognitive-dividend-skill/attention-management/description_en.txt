A framework for managing attention allocation. Use it when you realize you’re spending a large amount of time each day on gossip hot searches, aimlessly scrolling short videos, or passively receiving push content, and need to re‑examine where your attention is going. Core logic: Attention is the only inherently productive asset that we can voluntarily control; where attention goes determines what we produce. It is not applicable to those who already have stable deep‑work habits and a clear attention distribution. Key trigger signals: “Where did my time go?”, “I ended up scrolling on my phone all night again,” “Why am I so tired but have produced nothing?”
