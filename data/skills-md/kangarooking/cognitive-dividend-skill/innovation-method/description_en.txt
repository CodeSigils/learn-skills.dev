Achieve innovation through two systematic approaches: recombinant innovation (gathering elements from the outside and recombining them) and mutational innovation (taking apart and altering existing structures from within). Every innovation must simultaneously satisfy three components: new element + value increment + feasibility. Applicable when you need to break existing frameworks, seek new products/solutions, or fix the “innovation depends on luck” problem. Not suited for purely executional optimization (which requires no new elements). Key trigger signals: “need new ideas,” “how to innovate,” “creativity dried up,” “severe product homogeneity.”
