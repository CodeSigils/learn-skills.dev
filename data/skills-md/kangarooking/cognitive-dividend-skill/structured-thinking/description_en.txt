Break down complex problems into a clearly layered, non-overlapping, and exhaustive structural framework. Suitable for scenarios requiring organizing chaotic information, communicating clearly to others, or conducting comprehensive problem analysis. Use this skill when facing scattered elements that prevent forming a holistic view, or when needing to deliver structured presentations to others. Not applicable to already highly structured problems or rapid decision-making scenarios requiring intuitive judgment. Key trigger signals: "too much information to organize," "how to explain this clearly," "did I miss anything," "help me break down this problem."
