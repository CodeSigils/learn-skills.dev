Create scarcity by combining multiple abilities, turning personal value from “replaceable” to “unique”. This applies to career planning, personal brand positioning, and core competency design scenarios. Use this skill when you feel “I know a little of everything but am not proficient in anything,” or when you want to build a unique competitive barrier. It is not suitable for stages where no strong individual capability has been established yet (first develop a single-point value before combining), nor for scenarios that require deep specialization in a single professional field (such as academic research). Key trigger signals: “I don’t have any special advantage,” “Others can do what I do,” “How can I become irreplaceable,” “I have broad interests but can’t make money.”
