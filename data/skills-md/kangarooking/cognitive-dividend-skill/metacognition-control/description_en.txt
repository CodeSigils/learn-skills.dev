Control your brain's information processing workflow through three metacognitive stages. Use when you "know what to do but can't do it," when emotions hijack rational thinking, or when you need to systematically improve brain information processing quality. Three stages: control input (attention gates information entry) → control brain (discard/store/process information) → control output (execute thinking results with high quality). Not suitable for emergency scenarios requiring rapid instinctive reactions. Key trigger signals: "know but can't do," "I'm being emotional again," "my mind is chaotic," "learned but can't apply."
