Make rational, objective decisions in four steps: keep your goal in sight and filter out the trivial → find a third option to break past the limits of the given choices → sidestep cognitive biases → score the options quantitatively for the final pick. Use it when you’re stuck on a major decision, the options seem either-or, or you fear being misled by intuition. Don’t use it for everyday low-risk choices—over-quantifying just wastes time. Key trigger phrases: “I can’t decide between A and B,” “I don’t know how to choose,” “Neither option satisfies me but I must pick one,” “Help me decide.”
