Activate this skill when the user is generally passive but needs to create initiative locally. Typical trigger signals: the company as a whole is contracting, morale is low, and under a resource disadvantage it doesn’t know how to break the deadlock; macro-level passivity requires finding a micro-level breakthrough. Scenarios where it is not used: both sides have comparable strength and compete normally, there is already an overall advantage that only needs to be executed, and the issue is not about switching between offense and defense. Difference from the “Sixteen‑Character Formula” skill: this skill solves “how to generate micro‑level initiative within macro‑level passivity” (the dialectic of offense and defense), whereas the latter solves “choosing rhythm based on the opponent’s state” (timing judgment).
