Activate this skill when the user wavers between confidence and caution—either blindly optimistic and reckless, or overly anxious and timid.  
Typical trigger signals: the team loses confidence in the face of difficulty, or conversely becomes overinflated during smooth periods, or wavers indecisively when making decisions.  
Scenarios where it should not be invoked: the team’s condition is healthy (both confident and cautious), pure execution tasks that don’t require psychological adjustment, or the issue lies at a technical rather than mental level.  
Difference from the “Strategy‑Tactics Dialectics” skill: this skill addresses “the executor’s psychological state management” (mindset layer), whereas the latter deals with “strategic design of offense‑defense hierarchies” (strategy layer).
