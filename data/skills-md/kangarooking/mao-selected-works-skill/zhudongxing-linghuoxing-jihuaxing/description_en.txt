When the user's team execution capability encounters problems—such as failing to complete OKRs, constantly changing plans, or the team being passive—activate this skill.  
Typical trigger signals: the goal is set but execution deviates, the plan cannot keep up with changes, the team only does assigned tasks without proactive thinking.  
Scenarios where it should not be invoked: execution is already good and only needs fine‑tuning, the issue lies at the strategic level rather than the execution level, or a single person’s work does not require a team execution framework.  
Difference from the "Strategic‑Tactical Dialectics" skill: this skill addresses "how to balance the three dimensions of execution" (execution layer), whereas the other addresses "dialectical design of offense‑defense hierarchies" (strategy layer).
