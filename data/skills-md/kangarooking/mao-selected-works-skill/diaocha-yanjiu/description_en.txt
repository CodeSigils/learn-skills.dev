Activate when the user is about to make a major decision based on second‑hand information (reports, rumors, others’ summaries), or when the team shows a tendency to “sit in the office and guess.”  
Typical trigger signals: “I’ve read a few industry reports,” “Just send someone to do some research,” “Based on my experience, it should be like this.”  
Scenarios not to invoke: decisions already based on sufficient primary information, low‑risk small decisions that don’t require deep research, existing reliable data sources are being continuously monitored.  
Difference from the “Seek Truth from Facts” information processing method: this skill focuses on “how to collect high‑quality primary information,” whereas the latter focuses on “how to process and refine information after it is obtained.”
