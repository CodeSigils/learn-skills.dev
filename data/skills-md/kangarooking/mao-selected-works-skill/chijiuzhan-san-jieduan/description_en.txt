Activated when the user faces a clearly stronger opponent (such as a giant entering the market, a resource gap, or a brand disadvantage) and needs to plan a long‑term competitive strategy.  
Typical trigger signals: “Big companies are entering our niche too,” “Their resources are a hundred times ours,” “What do we do if we can’t beat them?”  
Scenarios where it is not invoked: evenly matched or self‑advantaged competitive situations, challenges that can be resolved in the short term, non‑competitive decisions.  
Difference from “concentrate superior forces”: this skill outlines the overall timeline and phased strategy, whereas the latter focuses on resource deployment for specific campaigns.
