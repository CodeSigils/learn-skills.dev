When users face multiple interwoven problems, cannot determine priorities, and fall into decision paralysis of “everything is important,” activate this skill. Typical trigger signals: company growth stalls while several departments report errors simultaneously, resources are limited but demand is infinite, the team debates direction endlessly and cannot focus. Scenarios where it should not be invoked: the problem is already clear and singular, only execution is needed rather than analysis, the user wants a tool operation rather than strategic judgment. Difference from the “Concentrate Strength” skill: this skill solves “seeing the full picture of the problems and identifying bottlenecks,” whereas the latter solves “how to concentrate resources to break through after seeing.”
