Activate this skill when the user is at a disadvantage against a stronger competitor and feels “passive and unable to cope everywhere.”  
Typical trigger signals: a small brand facing pressure from industry giants; a startup having its product copied by a big company; not knowing how to compete with a resource disadvantage.  
Scenarios where it is not invoked: both sides have comparable strength or we have the advantage; a clear competitive edge already exists; the issue is not at the competition level.  
Difference from the “Sixteen‑Word Formula” skill: this skill solves “how the weaker side defines the competitive paradigm” (strategic level), whereas the latter solves “what to do at each specific moment” (tactical/rhythmic level).
