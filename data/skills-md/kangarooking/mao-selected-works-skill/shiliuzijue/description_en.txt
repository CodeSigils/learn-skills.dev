When the user needs to choose their own action rhythm based on the competitor’s state, activate this skill.  
Typical trigger signals: facing a strong opponent and not knowing whether to attack or defend, the rhythm being driven by the opponent, passive responses always lagging one step.  
Scenarios where it is not invoked: a blue‑ocean market with no clear opponent, a stable competitive situation that doesn’t require rhythm adjustment, the user needs strategic planning rather than action pacing.  
Difference from the “asymmetric strategy” skill: this skill solves “whether to attack or defend right now” (the rhythm layer), whereas the latter solves “which paradigm to compete with” (the strategic layer).
