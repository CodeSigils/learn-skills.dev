Activate this skill when the user falls into “analysis paralysis” (wants to act but is afraid to) or “blind action” (acts without summarizing or improving). Typical trigger signals: “I need to finish the research before I start,” “I don’t know if I understand the industry well enough to start a business,” “We’ve been doing it all along but have no methodology.” Scenarios where it is not invoked: situations where a mature methodology already exists and is functioning effectively, or pure theoretical learning needs. Difference from the “pragmatic information processing method”: this skill focuses on the complete cognitive loop (practice → insight → practice again), whereas the latter focuses on the specific techniques of information processing within a single loop.
