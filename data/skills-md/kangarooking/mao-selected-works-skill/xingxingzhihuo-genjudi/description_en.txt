When the user is in an early, weak stage and needs to plan a 0‑to‑1 expansion path, activate this skill.  
Typical trigger signals: limited resources but big ambitions, facing the choice of “full rollout versus focused breakthrough,” or the team showing a “bandit” tendency (chasing trends without deep cultivation).  
Scenarios where it is not invoked: a solid base already exists that only needs optimization, the market size is limited and does not require expansion, or the problem lies at the execution level rather than the strategic path.  
Difference from the “asymmetric strategy” skill: this skill addresses “where to play and how to expand” (the path layer), whereas the latter addresses “what paradigm to compete with” (the strategic layer).
