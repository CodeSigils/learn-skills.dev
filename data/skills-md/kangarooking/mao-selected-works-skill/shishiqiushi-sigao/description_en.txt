Activated when the user faces a large amount of chaotic information (user feedback, competitor data, market reports, internal metrics) and needs to extract decision signals from it.  
Typical trigger signals: “There’s too much data, I don’t know which to trust,” “The user says everything,” “I received a bunch of feedback but don’t know what the conclusion is.”  
Scenarios where it is not invoked: the information is already structured and clear, requiring only simple judgment; or there is no information at all, requiring intuitive decisions.  
Difference from the “research method”: this skill is information processing (how to handle the raw material once obtained), whereas the latter is information gathering (how to obtain good raw material).
