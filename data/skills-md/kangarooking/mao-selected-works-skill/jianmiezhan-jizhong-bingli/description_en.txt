Activated when the user has limited resources but faces multiple opportunities or threats and needs to make trade‑offs among several directions. Typical trigger signals: “Five customer opportunities appear at the same time,” “Should we pursue B2B and B2C simultaneously,” “What to do when resources are insufficient.”  

Scenarios where it is not invoked: resources are abundant and multiple lines can proceed in parallel, the main direction has already been decided and only execution is needed, a single goal with no trade‑offs.  

Difference from the “contradiction analysis method”: this skill solves “how to concentrate resources to completely resolve the issue after identifying the bottleneck,” whereas the latter solves “where the bottleneck is.”
