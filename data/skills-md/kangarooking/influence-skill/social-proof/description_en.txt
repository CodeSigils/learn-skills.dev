When users tend to rely on others' behavior for decision‑making in uncertain situations, or find themselves following the crowd without being able to explain why, this skill is activated. Typical scenarios: trusting a “Best Seller” label, doing what others do in a new environment, being influenced by fabricated social evidence (canned laughter, fake reviews). Not applicable: decisions with clear standards and professional knowledge, deliberate social learning and imitation, rational comparisons that consciously reference others' experiences.
