When the user needs to amplify the product’s advantage over competitors, invoke this skill. The core operation is to first describe the competitor’s shortcomings and fewer benefits, then describe one’s own strengths and greater benefits, using the psychological principle of contrast to create a mental gap. It is especially suitable for products in mature categories that are “better in certain aspects.” It is not suitable for brand‑new categories (no competitors to compare) nor for brand‑image advertising (comparison appears low‑brow). Key trigger signals: the user mentions “the product is better than the competitor but readers don’t perceive it,” “how to highlight differentiated advantages,” or “the selling points are too bland and we want to amplify the effect.”
