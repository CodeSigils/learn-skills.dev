When a user is preparing to make a major decision (investment/acquisition/commitment) and needs to evaluate whether downside protection is sufficient. Or when deciding whether to act amid valuation uncertainty.
Language signals: "Is this price low enough" / "What if I'm wrong" / "How much loss can I afford" / "How much downside is there"
Not applicable to: everyday low-risk decisions, pure upside analysis (when downside protection is not a concern).
