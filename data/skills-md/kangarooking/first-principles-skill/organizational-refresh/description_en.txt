Activate when the user is a manager/founder/leader facing stagnant growth, rigid culture, strategic transformation difficulties, or asking "how to drive organizational change," "how to transform corporate culture," or "how to define mission and vision."

Core signal: The user is describing organizational-level issues and has already recognized that "it's not a business problem but a fundamental problem."

Not applicable to: Personal growth issues (unless explicitly mapped to organizational context), purely tactical business adjustments.
