When users feel the tool is inadequate for complex decision-making/analysis, or ask, “How can I see the issue more comprehensively?” “Why do I always think in the same way?” “How do I build an interdisciplinary mindset?” “What is Munger’s latticework of mental models?”
Core signal: The user realizes their thinking tools are too narrow and needs to build a diverse cognitive toolkit.
Not applicable to: deep specialization in a single field or routine problems that don’t require an interdisciplinary perspective.
