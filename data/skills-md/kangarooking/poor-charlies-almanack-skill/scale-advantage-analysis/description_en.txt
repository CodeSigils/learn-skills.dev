When the user needs to analyze the competitive landscape of an enterprise/organization/product, evaluate whether "getting bigger" is truly better, or determine whether a company's sources of moat are sustainable. Typical trigger signals: topics involving "economies of scale," "market share," "the predicament of large enterprises," "first-mover advantage," "network effects," "winner-take-all," etc. Not applicable for pure financial analysis (use value-assessment) or pure cognitive bias checks (use misjudgment-checklist).
