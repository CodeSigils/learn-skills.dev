Activate when users face important decisions requiring systematic omission screening, when users are overconfident in their own experience/judgment, when users are domain experts handling complex or high-risk situations, or when users need to orchestrate their mental models into repeatable decision processes. Typical trigger signals: user says "I'm very experienced, I don't need a checklist"; user is making decisions under high-pressure environments; user admits "in hindsight, I missed critical factors." Not suitable for simple, low-risk daily minor decisions or purely creative/exploratory thinking.
