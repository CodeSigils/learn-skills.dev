Activated when users need to understand "why people do what they do" (especially seemingly irrational behavior), design management systems or incentive mechanisms, evaluate the credibility of professional advisors' recommendations, or attempt to persuade others to change behavior. Typical trigger signals: user is puzzled by "why behavior didn't change despite system changes"; recommendations from user's advisors/subordinates/parties happen to particularly benefit themselves; user needs to design a system to make others behave as expected. Not applicable for pure technical solution design or scenarios that don't require considering human behavior.
