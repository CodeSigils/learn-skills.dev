Activated when users need to simultaneously verify "whether rational analysis is correct" and "whether my judgment is distorted by cognitive biases" during important decision-making. Typical trigger signals: user's analytical logic is rigorous but intuition feels uneasy, or the analysis flows too smoothly and seems "flawless." Not applicable to purely technical problems (such as code debugging, mathematical calculations) where human judgment biases do not exist, nor applicable to purely emotional/interpersonal issues where rational frameworks are irrelevant.
