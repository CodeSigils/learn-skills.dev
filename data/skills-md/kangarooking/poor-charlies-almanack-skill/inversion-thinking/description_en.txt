Activated when users face complex decisions, planning, or problem-solving—especially when forward reasoning is stuck, there are too many options to prioritize, or hidden risks need to be identified. Typical trigger signals: users get stuck when asking "how can I succeed," "how can I avoid failure," or "what should I do." Not applicable to simple tasks where the optimal path is already clear and only execution is needed, nor to scenarios requiring creative divergence rather than risk avoidance.
