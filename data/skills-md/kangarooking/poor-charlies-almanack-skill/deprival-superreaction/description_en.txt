Use when a user reacts with disproportionate intensity to losing (or the prospect of losing) something, or, after a loss, rushes to “get even” and cannot cut further losses. Typical trigger signals: sunk-cost trap, gambler-style doubling down, irrational bidding at auctions, inability to abandon a failing project into which heavy resources have already been sunk. Not applicable to ordinary investment valuation (use value-assessment) or herd behavior (use misjudgment-checklist).
