Activated when users consider making decisions in unfamiliar domains, or need to judge whether they truly "understand" something. Typical trigger signals: users say "I can learn this," "should be about the same," or "this isn't hard" but lack deep experience in the relevant domain. Not applicable to scenarios where users already have long-term successful track records within the domain and are operating within their circle of competence, nor to early exploration stages where risk-taking and trial-and-error are necessary to expand boundaries.
