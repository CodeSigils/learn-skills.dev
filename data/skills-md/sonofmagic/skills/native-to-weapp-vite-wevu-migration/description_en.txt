A structured migration workflow for migrating native mini-programs to the `weapp-vite + wevu + Vue SFC` architecture, covering phased rollout, behavioral equivalence refactoring, `Page/Component` to `.vue` conversion, typed props/events, `definePageMeta`/layout, platform guards, `.weapp-vite` support files, automatic routing with AI workflow alignment, and post-migration screenshot/log/e2e validation with rollback checkpoints.
