An engineering handbook for repositories that adopt the weapp-vite project layout, covering weapp settings in `vite.config.ts`, `app.json` routes/subPackages, chunk strategies, and CI/DevTools automation. Ideal for creating or refactoring weapp-vite projects, configuring auto-routing/auto-components, splitting subpackages, optimizing chunk output, or troubleshooting build and output issues (e.g., "configure weapp-vite", "subpackage strategy", "build output anomalies", "typed-router.d.ts generation problems").
