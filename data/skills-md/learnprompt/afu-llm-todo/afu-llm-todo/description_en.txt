Even if the user merely says “my Obsidian has a pile of unprocessed stuff,” it should trigger.  

Do not use for: ordinary task management unrelated to an Obsidian/Markdown vault (use a regular todo tool); rewriting, polishing, or summarizing the original note content (Afu only organizes location, does not alter the original); Notion or other non‑Markdown knowledge bases.
