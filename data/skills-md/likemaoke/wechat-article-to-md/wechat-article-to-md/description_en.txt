Scrape WeChat Official Account articles and convert them to Markdown documents. Use a script to automatically fetch the article title, author, and body content, preserve the formatting, and save it as an .md file; supports downloading images locally. Use case: the user requests to scrape/get/download/save WeChat Official Account article content in Markdown format, or provides an mp.weixin.qq.com link requiring content extraction. Supports Obsidian mode (use the -obsidian parameter).
