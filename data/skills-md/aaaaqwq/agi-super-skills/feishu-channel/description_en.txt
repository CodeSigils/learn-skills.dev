A two-way integration channel between Lark/Feishu and OpenClaw. Use a Feishu bot to receive and send messages, supporting private chats, group chats, @mention detection, card messages, and file transfers. Use this skill when you need to interact with an AI assistant via Feishu, trigger AI responses from incoming Feishu messages, or send messages from OpenClaw to Feishu. Difference from feishu-automation: this skill focuses on message-channel integration, while feishu-automation focuses on automating Feishu platform operations (e.g., multidimensional tables, documents).
