Feishu (Lark/Feishu) bidirectional integration channel with OpenClaw. It uses a Feishu bot to receive and send messages, supporting direct messages, group chats, @mention detection, card messages, and file transfer. Use this skill when you need to interact with an AI assistant through Feishu, trigger AI responses by receiving Feishu messages, or send messages from OpenClaw to Feishu. Difference from feishu-automation: this skill focuses on message channel integration, while feishu-automation focuses on automation operations on the Feishu platform (multidimensional tables, documents, etc.).
