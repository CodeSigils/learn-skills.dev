A bidirectional integration channel between Feishu (Lark) and OpenClaw. Enables message receiving and sending through Feishu bots, supporting private chats, group chats, @mention detection, card messages, and file transfers. Use this skill when you need to interact with AI assistants via Feishu, trigger AI responses from Feishu messages, or send messages from OpenClaw to Feishu. Difference from feishu-automation: This skill focuses on message channel integration, while feishu-automation focuses on Feishu platform automation operations (Bitable, documents, etc.).
