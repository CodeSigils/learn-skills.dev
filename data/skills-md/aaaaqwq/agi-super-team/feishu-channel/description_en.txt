Lark/Feishu and OpenClaw bidirectional integration channel. Enables message receiving and sending via Lark bot, supporting private chats, group chats, @mention detection, card messages, and file transfers. Use this skill when you need to interact with AI assistants through Lark, trigger AI responses from Lark messages, or send messages from OpenClaw to Lark. Difference from feishu-automation: this skill focuses on message channel integration, while feishu-automation focuses on Lark platform automation operations (Base tables, documents, etc.).
