Feishu (Lark/Feishu) and OpenClaw bidirectional integration channel. It enables receiving and sending messages via a Feishu bot, supporting private chats, group chats, @mention detection, card messages, and file transfer. Use this skill when you need to interact with an AI assistant through Feishu, trigger AI responses upon receiving Feishu messages, or send messages from OpenClaw to Feishu. Difference from feishu-automation: this skill focuses on message channel integration, whereas feishu-automation focuses on automation operations on the Feishu platform (multidimensional tables, documents, etc.).
