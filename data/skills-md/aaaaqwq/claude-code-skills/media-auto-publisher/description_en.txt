Universal self-media article auto-publishing tool. Supports automated publishing workflows for three platforms: Baijiahao, Sohuhao, and Zhihu. Utilizes the Playwright MCP tool to navigate platforms and access the publishing pages, and supports account switching by managing cookies via storageState. This skill is used when a user needs to publish an article to a self-media platform, open the platform's publishing page, or switch self-media accounts. Workflow: 1) Check and open the specified platform 2) Verify login status 3) Automatically close ad pop-ups 4) Navigate to the article publishing page 5) Collect publishing information 6) Automatically fill in and publish.
