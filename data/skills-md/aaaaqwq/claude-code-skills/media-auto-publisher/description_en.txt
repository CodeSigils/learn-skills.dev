A universal automated publishing tool for self-media articles. It supports automated publishing processes for three platforms: Baijiahao, Sohuhao, and Zhihu. It uses the Playwright MCP tool to navigate to platforms and enter the publishing page, and supports account switching by managing cookies through storageState. This skill is used when users need to publish articles to self-media platforms, open the platform's publishing page, or switch self-media accounts. Workflow: 1) Check and open the specified platform 2) Verify login status 3) Automatically close ad pop-ups 4) Navigate to the article publishing page 5) Collect publishing information 6) Automatically fill in and publish.
