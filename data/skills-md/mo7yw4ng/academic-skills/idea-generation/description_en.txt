Academic research idea generation skill—systematically producing high‑quality research concepts from divergent thinking to convergent focus. When users want to brainstorm research directions, find a new research idea, or ask “What research could I do next,” this skill must be used. Trigger words include: brainstorm, think of an idea, research direction, what to do next, what can be researched, find a gap, research proposal. It applies to generating academic research concepts at any stage.
