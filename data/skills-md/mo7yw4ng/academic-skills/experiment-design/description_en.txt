Academic research experiment design skill — a complete process from research hypothesis to a reproducible experimental plan. When users need to plan experiments, design ablation studies, select baselines, determine evaluation metrics, or ask “which experiments should I run,” they must use this skill. Trigger words include: experiment design, ablation, baseline, which experiments to run, evaluation metric, how to validate methods. Applicable to experiment planning in fields such as machine learning, NLP, CV, etc.
