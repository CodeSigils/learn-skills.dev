Academic Paper Review Skill — Complete an in‑depth manuscript evaluation using a structured four‑step process that includes critical review, score prediction, key‑point refinement, and formal review output. When a user needs to review a paper, simulate a reviewer’s response, assess whether the paper can be accepted, or help judge its strengths and weaknesses, this skill must be used. Trigger phrases include: “review this,” “help me review,” “what would the reviewer say,” “can this be accepted,” “paper review,” “give a score,” “find weakness.” Applicable to any academic paper’s review simulation and evaluation.
