Conduct market and business strategy analysis, including Competitor Analysis, SWOT/TOWS strategic derivation, Lean Canvas, and Pricing Strategy. Use this skill whenever the user needs to analyze the competitive landscape, compare strengths and weaknesses of competing products, assess strategic positioning, craft differentiated positioning, design business models, plan pricing schemes, or perform any form of market/business/strategic analysis. Even if the user mentions only one module (e.g., just SWOT), this skill must still be used.
