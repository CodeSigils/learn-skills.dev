Financial Content Platform — a professional research and content production toolkit for A‑shares and Hong Kong stocks targeting individual investors, researchers, and brokerage advisors/client managers. It is used for comprehensive analysis of company fundamentals, commentary on earnings reports, assessment of valuation, identification of financial risks, mapping of industry chains, and health checks of new A‑share/HK‑share listings; all users automatically receive the same professional research core and report‑level docx, client‑compliant versions generated on demand, and cards/copy/PPT triggered by user request. Stable commands: /caijing:fundamental, /caijing:earnings, /caijing:valuation, /caijing:risk, /caijing:industry, /caijing:ipo-a, /caijing:ipo-hk.
