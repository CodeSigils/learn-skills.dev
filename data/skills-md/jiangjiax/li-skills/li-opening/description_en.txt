Use this skill when the user says “optimize the opening,” “how to start,” “write me a hook,” “the intro is weak,” or supplies a script that needs a better beginning.

First gatekeep content quality (check if material is sufficient) and perform a viewer-perspective diagnosis (is it framed from the audience’s viewpoint), then concurrently generate 10–15 opening options, include Top 3 recommendations and a blockbuster-template explanation.

Do not trigger: generating a full script (use li-writer) or creating cover/title (use li-cover).
