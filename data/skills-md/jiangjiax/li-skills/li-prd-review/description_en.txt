PRD Expert Consulting Mode. Three well-known PMs/CEOs act as consultants, proactively review the PRD, identify weak points, and provide improvement suggestions that can be directly written into the document; the user decides whether to adopt them by accept/reject.  

Trigger phrases: /li-prd-review, “strengthen PRD”, “expert consultant”, “help me look at this PRD”, “let the expert review the PRD”, “what are the PRD’s loopholes”, “help me optimize the PRD”, “what other issues does the PRD have”.  

Even if the user doesn’t mention “PRD”, as long as they want an expert role to review a product document and give improvement suggestions, this skill should be triggered.  

DO NOT trigger for: PRD generation tasks (using li-prd), content script generation, topic analysis, and other content creation tasks.
