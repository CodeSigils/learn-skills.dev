Product PRD generation tool. Combining the product storytelling methodology from “Creation,” the product-position framework, and Lenny’s Newsletter’s PRD best practices, it helps you turn an idea into an actionable product requirements document.

Trigger phrases: /li-prd, “help me write PRD,” “generate requirements document,” “write product document,” “turn this idea into PRD,” “hackathon PRD,” “organize product requirements,” “I’m about to start, help me clarify the requirements.”

Even if the user doesn’t say “PRD,” as long as they want to organize a product idea into an actionable document, this skill should be triggered.

DO NOT trigger for: content script generation, topic analysis, data review, and other content creation tasks.
