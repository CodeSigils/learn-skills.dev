Guides PostgreSQL query design, indexing strategy, data types, and row-level security patterns — the query/schema layer, as distinct from the migration-execution safety covered by database-migrations and the hotel_id partition-pruning rules covered by postgres-hotel-partitioning. Use when choosing an index type, designing a composite or partial index, picking a column data type, writing an UPSERT or a queue-processing query, or setting up row-level security.
