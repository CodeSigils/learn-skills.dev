Reconstruct the research design behind a completed paper, article, thesis, or dissertation by tracing the decision chain among its research question, motivation, theory, hypotheses or propositions, data, and method choices. Use whenever a user wants to reverse-engineer a finished study, understand why its question or methods were chosen, compress it into a retrospective PAP-like blueprint, or train research-design judgment. Do not use this skill merely to summarize findings or results.
