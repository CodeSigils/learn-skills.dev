Use when drafting or reviewing Vietnamese content for a school, teacher, university, parent, or student audience, choosing thầy/cô-em versus thầy/cô-con versus quý phụ huynh, or applying MoET statutory grading terms (Thông tư 22/2021, 27/2020, 08/2021, 21/2019). Do NOT use this skill for e-learning app UI, gamification, or subtitles (use vietnamese-tech-writing) or for tutoring-centre and study-abroad advertising (use vietnamese-business-comms).
