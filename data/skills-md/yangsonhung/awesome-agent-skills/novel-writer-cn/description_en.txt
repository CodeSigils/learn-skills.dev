Novel-writing assistant that supports everything from scratch: drafting stories, continuing chapters, designing characters, building worlds, etc. Works for any genre—sci-fi, fantasy, mystery, romance, wuxia, and more. Triggered when: (1) user asks to write a story, e.g. “Help me write a sci-fi novel” or “Create a detective tale”; (2) continuing or revising existing content, e.g. “Add another chapter to this story” or “Revise this character”; (3) character tasks, e.g. “Design a villain for me” or “Give the protagonist a love interest”; (4) world-building, e.g. “Create a magic system” or “Build a cyberpunk world.”
