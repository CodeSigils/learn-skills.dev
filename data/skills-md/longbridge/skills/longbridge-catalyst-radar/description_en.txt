Self-selected stock tracking and scanning radar. Monitors users' chosen stocks and generates timed morning and evening reports. Based on the Longbridge open platform, it monitors the user's self-selected stock list, scans for seven catalyst signals—including earnings surprises, policy changes, abnormal capital flows, insider trades, analyst rating changes, etc.—and produces pre‑market/post‑market incremental briefings grouped by market. Supports four markets: US stocks, A‑shares, Hong Kong stocks, and Singapore stocks. When the user asks “what should I pay attention to today,” “show me the morning report,” “morning briefing,” “evening report,” “review,” “any news on my selected stocks,” “morning briefing,” “catalyst update,” or other investment‑related questions, this skill is triggered.
