Given a field, specialty, or role, identify the actual constraints that truly bound it, see clearly the solution space defined by this set of constraints—and how it explains the various practical operations of that role. Constraints are not limitations; they are the set of equations that collapse infinite possibilities into “this one.” Each constraint is assigned to one of three layers—hard, soft, or self‑imposed—and its true rigidity is determined through truth testing, identifying the constraints whose removal would disrupt its very identity. The core is to describe the current essential constraints (what they are, what solution space they delineate, what behavior they explain); how to loosen them is secondary and does not affect the diagnosis. Use when the user says “约束”, “找约束”, “这个领域的约束是什么”, “本质约束”, “什么框住了它”, “硬约束还是自设约束”, “边界在哪”, “这行为什么这么做”, “constraint”, “/ljg-constraint”, or provides any domain/profession/role and wants its defining constraints and solution space mapped.
