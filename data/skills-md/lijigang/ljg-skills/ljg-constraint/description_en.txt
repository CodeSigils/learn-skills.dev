Given a field, profession, or role, identify the actual constraints that truly bound it, and clearly see the solution space defined by this set of constraints—as well as how they explain the various practical actions of that role. Constraints are not limitations; they are the equations that collapse infinite possibilities into “this one.” Assign each constraint to one of three layers—hard, soft, or self‑imposed—and use truth‑testing to determine its true rigidity, pinpointing the constraints whose removal would cause the identity to disappear. The core is to describe the current essential constraints (what they are, what solution space they define, what behavior they explain); how they might be loosened is secondary and does not affect the diagnosis. Use this when a user mentions “约束,” “找约束,” “这个领域的约束是什么,” “本质约束,” “什么框住了它,” “硬约束还是自设约束,” “边界在哪,” “这行为什么这么做,” “constraint,” “/ljg-constraint,” or provides any domain/profession/role and wants its defining constraints and solution space mapped.
