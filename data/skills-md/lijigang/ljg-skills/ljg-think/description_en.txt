Arrow to the Root—a tool for vertical deep-drilling thought. Given a view, phenomenon, or problem, it shoots like an arrow straight down until it strikes the irreducible essence. Use when the user says "think it through," "trace the root," "what’s the essence," "why is it so," "dig deep," "drill to the bottom," "think deep," "drill down," or wants to trace any idea/phenomenon vertically to its irreducible root. Also trigger when the user provides a statement and wants depth analysis, not breadth survey.
