SupSub WeChat Official Account discovery — to be used only when the user wants to search for or discover a WeChat Official Account itself (usually to obtain its mpId and then subscribe). Matches phrases such as “search official account X”, “find the official account X”, “look up official account X”, “want to subscribe to official account X but don’t know the ID”, “discover / find a WeChat MP account”. ⚠️ Do not use it to search for article text/content or articles related to keywords — such intents (e.g., “search for content related to X”, “find articles related to X”) must go through supsub-search. It also includes canceling an ongoing official account search task.
