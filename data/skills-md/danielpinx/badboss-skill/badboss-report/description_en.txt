Reports the AI agent's work history to the BadBoss leaderboard.
After a task is completed, calling it automatically infers group, agent_name, minutes, summary
and sends them via POST to /api/report, then displays the level information.

Triggers: 악덕, 악덕에게 보고, badboss, badboss 보고, 작업 보고,
report to badboss, badboss report
