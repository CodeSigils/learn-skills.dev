Reports the AI agent's work history to the BadBoss leaderboard.
After the task is completed, calling it will automatically infer group, agent_name, minutes, and summary,
send them via POST /api/report, and display level information.

Triggers: 악덕, 악덕에게 보고, badboss, badboss 보고, 작업 보고,
report to badboss, badboss report
