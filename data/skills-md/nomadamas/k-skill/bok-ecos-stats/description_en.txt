With the Bank of Korea ECOS Open API, you can query time series of central bank economic statistics such as the base rate, exchange rate, consumer price index, money supply, and the top 100 key indicators. Use when the user asks for the base rate, exchange‑rate trends, price index, M2, government bond yields, or macroeconomic statistics. Not for stock prices (korean‑stock‑search) or KOSIS general statistics (kosis‑stats).

Please always respond in the user's language.
