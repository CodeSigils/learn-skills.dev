The Ministry of Environment’s integrated zero‑emission vehicle website is used to look up private announcements, applications, deliveries, remaining delivery numbers, and the announcement status of electric‑vehicle purchase subsidies by Korean local governments. It is used to check the remaining subsidy status for electric passenger cars, electric freight vehicles, and electric vans in the user’s region, whether applications are being accepted, deadlines, depletion, planned supplemental budgeting, and the subsidy conversion amounts for specific models.
