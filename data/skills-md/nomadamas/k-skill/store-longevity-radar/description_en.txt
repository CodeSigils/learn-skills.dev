Using the Small Business Market Promotion Corporation’s publicly released store (commercial district) information file (unverified), we compile a comprehensive list of nationwide stores that match industry and name keywords, and by matching past snapshots with name + coordinates we extract a list of long‑standing stores that “existed N years ago and are still operating today.” The approach is based on store names rather than business registration numbers, providing a lower bound for the earliest observation date instead of the opening date.
