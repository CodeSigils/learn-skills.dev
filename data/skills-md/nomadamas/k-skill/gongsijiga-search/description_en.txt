Search for the "Individual Official Land Price" (KRW/㎡) published annually by South Korea's Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport.  
It is the government‑published unit price for land by lot number and serves as the legal basis for calculating taxes such as property tax, comprehensive real estate holding tax, and transfer tax. **It is not a market price or actual transaction price.**  
Use this when the user asks for official land price, individual official land price, land official unit price, tax‑calculation land unit price, or “What is the official land price of this land?”  
Do NOT use it for market price, actual transaction price, sale price, asking price, or apartment price (those require a different data source).
