Financial data analysis capability (based on `bl mcp` + Alibaba Cloud Baolian MCP Marketplace `market-cmapi00073529`), covering Chinese A‑shares, funds, bonds, and other financial instruments. Supports stock screening, fund screening, fund‑manager screening, financial data queries (net profit / revenue / ROE, etc.), macro and industry time‑series data (GDP / CPI / production‑sales‑price), brokerage research‑report retrieval, and A‑share listed‑company announcement retrieval. When users mention any of the following keywords, it must be activated: stock picking / stock screening, fund screening, fund‑manager screening, financial data / net profit / revenue / valuation, macroeconomics / GDP / CPI, industry production, sales, price, brokerage research reports / industry research reports, listed‑company announcements. Not applicable to: general programming questions, non‑financial data, non‑Chinese‑market instruments.
