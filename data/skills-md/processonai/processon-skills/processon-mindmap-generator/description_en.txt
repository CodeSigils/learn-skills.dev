ProcessOn's officially developed AI mind‑map generation tool focuses on turning natural language, Markdown, long text, documents, web pages, image text, and other content into clear, hierarchical, editable professional mind maps with a single click. 

Whether it's article summarization, data organization, document breakdown, knowledge point aggregation, learning path mapping, or reading notes, literature reviews, meeting minutes extraction, work report summarization, outline generation, project task decomposition, brainstorming, and creative ideation, this skill can quickly generate professional mind maps, helping users transform scattered information into clear, structured knowledge.

The skill supports seven professional diagram layouts—mind maps, logic charts, organizational charts, fishbone diagrams, timelines, tree diagrams, and table charts—and is deeply integrated with the ProcessOn online collaboration platform. The generated mind maps can be edited, collaboratively revised, and efficiently reused online, making them suitable for workplace tasks, study and review, research reading, knowledge management, and planning scenarios.

Note: This skill is primarily for generating mind maps and knowledge‑structure diagrams and is not intended for flowcharts, swimlane diagrams, sequence diagrams, system architecture diagrams, ER diagrams, Mermaid diagrams, or other process or technical chart generation scenarios.
