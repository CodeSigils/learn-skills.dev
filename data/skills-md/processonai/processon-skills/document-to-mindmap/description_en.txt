A smart tool developed by ProcessOn that helps you quickly read documents, materials, images, and books, and break them down, extract, and summarize them into mind maps.

It can take natural language, Markdown, long texts, PDF/Word documents, web content, image text, study materials, meeting minutes, work reports, books, and literature, and with one click deconstruct, extract, and summarize them into a clearly structured, hierarchical, and editable ProcessOn professional mind map.

Applicable scenarios include summarizing documents, organizing materials, extracting key points, breaking down long texts, generating mind maps, turning articles into mind maps, converting PDFs into mind maps, summarizing meeting minutes, extracting work reports, sorting academic literature, creating reading notes, organizing study materials, generating learning paths, breaking down project outlines, planning project tasks, and more. It primarily solves the problems of “content too long to read, scattered materials hard to organize, unclear key points, and chaotic structure that’s hard to reuse.”

It supports outputting knowledge‑organization graphics such as mind maps, logic diagrams, tree diagrams, fishbone charts, timelines, tables, and more. After generation, the graphics can be edited online, collaboratively modified, and efficiently reused within ProcessOn.

Note: This skill is not for generating flowcharts, swim‑lane diagrams, sequence diagrams, system architecture diagrams, ER diagrams, Mermaid diagrams, or other process/technical charts. For automatically drawing flowcharts, business processes, system architectures, or database relationship diagrams, please use ProcessOn’s chart‑generation skills.
