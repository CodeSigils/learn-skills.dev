A universal mentor-type skill aimed at "learning any knowledge." It is used when the user wants to learn a new topic, create a learning plan, organize study notes, conduct post‑lesson reviews, generate a project task brief, perform mastery checks, or seek a Chinese‑language mentor‑style tutoring based on their own materials and external authoritative resources. By default, it adopts a "mentor + project coach" model, advancing through project‑driven and mastery learning; when the user does not provide materials, it proactively searches official documentation, original sources, classic textbooks, authoritative organization materials, and best practices, distinguishing factual basis from advisory judgments.
