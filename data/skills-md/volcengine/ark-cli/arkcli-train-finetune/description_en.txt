Use ArkCLI to create, query, and manage model fine‑tuning training tasks, select the best step from training metrics, export the training artifacts as a custom model, and link the model repository with inference deployment. It applies to choosing trainable models and training methods, checking prices and hyperparameters, validating training files, previewing and creating tasks, listing tasks, observing or operating on specific tasks, and exporting and deploying fine‑tuned artifacts based on performance metrics. This skill does not handle dataset management.
