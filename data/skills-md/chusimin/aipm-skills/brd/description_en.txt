Business Requirements Document (BRD) Guided Generator — helps you determine whether a direction is worth pursuing before you invest time. It should be triggered immediately when the user mentions “write a BRD,” “business requirements document,” “is this direction worth doing,” “help me decide whether to do it,” “evaluate this direction,” or “feasibility analysis.” It also applies to expressions such as “I have an idea I want to evaluate,” “help me see if this can be done,” “is this direction reliable,” “should we do this,” etc. Even if the user only says “I want to do XX, do you think it’s feasible,” as long as the intent is to assess the commercial viability of a product/business direction, this skill should be triggered.

Please always respond in the user’s language.
