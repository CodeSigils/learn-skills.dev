48‑hour domain acceleration learning engine — based on a “key‑question‑driven” methodology, it helps users quickly build a comprehensive cognitive map of any field and reach a level where they can converse with experts. It is triggered immediately when the user mentions “I want to quickly understand XX,” “Learn XX in 48 hours,” “Quickly get started with XX,” “Accelerated learning of XX,” “I need to master the XX domain in a short time,” “Help me fast‑track XX.” It also applies to expressions such as “I know nothing about XX but need a rapid overview,” “I have an interview about XX and I don’t understand it,” “We have a meeting to discuss XX and I must catch up beforehand,” “I want to build a panoramic understanding of the XX field,” “Help me draw a knowledge map of the XX domain,” and similar. Even if the user only says “Help me understand XX” or “What’s the fastest way to get started in the XX field,” as long as the intent is to establish a systematic understanding of a domain in a short period (rather than a slow, deep‑learning approach), this skill should be triggered. Distinction from the interactive‑learning skill: interactive learning suits the long‑term scenario of “I want to study XX systematically,” whereas this skill fits the accelerated scenario of “I need to grasp XX as quickly as possible.” The two can be combined — when this skill detects that the user needs systematic supplementation in a sub‑domain, it can suggest switching to the interactive‑learning skill.
