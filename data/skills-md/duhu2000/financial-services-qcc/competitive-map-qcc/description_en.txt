Competitive Landscape Analysis Skill – Qichacha MCP Enhanced Edition.  
Deep-dive into a target company’s competitive position within its industry, identifying rivals, partners, and ecological niche.  

Core functions:  
- Competitor identification: direct rivals in the same industry, region, and track  
- Competitive benchmarking: multi-dimensional comparison of scale, qualifications, bidding power, growth rate  
- Ecological-niche analysis: target’s place and bargaining power in the value chain  
- Potential entrant scan: firms likely to enter the track  
- Competitive-strategy recommendations: data-driven, actionable tactics  

Use cases: strategic investment decisions, competitive intelligence, market-entry planning, investment due-diligence rival analysis.
