Credit Due Diligence Report SKILL · Qichacha MCP V2.0 Enhanced Edition. A full-dimensional corporate due-diligence tool before credit approval and disbursement. After entering the target company’s full legal name, it automatically completes a five-in-one credit-risk profile covering business-registration verification, real financial chassis, judicial-risk scan, credit-repair traceability, and ultimate controller’s personal risk, then outputs a credit-decision draft ready for archiving.  
Core capabilities:  
- Real financial chassis: `get_financial_data` debuts in credit scenarios, returning complete 3-year financial statements (asset-liability ratio / quick ratio / owners’ equity / operating cash flow), ending the old reliance on event signals to infer solvency.  
- Credit-repair traceability: qcc-history’s 14 historical-risk tools distinguish “repair-type entities vs. consecutive defaulters”, decisive for rating.  
- Ultimate controller × legal representative personal backstop assessment: qcc-executive’s core tool quickly flags hidden risks of “clean company, risky controller”.  
- Credit rating × proposed credit limit × risk-mitigation clauses: outputs decision materials ready for the credit committee.  
Applicable scenarios: corporate loan approval / supply-chain finance credit / financial-leasing risk control / factoring admission / working-capital × project × M&A loan pre-screening.  
Usage: /credit-due-diligence company_name [--amount credit_amount] [--tenor tenor] [--type working|project|M&A] [--format md|docx|pptx]
