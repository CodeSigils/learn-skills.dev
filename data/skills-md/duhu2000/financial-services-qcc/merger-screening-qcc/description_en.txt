Merger Screening Skill - Qichacha MCP Enhanced Edition. Screening and compatibility analysis of target enterprises for M&A transactions, quickly identifying potential acquisition targets.

Core Functions:
- Target Screening: Filter potential targets based on industry, scale, region, and risk level
- Compatibility Analysis: Business synergy and cultural compatibility between acquirer and target
- Valuation Reference: Preliminary valuation range based on industrial and commercial data
- Integration Risk Assessment: Key risk points for post-merger integration
- Transaction Feasibility: Equity structure, debt burden, regulatory obstacles

Applicable Scenarios: Strategic investment M&A, industrial consolidation, listed company M&A, PE/VC M&A investment.

Usage: /merger-screening-qcc Acquirer Company [--industry Target Industry] [--scale Scale Range] [--region Region]
