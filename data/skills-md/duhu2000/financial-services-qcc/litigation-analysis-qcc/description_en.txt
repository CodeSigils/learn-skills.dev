Litigation Risk Assessment SKILL · Qichacha MCP V2.0 Enhanced Edition.  
Performs a three-layer litigation panorama scan on the target company—“current status + historical record + key personnel”—and delivers a judicial-risk assessment report ready for filing, credit-granting, or investment decisions.  

Core capabilities:  
- Three-layer panorama: current status (17 qcc-risk tools) + historical record (14 qcc-history tools) + key personnel (18 qcc-executive tools)  
- Time-series trends: real 5–10-year trend analysis based on historical judgments, case filings, and hearing announcements  
- Enforcement diagnosis: double-layer scan of enforcement, dishonesty, high-spending restrictions, and case closure × current × historical, distinguishing “rehabilitated” from “chronically dishonest” entities  
- Key-person litigation profiles: full litigation landscape of legal representative, actual controller, chairman, and general manager  
- Risk-exposure calculation: potential payout of pending cases, outstanding enforcement amounts, ratio to registered capital  

Use cases: pre-litigation counterparty check, credit due diligence, investment due diligence, supplier onboarding, customer credit evaluation, bankruptcy early warning.  

Usage: /litigation-analysis-qcc CompanyName [--period lastNyears] [--type contract dispute|labor dispute|IP|...] [--person-scan whether to scan key personnel]
