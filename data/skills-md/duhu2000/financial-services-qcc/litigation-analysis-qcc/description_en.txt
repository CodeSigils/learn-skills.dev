Litigation Risk Assessment SKILL · Qichacha MCP V2.0 Enhanced Edition.  
Performs a three-layer litigation panorama scan of the target company—“current status + historical record + key personnel”—and outputs a judicial risk assessment report ready for filing decisions, credit decisions, and investment decisions.  

Core capabilities:  
- Three-layer litigation panorama: current company status (17 qcc-risk tools) + company history (14 qcc-history tools) + key personnel (18 qcc-executive tools)  
- Time-series trends: real 5–10-year trend analysis based on historical judgments, case filings, and hearing announcements  
- Enforcement capability diagnosis: enforced / dishonest / high-consumption restricted / case-closure statuses × current × historical, identifying “rehabilitated entities” vs. “chronically dishonest entities”  
- Key personnel litigation profiles: full personal litigation landscape of legal representative, actual controller, chairman, and general manager  
- Risk exposure calculation: potential payouts from pending cases, amounts to be enforced, ratio to registered capital  

Applicable scenarios: pre-litigation counterparty check / credit due diligence / investment due diligence / supplier onboarding / customer credit evaluation / bankruptcy early warning.  

Usage: /litigation-analysis-qcc Company Name [--period lastNyears] [--type contract dispute|labor dispute|IP|...] [--person-scan whether to scan key personnel litigation profiles]
