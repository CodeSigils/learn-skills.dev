Industry Analysis Assistant Skill – Qichacha MCP Enhanced Edition. Leveraging multi-dimensional data such as tendering records, qualification distribution, and enterprise counts to support industry analysis report generation. Core functions:  
- Industry size estimation: based on tender amounts and registered capital distribution  
- Competitive landscape scan: number of firms and concentration of top players  
- Qualification threshold analysis: key industry license requirements and certified company distribution  
- Regional heat map: geographic distribution of firms and identification of industrial clusters  
- Trend assessment: industry prosperity based on tendering data  

Applicable scenarios: industry research at securities institutes, consulting firm sector analysis, investment firm track selection, corporate strategy department market analysis.  
Usage: /industry-analysis-qcc [industry name] [--region region] [--focus sub-sector]
