Industry Analysis Assistant Skill – Qichacha MCP Enhanced Edition. Leveraging multi-dimensional data such as bidding records, qualification distribution, and enterprise counts, it helps generate industry analysis reports.  
Core functions:  
- Industry size estimation: based on bidding amounts and registered capital distribution  
- Competitive landscape scan: number of firms and concentration of top players  
- Qualification threshold analysis: key industry license requirements and certified companies  
- Regional heat map: geographic distribution and cluster identification  
- Trend judgment: industry prosperity based on bidding data  

Use cases: industry research at brokerages, consulting firms’ sector analysis, investment institutions’ track selection, corporate strategy market analysis.  
Usage: /industry-analysis-qcc [industry name] [--region region] [--focus sub-sector]
