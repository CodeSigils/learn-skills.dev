Equity Structure Penetration Analysis SKILL · Qichacha MCP V2.0 Enhanced Edition. A control-rights verification tool before investment decisions. Multi-layer equity penetration + historical ownership change dual analysis, identifying actual controllers, concert parties, and hidden related relationships, helping investment teams quickly clarify the actual control structure of target companies during DD, and identify potential related-party transaction risks and control dispute risks. Core capabilities: - Multi-layer equity penetration: trace from direct shareholders up to natural-person UBOs, up to 10 layers deep - Equity structure chart generation: structured output of shareholding ratios and paths at each layer - **V2.0 new capability: historical ownership changes** (qcc-history) — identify shareholders’ entry/exit timing and valuation inflection points - Concert party identification: lock hidden concert parties through co-investment relationships, same actual controller, affiliated enterprises - Related-party transaction risk screening: network analysis of UBO × outbound investment × cross-shareholdings Applicable scenarios: PE/VC project DD, investment bank M&A due diligence, control change approval, shareholder derivative lawsuit pre-background check, concert party agreement review. Usage: /equity-structure company name [--depth shallow|full] [--historical whether to include historical changes] [--format md|docx|pptx]
