Equity Structure Drilling Analysis SKILL · Qichacha MCP V2.0 Enhanced Edition. A control-rights verification tool before investment decisions. Multi-layer equity drilling + historical ownership evolution dual analysis, identifying actual controllers, concert parties, and hidden affiliations, helping investment teams quickly clarify the actual control structure of target companies during DD and spot potential related-party transaction risks and control disputes. Core capabilities:  
- Multi-layer equity drilling: trace from direct shareholders up to natural-person UBOs, up to 10 layers deep  
- Equity structure chart generation: structured output of shareholding ratios and paths at each layer  
- **V2.0 new capability: historical ownership changes** (qcc-history) — pinpoint when shareholders enter/exit and valuation inflection points  
- Concert-party identification: lock hidden concert parties via co-investment relationships, same actual controller, affiliated enterprises  
- Related-party transaction risk screening: network analysis of UBO × outbound investments × cross-shareholdings  

Applicable scenarios: PE/VC project DD, investment-bank M&A due diligence, control-change approvals, shareholder derivative-suit background checks, concert-party agreement reviews.  

Usage: /equity-structure CompanyName [--depth shallow|full] [--historical whether to include historical changes] [--format md|docx|pptx]
