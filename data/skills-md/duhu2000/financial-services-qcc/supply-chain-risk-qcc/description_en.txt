Supply Chain Risk Assessment Skill – Qichacha MCP Enhanced Edition  
Comprehensive supply-chain risk evaluation for manufacturing and retail enterprises, covering supplier risk, customer concentration, and logistics risk.  

Core functions:  
- Supplier risk assessment: business registration, risk profile, and operational health of key upstream suppliers  
- Customer concentration analysis: dependency on and stability of major downstream customers  
- Bidding activity: supply-chain vitality gauged through tender data  
- Industry position assessment: enterprise bargaining power and substitutability within the supply chain  
- Risk transmission early warning: identification of supply-chain risks likely to propagate to the enterprise  

Applicable scenarios: manufacturing supply-chain risk control, retail supplier management, supply-chain finance, procurement decision support.
