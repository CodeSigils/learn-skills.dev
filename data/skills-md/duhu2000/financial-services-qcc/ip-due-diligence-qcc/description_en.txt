IP Due Diligence Skill – Qichacha MCP Enhanced Edition.  
Full-dimensional IP due diligence for tech companies and IPO candidates, covering patents, trademarks, and software copyrights.

- Patent panorama scan: counts and quality of invention, utility-model, and design patents plus validity status  
- Trademark portfolio analysis: core marks, defensive marks, international footprint

- IP risk flags: pledges, assignments, invalidation actions, infringement suits  
- Tech competitiveness benchmark: IP comparison with peer companies  

Use cases: tech-investment DD, IPO IP review, tech M&A, patent-licensing talks.
