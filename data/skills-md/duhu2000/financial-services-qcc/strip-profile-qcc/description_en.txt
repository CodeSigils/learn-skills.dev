Company Snapshot Quick View SKILL · Qichacha MCP V2.0 Enhanced Edition. A lightweight due-diligence tool for PE/VC/FA before LP roadshows, initial project screening, and internal investment-committee briefings. Generates a one-page company profile in 3 minutes, integrating five structured sections: business registration, core risk signals, IP assets, V2.0 entity continuity, and key management summary.

Core capabilities:
- Basic business verification + entity continuity (V2.0 new feature, qcc-history governance-stability backtracking)
- Core risk tags: one-page display of key risk signals such as dishonesty, high-spending restrictions, enforcement, equity freezes
- IP asset overview + IP pledging (V2.0 new tool)
- Key management summary: actual controller, legal representative, and core executives’ names and brief profiles
- Financing & operational activity: financing records, hiring activity, honors information

Use cases: 5-minute full-view of target company before LP roadshow / initial project screening / internal investment-committee briefing / investment analyst quick background check / investment manager daily watchlist review.

Usage: /strip-profile-qcc Company Name [--depth quick|standard] [--format md|docx|pptx]
