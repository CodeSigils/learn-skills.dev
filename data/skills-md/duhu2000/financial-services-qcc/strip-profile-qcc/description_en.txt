Enterprise Snapshot Quick View SKILL · Qichacha MCP V2.0 Enhanced Edition.  
A lightweight due-diligence tool for PE/VC/FA when pitching to LPs, pre-screening projects, or presenting internal proposals. Generates a one-page company snapshot in 3 minutes, integrating five structured sections: business registration, core risk signals, IP assets, V2.0 entity continuity, and key management summary.

Core capabilities:  
- Basic business verification + entity continuity (V2.0 new feature, qcc-history governance-stability backtracking)  
- Core risk tags: one-page display of key signals such as dishonesty, high-consumption restrictions, enforcement, equity freezes  
- IP asset overview + IP pledging (V2.0 new tool)  
- Key management summary: actual controller, legal representative, and core executives’ names and brief profiles  
- Financing & operational activity: financing records, hiring activity, honors & awards
