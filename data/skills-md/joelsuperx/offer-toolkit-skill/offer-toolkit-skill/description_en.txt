Job-hunting toolkit. Break “spot a dream job → get an offer” into three independent sub-skills: ① Job Description Skill – decode the JD and produce an Offer Strategy Report (whether to apply / match level / interview question forecast), ② Resume Skill – structure the resume and render it into 11 print-ready templates, ③ BQ Skill – uncover real experiences, convert them to STAR format, and build a reusable behavioral‑interview story library. Any job‑search‑related request (whether to apply, revise a resume, prepare for a behavioral interview, read a JD, “Tell me about a time…” etc.) comes in here and is routed to the appropriate sub‑skill. Keywords: job hunting, offer, career, job hunt, JD, job description, resume, CV, behavioral interview, BQ, STAR, tell me about a time, interview preparation.
