Job-search toolkit. Break the process “see an attractive position → get an offer” into three independent sub‑skills: 1) Job Description Skill – decode the JD and produce an Offer Strategy Report (whether to apply / match degree / interview question predictions); 2) Resume Skill – structure the resume and render it into 11 print‑quality templates; 3) BQ Skill – uncover real experiences, convert them into STAR format, and build a reusable behavioral interview story bank. Any job‑search‑related request (whether to apply, revise a resume, prepare for behavioral interviews, read a JD, “Tell me about a time…” etc.) enters here and is then routed to the corresponding sub‑skill. Keywords: job search, offer, career, job hunt, JD, job description, resume, CV, behavioral interview, BQ, STAR, tell me about a time, interview preparation.
