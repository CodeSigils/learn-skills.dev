Session retrospective that turns THIS session's problems into durable, correctly-routed improvements. Trigger on /reflect, "what did we learn", "retro", "retrospective", "improve our workflow", "how do we not repeat this", or after a painful debugging round. It captures the DELTA (what went wrong, what was slow, what got corrected) and routes each lesson to where it will fire again: an owned skill, a project CLAUDE.md, a cross-project GOTCHAS.md file, or memory. NOT a what-was-done summary (that is invoice-generator) and NOT a single bug's root-cause record (that is post-mortem).
