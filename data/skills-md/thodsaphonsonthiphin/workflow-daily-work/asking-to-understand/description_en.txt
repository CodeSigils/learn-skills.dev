Socratic questioning discipline - the user is asked, not told, until they can state the rule themselves. Use when the user says "ช่วยถามคำถามหน่อย", "ถามให้คิด", "ask me questions", "grill me", "quiz me", "make me think"; when they own an artifact they do not understand - "ยังไม่ค่อยเข้าใจสิ่งนี้ที่ให้ AI ทำ", "I don't understand what was built for me", a plan/map/pipeline/codebase handed to them; before a sign-off, handover, or onboarding onto a system they now own. Not for explaining on request (feynman-explain) and not for challenging a plan before building it (grill-then-plan).
