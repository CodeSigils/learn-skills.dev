Only used when the author explicitly requests the AI to plan based on the current story fragments, or explicitly invokes qw‑plan‑assist. Applicable when the author has already provided some information about the theme, visuals, constraints, and relational direction, and wants the AI to first produce a pool of 2 to 4 comparable candidate outlines, which will then be refined through subsequent discussion into a spec.md. The first stage delivers only the candidate outline pool and spec.md, without handling subsequent segmentation or text generation.
