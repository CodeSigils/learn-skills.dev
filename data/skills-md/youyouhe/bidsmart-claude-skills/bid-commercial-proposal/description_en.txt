Write the business portion of the bid/response documents. Dynamically read the list of attachments, business conditions, qualification requirements, and scoring criteria from the analysis report, and after gathering company information, draft business documents such as quotation letters, qualification certificates, performance materials, and declaration letters for each attachment. Output the files in Markdown format to the response files/ directory. Trigger this when the user requests to write business sections, business documents, bid attachments, or quotation files. It also supports a repair mode: trigger when the user requests to fix/supplement business documents or handle quality inspection feedback. Prerequisite: the bid-analysis must have already generated the analysis report.
