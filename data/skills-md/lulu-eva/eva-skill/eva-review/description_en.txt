Eva Review 2.2.1 Full‑platform post‑release review entry point. It is used to review a piece of content that has already been published—short video, Xiaohongshu article, WeChat public account, etc.—by reading backend screenshots, Excel, CSV, Markdown tables, or multiple historical records, deciding what to prioritize testing for the next piece, and after the user’s first authorization, continuously maintaining the account review record repository for the current project. Triggers: /eva-review, Eva Review, review this already‑published content, see where this post might get stuck after publishing, back‑fill the previous result, revisit recent content, summarize recent content patterns, what I say to perform better. Pre‑publish review, directly drafting new content and platforms without a publishing target’s metaphysical issues do not enter this skill.
