Eva Learn 2.2.2 Independent Learning Project Entry. Used when the user explicitly says /eva-learn, eva-learn, Eva Learn, or uses natural language to explicitly request “take me to understand / take me to systematic learning / take me to read / thematic reading / continue the previous learning.” All Learn tasks must first create or restore a traceable record, then begin instruction in the same session; ordinary explanations, creations, revisions, titles, commercial order drafts, or converting material to short videos/articles are not handled.  
Trigger methods: /eva-learn, eva-learn, Eva Learn, enter Eva Learn mode, take me to understand, take me to systematic learning, take me to read, do inquiry‑based learning, do thematic reading, continue Eva Learn learning project, continue the previous learning project, continue the last guided reading.
