Eva Preflight 2.2.1 pre‑release review entry. It is used when users explicitly request a “pre‑release check, final draft review, can this piece be published, a complete review before publishing, what hard flaws does this manuscript still have” to review natural language content drafts that the user or their team has essentially formed and not yet published. According to short‑video, article, general social‑media, or commercial‑order scenarios, it read‑only accesses existing titles, openings, logic, facts, expressive assets, and the true source of the brief, providing three levels of publishing judgment and one next‑step action. It is not used when only a topic, material, or outline is available; already‑published content should be submitted to eva‑review; action plans, code, product releases, and deployment acceptance are not applicable.
