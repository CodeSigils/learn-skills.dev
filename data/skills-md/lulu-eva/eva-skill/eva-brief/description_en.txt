Eva Brief is an independent entry point for deconstructing commercial briefs. It is only used when the user explicitly wants to break down a brand brief, break down brand cooperation requirements, generate a commercial brief constraint card, check an existing commercial brief draft against the brief, or when migrating the structure of benchmark samples for the same product. It only produces brief analysis, a selling‑point pool, a commercial brief constraint card, or a revision handoff, and does not directly write a complete commercial brief draft, title, opening, or body text; ordinary requests such as “how to write a commercial brief / how to present product selling points / help me create commercial brief content” should be routed back to the main Eva creation workflow.  
Trigger phrases: /eva-brief, eva-brief, commercial brief, brand brief, brand cooperation requirement, help me deconstruct brief, deconstruct commercial brief constraint card, compare brief check, can this commercial brief draft be submitted, check commercial brief draft, same‑product commercial brief sample, brand reference example.
