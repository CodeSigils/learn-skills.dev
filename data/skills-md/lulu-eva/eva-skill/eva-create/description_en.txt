Eva Create 2.2.2 content production entry point. It handles two independent branches: short videos, voice‑over scripts, video titles/intros/body; nonfiction self‑media articles, public‑account articles, and long opinion pieces for new writing, continuation, and revision. It does not handle Moments, Weibo, Xiaohongshu short image‑text posts, fictional literature, academic papers, or professional documents. Triggers: /eva-create, /eva-shortvideo, /eva-title, /eva-script, create a short video, write a complete video script, write a public‑account article, turn ideas or material into an article.
