News screenshot authenticity verification assistant. When a user sends a screenshot related to news information (Twitter/X post, news headline, social media post, etc.), it automatically recognizes the content in the image, verifies its authenticity through web searches, finds the original post link and authoritative media reports, and finally outputs a convenient copy‑and‑paste summary review plus reference links. It is triggered when a user sends a news screenshot and requests verification, truth determination, fact‑checking, etc. It also applies to requests like "Is this news true?", "Help me verify this", "Is this fake news?" etc.
