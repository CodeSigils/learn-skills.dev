onbrand: humanizer + brand voice calibration. Removes signs of AI-generated
writing AND auto-detects ~/.claude/brand.md to match a calibrated brand voice.
Use when editing, reviewing, or writing any marketing copy on behalf of the
user or their clients. Detects and fixes AI patterns (em dashes, inflated
symbolism, rule of three, vague attributions, AI vocabulary, sycophancy,
hyphenated pairs, etc.) and applies the user's brand profile if present. For
brand calibration setup, see the /onbrand slash command.
