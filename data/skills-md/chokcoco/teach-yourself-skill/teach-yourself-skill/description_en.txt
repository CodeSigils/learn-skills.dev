Targeting different ages, backgrounds, professions, and learning goals, it plans, generates, quality‑checks, and iterates generic courses or series of tutorials. Starting from task definition and reliable sources, it dynamically selects writing styles and instructor tones for various content types—such as environment setup, language fundamentals, framework application, principle diagnostics, integrated practice, and introductory summaries—designs chapter structures of any length, and produces Markdown, HTML, exercises, projects, and reference materials. Quality is ensured through deterministic scripts, course evaluation rubrics, humanizer‑zh, regression testing, and manual review, creating a closed‑loop quality process; it can also be used to refactor, expand, review, or publish existing courses.
