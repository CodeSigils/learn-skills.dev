A powerful academic paper search and literature management tool designed specifically for OpenClaw.  
Supports searching, citation generation, and literature organization from academic databases such as arXiv, Google Scholar, and PubMed.

Use cases:  
- (1) Rapid literature review and tracking of cutting-edge research  
- (2) Reference management during academic writing  
- (3) Batch processing of paper metadata and citation information  
- (4) Research trend analysis and hotspot discovery  

Use this skill when you need to search for academic literature, manage references, or track research progress.

Skill features:  
- 🔍 Multi-database support (arXiv, Crossref, etc.)  
- 📚 Automatic BibTeX citation generation  
- 📊 Multiple output formats (Text/JSON/Markdown)  
- 🎯 Smart search and filtering  
- 🔄 Batch processing and automation
