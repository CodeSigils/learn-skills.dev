Legal Visualization. A skill for generating legal diagrams and charts tailored to legal business scenarios; used when the user requests to organize case materials, contract materials, compliance items, transaction arrangements, evidence chains, litigation processes, timelines, legal relationships, client reports, legal service plans, or lawyer team work into relationship diagrams, flowcharts, timelines, evidence chains, risk maps, roadmaps, PNG/SVG/PDF/.drawio files; it also accommodates terms such as “legal visualization,” “case fact diagram,” “legal relationship diagram,” etc. First filter scenarios by audience, task verbs, and routing rules, then generate deliverable images and retain the draw.io source file as an editable draft. This skill is not for factual verification and does not replace legal conclusion judgments.
