Organize case or consulting materials into a standardized directory structure. Support two presets: litigation cases (12 folders) and potential projects/consultations (3 folders). This capability should be used when the user needs to create a new case, initialize the case directory structure, organize consulting materials, or quickly create a case by specifying the case number, client, case type, and other information through parameters and natural language. Do not use for: separately generating legal documents, conducting legal research, evidence analysis, or other non‑case‑initialization tasks.
