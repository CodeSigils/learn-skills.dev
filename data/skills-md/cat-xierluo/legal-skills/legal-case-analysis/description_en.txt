This capability should be used when the user needs to conduct legal analysis, case assessment, risk evaluation, litigation strategy, non‑litigation risk judgment, client explanation, or a legal analysis report based on case materials, consulting materials, contract documents, evidence materials, or search results. It serves as a pre‑analysis engine for legal tasks and does not require generating a formal report each time. Do not use it for: pure OCR, simple statutory or case law retrieval, basic Word formatting conversion, or document generation that only requires packaging a service plan without adding new legal analysis.
