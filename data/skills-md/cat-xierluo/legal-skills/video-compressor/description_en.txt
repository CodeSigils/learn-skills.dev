Video compression and silent segment cutting tool. Uses FFmpeg CRF mode to compress videos, suitable for screen recording/courseware scenarios; supports detection and removal of silent and static segments. Automatically detects hardware and selects the optimal encoding scheme (Apple Silicon defaults to VideoToolbox hardware acceleration, speed increase of 5–15×). This capability should be used when the user needs to compress video, reduce video size, or remove video gaps. Do not use for: video editing, audio extraction, format conversion.
