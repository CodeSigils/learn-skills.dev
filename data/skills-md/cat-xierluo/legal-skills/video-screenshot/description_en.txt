Video screenshot extraction tool. Automatically extracts key frames from screen recording videos (such as WeChat chat recordings, conference recordings, etc.), deduplicates them, and saves them as image files, which can be used as legal evidence. Supports four strategies: scene change detection, key frame extraction, fixed interval, and intelligent deduplication, combined with content-area dHash, pixel differences, SSIM, scrolling frame merging, and OCR text deduplication. Trigger keywords: video screenshot, screen recording screenshot, chat record screenshot, frame extraction deduplication, video frame capture, video key frame extraction. Not for: video compression, video editing, audio extraction.
