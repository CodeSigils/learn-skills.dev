A PaddleOCR structured parsing skill for legal PDFs and scanned documents. By default, it converts local PDFs or images to Markdown and retains traceable archive records within the skill. Suitable for case files, medical records, evidence materials, invoices, financial reports, complex scanned documents, table‑dense documents, formulas, and multi‑column layouts. Trigger words include: legal PDF OCR, case file OCR, medical record OCR, evidence scan to Markdown, PaddleOCR, table recognition, formula recognition, layout analysis, PDF to Markdown, complex PDF parsing.
