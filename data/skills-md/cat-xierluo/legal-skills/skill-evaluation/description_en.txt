Scenario-based evaluation tool for legal Skills (based on the DR-5 frontier: scenario rubric + multiple grader mix + expert calibration). This tool should be used when a user needs to assess whether a legal Skill (contract, litigation, compliance, legal research, document drafting, due diligence, etc.) is usable, whether its output quality meets standards, and whether it aligns with the real judgment criteria of a specific legal scenario; trigger phrases include “evaluate skill,” “verify skill usability,” “skill quality,” “eval skill,” “skill assessment,” “is this legal skill usable,” “how to evaluate contract skill,” “how to evaluate litigation skill.” It does not construct a universal six-dimension benchmark; consistency (directory, frontmatter, security, release) is handled by skill-lint, and this tool does not duplicate that—rather, it integrates its conclusions and performs content quality evaluation on that basis. Do not use it for: creating new Skills (use skill-creator / legal-knowledge-engine), pure format linting (use skill-lint), generic code review.
