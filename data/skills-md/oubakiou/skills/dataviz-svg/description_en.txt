A skill for generating SVG/PNG data‑visualization charts with Vega‑Lite and embedding them in Markdown. It is used in situations that require statistical charts that Mermaid cannot express (scatter plots, heatmaps, histograms, box‑and‑whisker plots, area charts, composite charts, etc.) or advanced, data‑driven graphs. Activate this skill when a user says “make a graph,” “generate a chart,” “visualize the data,” or “with Vega‑Lite,” or when, during document creation, a level of expressiveness beyond Mermaid’s pie charts or Gantt charts is needed. Simple flowcharts and sequence diagrams are adequately handled by Mermaid, so they are excluded.
