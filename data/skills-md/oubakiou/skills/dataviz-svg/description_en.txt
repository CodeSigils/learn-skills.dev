Skill for generating SVG/PNG data visualization charts using Vega-Lite and embedding them in Markdown. It is used in situations that require statistical charts (scatter plots, heat maps, histograms, box plots, area charts, composite charts, etc.) or data‑driven advanced graphs that cannot be expressed with Mermaid. When the user says “make a graph,” “generate a chart,” “visualize data,” or “with Vega‑Lite,” or when documentation creation demands more expressive power than Mermaid’s pie charts or Gantt charts, this skill should be invoked. Simple flowcharts and sequence diagrams are sufficiently handled by Mermaid, so they are excluded.
