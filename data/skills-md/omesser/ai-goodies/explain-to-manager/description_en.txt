Interview an engineer about the gap between what their non-technical manager expects and the actual state of the work, then produce a business-friendly status update that preserves trust. Use when the user needs to deliver bad news upward — work isn't ready, a deadline will slip, AI or a technology under-delivered — or asks anything like "how do I explain this to my boss / manager / management". Also use for justifying a cost, tooling spend, or risk-closure proposal to management, and for defending existing spend or invisible engineering work under scrutiny.
