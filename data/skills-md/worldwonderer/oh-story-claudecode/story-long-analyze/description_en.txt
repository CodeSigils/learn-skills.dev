Long-form web-novel teardown. Deep dissection of the golden first three chapters, character framework, thrill-point design, and pacing control of hit full-length novels.  
Two modes supported:  
- Quick teardown: analyzes the golden three chapters and overall structure (default)  
- Deep teardown: dissects the entire novel chapter by chapter, exporting structured files to a specified directory  
Trigger commands: /story-long-analyze, /长篇拆文, “Help me tear this book apart,” “Analyze the golden three chapters”  
Deep mode triggers: “deep teardown,” “full teardown,” “systematic teardown,” or providing the path to the novel text file
