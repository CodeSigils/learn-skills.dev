Based on file system initialization, continuation, recovery, and delivery of short drama or comic drama projects, and providing a local dashboard for creators; it also handles organizing project‑level Look Development / style‑frame direction and authorized production observation calibration. It serves screenwriters, comic drama studios, and directors. Users may request “create/continue short drama project,” “view progress/next steps,” “perform Look Development,” “calibrate this project with production observation,” “open dashboard/short drama creation station,” “recover an interrupted or incomplete release,” “export production materials,” or, when tasks span multiple stages and the intent is unclear, first determine the current status and responsible skill; explicit writing, assets, prompts, storyboards, or review requests are handled directly by the corresponding sub‑skill.
