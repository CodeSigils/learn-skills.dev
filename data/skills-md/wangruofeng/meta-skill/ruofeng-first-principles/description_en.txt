First‑principles interpretation and analysis. Strip any complex tech stack, new concept/field, vague problem, or piece of content down to its indivisible “fundamental question,” establishing a transferable mental model. Use this when the user truly wants to “understand” the essence of something rather than just its surface usage—e.g., to grasp what fundamental problems a project’s libraries/frameworks each solve, to quickly build a cognitive framework when entering a new domain, to find the essence when facing a complex problem, or to extract the underlying logic of an article or book. Whenever the user mentions “first principles,” “essence,” “why,” “fundamental,” “underlying logic,” “decomposition,” “understanding from zero,” “mental model,” or expresses “can’t make sense of a bunch of terms and want to clarify,” this skill is triggered.
