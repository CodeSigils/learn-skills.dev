PDF data extraction tool. Triggered when users mention "PDF extraction", "PDF to Markdown", "PDF parsing", "extract PDF content", "PDF to JSON", or "RAG PDF". OpenDataLoader PDF is currently the top-ranked PDF parser in benchmarks, supporting local mode (fast, deterministic) and hybrid AI mode (complex tables, scans, formulas), and outputs Markdown, JSON (with bounding boxes), and HTML. Ideal for extracting structured data from PDFs for RAG/LLM pipelines or for batch-processing PDF documents.
