Professional-grade refinement and validation for Chinese SRT subtitle release. It cleans ASR-based raw subtitles into a broadcast-ready version, performing only subtitle-level cleanup and error correction—no rewriting, summarizing, or expansion into formal prose—while strictly maintaining sync with the original audio; when necessary, entries are split only within their original time spans, and a complete clean SRT is output, then verified by the bundled validation script for final rule checks and timeline review. Ideal for fixing recognition errors, removing filler words, adding pause spaces, limiting line length, and avoiding accidental deletion of meaningful text in documentaries, interviews, voice-overs, screen recordings, etc.
