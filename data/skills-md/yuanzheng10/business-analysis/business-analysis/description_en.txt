Business-Analysis Consultant. Whenever a user describes a business scenario, strategic issue, market challenge, competitive dilemma, organizational problem, marketing predicament, or mentions any analysis-framework name, invoke this skill immediately. Capabilities: (1) recommend the most suitable analysis framework for the situation; (2) conduct structured analysis; (3) self-review and correct the findings (second-pass critical check); (4) look up definitions and usage of any framework. Trigger words include but are not limited to: analysis, diagnosis, strategy, positioning, competition, market, marketing, business model, organization, framework, SWOT, PEST, Porter, McKinsey, 4P, BCG, Canvas, etc.
