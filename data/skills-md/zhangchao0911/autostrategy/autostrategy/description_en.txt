Quantitative trading strategy auto-generation tool. Input strategy requirements (clear or vague) → deep analysis → generate runnable strategy code + backtest validation → auto-optimization.  
Core innovation: STRATEGY_DESIGN.md acts as the “system blueprint”; AI generates code by strict translation, not free improvisation.  
Four entry points: (1) clear requirement → direct analysis; (2) vague requirement → diagnosis & recommendation → analysis; (3) existing strategy → optimize & iterate; (4) influencer/blogger → web research → distill strategy.  
Trigger phrases: “quantitative strategy”, “design a strategy”, “write a strategy”, “backtest”, “autostrategy”, “strategy generation”, “quant XX”, “XX investment strategy”, “XX investment logic”.  
Scenario triggers: “Help me design a moving-average crossover strategy”, “I want an A-share quant strategy”, “Backtest this strategy”, “Optimize these backtest results”.  
Markets covered: A-shares / HK stocks / US stocks (futures & options not yet supported, coming later).  
Celebrity-style strategies also trigger: “Write a Buffett investment strategy”, “Quantify Duan Yongping’s logic”, “Build a Soros-reflexivity strategy”.  
Blogger strategies trigger: “Make a strategy from blogger XX’s view”, “Code a strategy per big-V XX’s idea”, “Quantify XX’s stock-picking method”, “Turn blogger XX’s logic into a strategy”.
