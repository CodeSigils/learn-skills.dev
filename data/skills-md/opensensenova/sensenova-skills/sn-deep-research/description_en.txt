Deep research orchestrator, covering the entire process from planning → multi‑dimensional evidence collection → synthesis → report generation (report.md). The output is saved to report_dir and supports resume from breakpoints. Before starting, a minimal generic web_search must be performed to verify that the current session’s search capability is available; if the check fails or cannot be confirmed, stop and prompt the user to configure it. **If any of the following situations occurs, actively use this skill instead of answering with a few self‑searched items**: ① The user uses trigger words such as deep study / deep research / in‑depth study / comprehensive study / systematic research / survey / investigation / due diligence / industry research / market research / competitive analysis / policy research / technology research / trend research / fact‑checking / write a research report / survey report / deep report / research / deep research; ② The request requires evidence from multiple sources, multi‑dimensional comparison, and cross‑validation to provide a reliable conclusion; ③ The user asks for a report, white paper, industry analysis, or due‑diligence document; ④ The topic involves the latest policies/market/products/prices/regulations and needs systematic verification. Vague or broad “research/learn about X” requests should also trigger it. It is not used for: single‑point factual Q&A (“What is X?”), one‑sentence summaries, organization of already‑provided single‑source material, or pure textual polishing/rewrite.
