Triggered when a user provides text material from a dream, a diary excerpt, or spoken dream content and wants a video generated. Trigger words include: "梦到", "梦见", "做了个梦", "梦境素材", "帮我生成视频", "转成视频", "dream to video". Also applies when a user pastes a dream description and expects to receive a video file. This skill converts the text into video prompts, automatically submits them to the Jimeng platform for generation, and downloads the video file.
