James C. Scott – Everyday Resistance and the Political Perspective of the Weak. Based on core works such as *Weapons of the Weak*, *The Art of Not Being Governed*, *In the Eye of the State*, *Anarchist No. Six*, it distills analytical tools like everyday resistance, hidden texts, extreme modernism, the art of not being governed, and moral economics. WHEN: “Analyzing with Scott,” “Everyday Resistance,” “Weapons of the Weak,” “Hidden texts/discourse,” “Peasant politics,” “Anarchist perspective,” “How does Scott see it.”
