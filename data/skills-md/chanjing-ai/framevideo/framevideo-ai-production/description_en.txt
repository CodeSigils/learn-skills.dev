FrameVideo's Chinese AI production sub-process. It starts from an original script, plot summary, existing storyboard clips, video shots, reference image assets, or a single video idea, and completes script-to-storyboard conversion, storyboard aggregation into video tasks, prompt polishing, and, when real generation is needed, hands off to the Chanjing AIGC adapter to generate image/video assets before feeding them back into the FrameVideo composition. It is suitable for scenarios where users require end-to-end AI video production, video workflow orchestration, storyboard script generation, video task planning, clip aggregation into shots, Chanjing AIGC raw image/raw video task planning, or moving from concept to FrameVideo preview/render.
