Asset preprocessing for FrameVideo compositions — text-to-speech narration (Kokoro), audio/video transcription (Whisper), and background removal for transparent overlays (u2net). Use when generating voiceover from text, transcribing speech for captions, removing the background from a video or image to use as a transparent overlay, choosing a TTS voice or whisper model, or chaining these (TTS → transcribe → captions). Each command downloads its own model on first run.
