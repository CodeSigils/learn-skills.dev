The writing style constraints and proofreading for Chinese graduate theses in science and engineering. Dual mode: 1) Writing mode – follow the standards when drafting or expanding the main text; 2) Proofreading mode – diagnose and provide rewrite suggestions for already written paragraphs or sections. The core goal is to make the thesis read like a graduation thesis written by a real person, without AI traces and without slipping into overly colloquial language. Triggered by user requests to write, polish, or proofread Chinese graduate thesis content, chapters, abstracts, introductions, conclusions, method descriptions, experimental descriptions, figure/table captions, and similar contexts.
