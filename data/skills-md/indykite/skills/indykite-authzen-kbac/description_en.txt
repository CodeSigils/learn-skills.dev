Author an IndyKite KBAC (Knowledge-Based Access Control) authorization policy - subject, actions, resource, and a Cypher condition over the IKG - then evaluate it via the AuthZEN REST API (`POST /access/v1/evaluation`). Use when deciding whether a subject may perform an action on a resource, running batch decisions, or searching the actions a subject is allowed on a resource. Not for reading, returning, or modifying graph data - KBAC only renders a yes/no decision.
