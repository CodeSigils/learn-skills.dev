Chinese Content Quality Acceptance Tool, the main entry (gateway) for the writing series. It conducts a comprehensive check (16 items) of a Chinese manuscript from four perspectives—editor, reader, author, and platform—producing a score, an issue list, and routing recommendations. No rewriting, only acceptance and routing.

Triggers: “Help me see how this is written,” “Does this have any problems,” “Check this article,” “Is this manuscript qualified,” “Help me review it once,” “Article quality acceptance,” “Chinese writing diagnosis,” “Can this be published,” “What needs to be changed in the manuscript,” “How to change it.”

EN: “review my Chinese article,” “check this article quality,” “Chinese writing diagnosis,” “proofread this piece,” “is this article good enough.”

Not for / Not applicable: direct rewriting (→ humanize‑ai / editor‑revisor), only change title/layout/punctuation (→ packaging‑workshop), can’t find one’s own voice (→ voice‑dissolver).
