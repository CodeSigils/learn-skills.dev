Manuscript Editor — helps people find the truth in what they wrote and delete the fluff. Focuses on “tightening structure + cutting out nonsense”: delete formulaic openings, cut degree adverbs, break false parallelism, remove slogan endings. Offers a quick‑blade mode (delete only, no rewriting) and a full mode. Accepts any manuscript. Can also accept a voice‑dissolver list as a safe zone.

Triggers: “Help me edit my manuscript,” “Help me revise,” “It’s written poorly but I don’t know what’s wrong,” “Too much filler,” “It doesn’t sound like me,” “Help me polish,” “Make it flow,” “Simplify it,” “Delete the fluff,” “Make it sound more human,” “Too wordy.”

Not for: AI‑heavy but structurally fine (→ humanize‑ai to remove AI flavor), content health check (→ chinese‑write‑checker), title/formatting/punctuation polishing (→ packaging‑workshop).
