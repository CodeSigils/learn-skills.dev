Manuscript Editor — helps locate the truth in what people write and cut out the fluff. Focuses on “tightening structure + removing nonsense”: delete cliché openings, slash degree adverbs, break false parallelism, eliminate slogany endings. Offers a quick‑blade mode (delete only, no rewriting) and a full mode. Accepts any manuscript. Can accept a voice‑dissolver audio list as a safe zone.

Triggers: “help me edit,” “help me revise,” “the writing is bad but I don’t know what’s wrong,” “too much filler,” “doesn’t sound like me,” “help me polish,” “make it flow,” “streamline it,” “remove fluff,” “make it sound more human,” “too verbose.”

Not for: AI‑flavored text with fine structure (→ humanize-ai for de‑flavoring), content health check (→ chinese-write-checker), title/formatting/punctuation polishing (→ packaging‑workshop).
