【Punctuation Polishing】The most common flaw of AI when writing Chinese is punctuation: half‑width commas, missing Chinese‑English spaces, straight quotation marks, overuse of dashes… This skill solves it all at once. It covers four scenarios—Chinese prose, technical documentation, Chinese‑English mixed layout, and social media—while leaving code and URLs completely untouched, and it also has English punctuation proofreading capability.  
Triggers: check punctuation, correct punctuation, punctuation issues, punctuation standards, half‑width to full‑width, Chinese‑English spacing, punctuation symbol check, punctuation, fix punctuation.  
Not for: pure English text proofreading (→ English editing tools), pure code files, user explicitly says “don’t change punctuation.”
