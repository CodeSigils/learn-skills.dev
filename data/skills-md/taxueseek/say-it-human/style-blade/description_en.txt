[L1.5 Stylized Writing] After removing the AI flavor, you can choose a writing style that conveys attitude. Three modes: angular (attitudinal, not smooth), sharp (short, forceful, precise, with the key sentence placed at the end), density compression (every character must bear weight). It’s not about eliminating AI flavor; it’s about actively injecting personality.  
Trigger: write more incisively, produce a passage with attitude—angular, unsmooth, aggressive, taking a stance, mainly short sentences, compress it, cut the fluff, trim each sentence until it can’t be trimmed any further.  
Not for: removing AI flavor (→ L1 humanize‑ai), cutting fluff structure (→ L2 editor‑revisor), title layout (→ L3 packaging‑workshop).
