Feishu Calendar: provides full management of calendars and schedules (meetings). Core scenarios include: viewing/searching schedules, creating/updating schedules, managing attendees, checking availability, and recommending free time slots. For frequent actions, please use Shortcuts first: +agenda (quick overview of today/upcoming events), +create (create a schedule and invite attendees as needed), +freebusy (check the availability of the user's primary calendar and RSVP status), +suggestion (for appointment requests without a set time, provide multiple recommended time options).
