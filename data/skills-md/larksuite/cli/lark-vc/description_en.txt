Feishu Video Conferencing: Query meeting records, obtain meeting summary products (summary, action items, chapters, verbatim transcript).  
1. Use this skill when querying the number or details of meetings that have ended (e.g., yesterday | last week | meetings already held today); use the lark-calendar skill for querying upcoming meeting schedules.  
2. Supports searching meeting records through filters such as keywords, time range, organizer, participants, meeting room, etc.  
3. Use this skill when obtaining or organizing meeting summaries.
