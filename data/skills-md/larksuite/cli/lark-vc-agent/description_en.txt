Feishu video conference: Allows a bot to join or leave an ongoing meeting on behalf of the current user and read real-time events during the meeting (participants joining and leaving, speaking, chatting, screen sharing, etc.). 1. The user provides a 9‑digit meeting number and uses +meeting‑join / +meeting‑leave to request the bot to join or leave—actual join/leave records will be generated. 2. While the meeting is in progress, if the user wants to know “who joined,” “who left,” “who is speaking,” “is anyone sharing a screen,” and other dynamics, the bot joins the meeting and uses +meeting‑events to read the event timeline. 3. Typical scenarios: participant bot, in‑meeting assistant, attending as a listener, attending on behalf of someone. Prerequisite: the bot can only read events of meetings it has joined and that are still ongoing; to query participant lists, minutes, or transcripts of finished meetings, use the lark‑vc skill.
