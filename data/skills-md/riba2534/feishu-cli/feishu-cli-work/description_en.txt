Only for calendar, tasks, approvals, attendance, and OKR; it is not a universal entry point for all Feishu office requests. Users must use this Skill when requesting free‑busy information, finding common free time, booking meeting rooms, accepting/rejecting invitations, creating or responding to events, managing tasks/checklists, initiating, withdrawing, approving, rejecting, delegating, or copying approvals, querying clock‑in/late/leave statistics, or querying OKR cycles or updating progress. It is explicitly prohibited to use it for user/department address books, email, historical meeting retrieval, meeting recordings, or meeting notes; these respectively use feishu‑cli‑platform, feishu‑cli‑mail, and feishu‑cli‑meetings. Meeting notification messages use feishu‑cli‑messaging.
