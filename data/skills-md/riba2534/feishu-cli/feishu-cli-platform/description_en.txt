Here, “global search” only refers to `search docs/messages/apps`, not including queries within approval, meeting, email, or other business domains. If the request contains approval, approval_code, approval definition/instance/task, never use this Skill; use feishu-cli-work instead. If it involves video meetings, minutes, recordings, or transcripts, use feishu-cli-meetings.
