We produce a 1280×720 single HTML web keynote and report‑style slides in the Shin Seong‑jin navy‑coral style. Using lecture content retrieved by cdsa‑lecture‑wiki, it is employed when creating presentation materials that require relational diagrams and decision‑making messages, such as director/executive keynotes, kickoff/mid‑term/final reports, management reports, proposals, and performance reports. For standard educational faithful‑original HTML we use “lecture‑deck,” and for actual PPTX we use “ssjppt1.” The message, evidence, and relationships are organized into hub‑spoke, matrix, timeline, problem‑solution‑output, and alternative‑comparison diagrams, and the information density and canvas fill are validated.
