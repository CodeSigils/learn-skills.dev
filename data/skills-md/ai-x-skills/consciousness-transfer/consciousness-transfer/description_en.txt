Four-phase cognitive internalization methodology (感悟用破 Feel-Discover-Apply-Stress-test). Transforms structured knowledge entries into actionable cognitive skills through guided dialogue. 四阶段认知内化方法论：通过引导式对话将结构化知识转化为可执行的认知技能。 Use when the user wants to deeply learn a mental model, decision framework, or thinking pattern — not just read about it. 当用户想要深入内化一个思维模型、决策框架或认知模式时触发。
