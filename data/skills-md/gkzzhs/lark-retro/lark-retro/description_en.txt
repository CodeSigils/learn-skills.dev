Sprint/periodic review workflow: automatically collect data from calendars (including meeting minutes/recorded meeting logs), OKRs, approvals, tasks, messages, documents, and whiteboards; AI generates structured reports and tracks the closure of improvement items. Supports OKR alignment and progress record analysis, reinforcement of approval-blocking signals, remediation of historical document permission requests, enhanced private cloud document search, @mention message filtering, automatic closure of action items, custom grouping of task lists, historical report comparison, Bitable archiving, record-sharing links, report space initialization, report shortcut archiving, and searching/reserving/updating the next review meeting room. Used when users need to conduct reviews, retrospectives, or weekly report summaries.
