Use the A‑C‑R‑E framework to proofread experiences and adapt content density to sparse, balanced, or dense levels. Fabricating experiences or numbers is prohibited; by default generate a single, most suitable single‑column résumé, and only produce bulk versions when the user explicitly asks for comparisons. Do not use for academic CVs, senior executive résumés, portfolio websites, proxy applications, or pure mock interviews.
