Interview/work meeting audio transcription and review assistant that handles interview or meeting recordings and transcribed text, supports saving to Feishu documents and automatically archiving to the corresponding multidimensional tables (Interview Record Table / Meeting Record Table, kept separate). Trigger scenarios: the user mentions “interview review,” “meeting minutes,” “organize this meeting,” uploads an audio file (.wav/.m4a/.mp3/.mp4) needing transcription, or uploads a PDF transcript file that needs to be organized.
