Provide a pre-submission self‑check tool for professional master’s theses in the School of Software and the School of Intelligent Software and Engineering at Nanjing University. The student supplies the thesis PDF; the system first extracts the actual thesis title from the abstract page or cover of the PDF and asks the student to confirm it, then automatically determines whether the thesis is “engineering‑type” or “academic‑type” and asks the student to confirm that as well. According to the corresponding checklist (48 items for engineering‑type / 51 items for academic‑type, including 5 red‑line items), the system conducts an item‑by‑item review, provides feedback with original page‑number references, highlights red‑line issues, estimates a review conclusion, and finally writes the results to <thesis‑directory>/<thesis‑title>-review.md (the title is taken from the PDF content, not the file name). This feature is only activated when the user explicitly uses trigger phrases such as “thesis self‑check”, “self‑inspection”, “pre‑review self‑check”, “what problems does my thesis have”, etc., and provides a PDF path.
