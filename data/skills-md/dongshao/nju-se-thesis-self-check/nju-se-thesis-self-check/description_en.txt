Provide a self‑check skill for professional master’s theses from the School of Software and the School of Intelligent Software and Engineering at Nanjing University before submission for review. The student supplies the thesis PDF; first extract the actual thesis title from the PDF’s abstract page or cover and ask the student to confirm it, then automatically determine whether it is “engineering‑type” or “academic‑type” and ask the student to confirm. According to the corresponding checklist (48 items for engineering‑type / 51 items for academic‑type, including 5 red‑line items), examine each item, give feedback with original page‑number references, highlight red‑line issues, estimate the evaluation conclusion, and finally write to <same directory as thesis>/<thesis title>-review.md (the title comes from the PDF content, not the file name). This functionality is enabled only when the user explicitly uses trigger phrases such as “thesis self‑check”, “self‑inspection”, “pre‑submission self‑check”, “what problems does my thesis have”, etc., and provides a PDF path.
