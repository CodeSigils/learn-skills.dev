A structured personal decision database system that helps users systematically handle complex choices in life and career. Use this skill when the user encounters the following situations: (1) expressions like "What should I do," "Help me make a decision," or "I'm torn between X and Y"; (2) decisions involving career (job hopping/ career change), lifestyle (moving), major purchases (buying a house/a car), or interpersonal relationships; (3) a need to weigh pros and cons systematically; (4) a desire to record the decision-making process for future review and reflection.
