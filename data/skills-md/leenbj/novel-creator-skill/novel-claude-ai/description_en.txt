Chinese long-form novel full-process creation skill (v1.0.0). When a user wants to write a novel, create a story, continue a chapter, devise a plot, build a world view, design character relationships, extract writing style, or imitate other novels, this skill must be used. It covers the complete chain from vague ideas to a finished product of up to 3 million characters: idea prompting, knowledge graph construction, outline management, storyboard writing, quality gate, cross‑agent review, style calibration, mid‑course outline changes with cascading updates. Even if the user simply says “Help me write a story” or “I have an idea for a novel,” this skill should be triggered.
