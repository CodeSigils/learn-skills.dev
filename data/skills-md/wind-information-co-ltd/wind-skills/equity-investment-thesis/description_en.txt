Used for in-depth research of core investment logic for individual stocks, focusing on stock research, fundamental analysis, and institutional research. This skill is primarily for answering user questions, writing reports, and drafting financial articles. The report output contains a large amount of content and is not suitable for simple dialogue scenarios. Various information and data can be obtained using the wind.financial.data tool with appropriate keywords or keyword combinations. After the user inputs a stock name, they want to quickly obtain a high-quality stock analysis that closely resembles the style of a brokerage analyst.
