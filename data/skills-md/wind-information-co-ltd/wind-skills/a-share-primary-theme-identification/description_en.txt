For identifying the main theme in the A-share market, focusing on market structure, thematic cycles, and capital behavior. This skill is primarily used for answering user questions, drafting reports, and writing financial articles. The report output is extensive and not suitable for simple dialogue scenarios. Various information and data can be retrieved using the wind.financial.data tool with appropriate keywords or keyword combinations. After the market opens, at midday, and after close each day, users need to quickly know: what the market is actually trading today, what the true main theme is, where sentiment stands, and what to watch tomorrow.
