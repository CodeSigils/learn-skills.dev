Access Wind financial data. Covers A‑share, Hong Kong, and US stock market data (latest price, K‑line, minute) and financial fundamentals (financial reports, share capital, events, technical indicators, risk); ETF and public fund market data with full‑dimensional information (profile, finance, holdings, performance, holders, management company); index and sector market data with profiles, fundamentals, and technicals; bond basic profiles, issuers, market valuation, and issuer finance; listed company announcements and financial news; macroeconomic and industry indicators. Requires a WIND_API_KEY (obtain it from the developer center by logging in at aimarket.wind.com.cn/#/user/overview). **Not included**: European stocks, Japanese stocks, exchange rates, futures order‑book data, cryptocurrencies, and non‑financial data.
