Used for post‑market review, focusing on daily retrospectives, market research, and trade summaries. This skill mainly addresses user questions, writes reports, and drafts financial articles. The report output is extensive and not suited for simple dialogue scenarios. Various information and data can be obtained using the wind.financial.data tool with appropriate keywords or keyword combinations. After the market closes, a quick review of the entire day’s market is needed to understand what happened, which signals deserve attention, and how to proceed tomorrow.
