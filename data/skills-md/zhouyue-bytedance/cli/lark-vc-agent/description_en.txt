Feishu video conference: let a bot join or leave an ongoing meeting on behalf of the current user, and read real‑time events during the meeting (participants joining and leaving, speaking, chatting, screen sharing, etc.). 1. The user provides a 9‑digit meeting ID; to request the bot to join or leave, use +meeting-join / +meeting-leave—this will generate actual join/leave records. 2. While the meeting is in progress, if the user wants to know “who joined”, “who left”, “who is speaking”, “is anyone sharing a screen”, etc., the bot joins the meeting and uses +meeting-events to read the event timeline. 3. Typical scenarios: participant bot, in‑meeting assistant, attending as a listener, attending on behalf of someone. Prerequisite: the bot can only read events of meetings it has joined and that are still ongoing; to query the participant list, minutes, or transcript of a finished meeting, use the lark‑vc skill.
