Feishu instant messaging: send and receive messages and manage group chats. Send and reply to messages, search chat history, manage group members, upload and download images and files (supports chunked download for large files), manage emoji replies, and send in‑app/SMS/phone urgent messages. Used when users need to send messages, view or search chat history, download files from chats, view group members, search groups, create group or topic chats, manage tagged data, manage feed pins (add/remove/query pinned conversations), and manage label data.
