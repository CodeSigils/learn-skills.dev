MiniQMT Rapid Investment Quantitative Trading Interface, based on the XtQuant Python library, supports obtaining market data for A‑shares, futures, and options (K‑lines, tick data, financial data, etc.) and trade order operations (order placement, cancellation, querying assets, orders, and positions). It is used when users need to get MiniQMT real‑time or historical market data, conduct quantitative trading, or perform backtesting.
