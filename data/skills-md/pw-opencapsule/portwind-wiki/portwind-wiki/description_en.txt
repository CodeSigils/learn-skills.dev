Portwind team's Feishu wiki structure, templates, and routing rules. Used when the user says “archive into wiki,” “archive into knowledge base,” “record a pitfall,” “record a gotcha,” “write a retrospective,” “write a postmortem,” “write a technical share,” “write a design doc,” “write a selection comparison,” “record a new tool,” “add to the tool radar,” “newcomer onboarding document,” or “archive according to Portwind guidelines.” Relies on lark‑cli + lark‑wiki / lark‑doc skills (no reinventing).
