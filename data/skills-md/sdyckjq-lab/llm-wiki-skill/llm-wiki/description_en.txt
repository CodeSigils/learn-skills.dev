Personal Knowledge Base Building System (based on Karpathy’s llm-wiki methodology). Let AI continuously build and maintain your knowledge base, supporting multiple content sources (webpages, Twitter, WeChat articles, Xiaohongshu, Zhihu, YouTube, PDFs, local files), automatically organizing them into a structured wiki.
Trigger condition: the user explicitly mentions “knowledge base,” “wiki,” or “llm-wiki,” or requests digestion, querying, health checks, or similar operations on an already-initialized knowledge base. Do not trigger when the user merely asks to “summarize this article”—the intent must be clearly knowledge-base-related.
