This skill is triggered when users need advice on Gaokao application choices, major selection analysis, interpretation of admission rules, school recommendations, rank analysis, subject matching, special program inquiries, extra-point policy analysis, major prospects assessment, employment market analysis, strategies for aggressive, stable, and safe applications, and risk checks for sliding or withdrawal of admissions.
