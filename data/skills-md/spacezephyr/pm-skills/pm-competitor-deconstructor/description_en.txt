Structure the disassembly of competitors along four dimensions—strategy, functionality, experience, and growth—to produce actionable takeaways, non‑copyable aspects, and differentiation recommendations. Apply this skill when a user says “competitor analysis,” “competitor teardown,” “help me analyze competitors,” “look at these competitors,” “competitor analysis,” “compare with competitors,” “benchmark analysis,” “how to differentiate,” or when the user provides a list of competitors and requests a systematic analysis. It also applies when a user uploads competitor screenshots, links, or experience reports and asks for a structured breakdown; when a user wants to compare their own product with competitors; or when a user seeks differentiation entry points. It does not apply to pure requirement document writing (use pm-prd-writer), pure priority ranking (use pm-prioritization-engine), or pure user research design (use pm-survey-designer).
