Design high-quality research questionnaires. When a user says “design a questionnaire,” “do a survey,” “help me create a questionnaire,” “user research,” “satisfaction survey,” “NPS questionnaire,” “market research,” “needs research,” “questionnaire design,” “survey design,” or describes a research objective but does not yet have a structured questionnaire, use this skill.
