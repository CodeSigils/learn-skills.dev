Generate a structured release post‑mortem report, including goal achievement, reasons for deviations, lessons learned, and follow‑up action items (with responsibility attribution and improvement owner). When the user says “write post‑mortem”, “release post‑mortem”, “project post‑mortem”, “post‑mortem report”, “postmortem”, “version review”, “iteration summary”, “release summary”, or provides release data/process records/problem lists and requests a structured summary, use this skill. It also applies to: incident post‑mortems (RCA reports after P0/P1 incidents), OKR post‑mortems, quarterly post‑mortems, A/B experiment post‑mortems. It does not apply to: pure requirement writing (use pm‑prd‑writer), pure priority ranking (use pm‑prioritization‑engine), weekly or daily reports (use status‑report).
