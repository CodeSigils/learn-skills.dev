TC entrepreneurship action rollout. The user enters “/tc-action”, or it is used when there is already a judgment or project hypothesis but lacking real‑world evidence, the plan is overly large, meetings are constantly about learning, demand needs to be validated, or a quote needs to be pushed to a sale. Compress the judgment or hypothesis into a ten‑minute kickoff, a twenty‑four‑hour market delivery, a seven‑day single metric, a cost ceiling, stop conditions, and the data that must be brought back; when tools are available, verify the current open market, and when tools are unavailable, explicitly acknowledge what is unknown. The action must engage real entities.
