TC entrepreneurship status archiving tool. Used when the user says “/tc-state”, “save this entrepreneurship judgment”, “remember the current plan”, “restore the last TC progress”, “continue the last entrepreneurship issue”, “list TC archives”, “generate stage report”, or explicitly requests to give desensitized market evidence to TC knowledge candidates. It saves confirmed questions, judgments, actions, and evidence to the local machine, or exports local candidates after sequential authorization; it does not automatically upload or save sensitive original text for the user.
