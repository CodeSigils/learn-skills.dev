TC entrepreneurship problem reconstruction and definition. Users input “/tc-diagnosis”, or use it when they need to find a single business bottleneck from chaotic narratives, entrepreneurial anxiety, multiple directions, cooperation disputes, traffic that doesn’t monetize, product that doesn’t sell, etc. First separate facts, explanations, emotions, and assumptions, then form a true problem with an object, goal, constraints, and verification criteria; after meeting the termination condition, one must stop probing further and return /tc or tc-action, not treating the diagnosis itself as the result.
