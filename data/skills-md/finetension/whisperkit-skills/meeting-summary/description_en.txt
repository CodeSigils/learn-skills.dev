It converts meeting transcripts or conversation texts into structured meeting summaries. It is used when the user requests meeting minutes, meeting summaries, main agenda items, decisions, action items, assignees, or deadlines. It is triggered by phrases like “Summarize this transcript,” “Create meeting minutes,” “Extract only the decisions,” or “Organize tasks and assignees.” If the input consists solely of audio or video, it first generates a transcript.txt using the WhisperKit skill, then proceeds with this skill. It is not used for simple dictation, subtitles, or speaker separation requests.
