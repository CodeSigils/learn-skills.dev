It is used when the user requests meeting minutes, meeting summaries, key agenda items, decisions, action items, or a整理 of responsible parties and deadlines. It is triggered by prompts such as “Summarize this transcript,” “Create meeting minutes,” “Extract only the decided items,” or “Organize tasks and assignees.” Se l'input è costituito esclusivamente da audio o video, genera prima un transcript.txt utilizzando la competenza WhisperKit, quindi continua con questa competenza.
