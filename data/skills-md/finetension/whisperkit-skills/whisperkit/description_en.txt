After normalizing audio and video files from Apple Silicon into audio for WhisperKit, speech-to-text conversion and speaker separation are performed. It is used when a user needs a transcript before dictating media, creating a transcript or subtitles, distinguishing speakers, summarizing a meeting recording, or when a media file is placed in the inbox. It is triggered by requests such as “transcribe this,” “extract audio from this video,” “convert to text,” “separate speakers,” or “I put a file in the inbox.” Summarizing meeting content, decisions, and action items is left to the meeting‑summary skill.
