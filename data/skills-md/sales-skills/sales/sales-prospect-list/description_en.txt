Builds targeted B2B prospect lists for outbound campaigns. Use when you don't know who to target, your outbound list is too broad and response rates are low, you can't find the right decision-makers at target accounts, your ICP is vague, or your prospect list is full of bad-fit contacts. Do NOT use for enriching existing contacts with emails/phones (use /sales-enrich), interpreting buying signals (use /sales-intent), or general Apollo platform help (use /sales-apollo).
