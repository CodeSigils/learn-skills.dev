Design and scaffold a NEW trapstreet.run task to evaluate a given agent/skill/tool -- the reverse of trapstreet-solution-scaffold (which builds a solution against an existing task). Generates the mechanical parts (traptask.yaml, judge.py/grader.py matching the TRAPTASK_MANIFEST contract, build_cases.py's validate-then-render pipeline) and guides the judgment-heavy parts through a structured interview (what does this agent/skill actually do, what counts as correct, where does ground truth come from, how is scoring made ungameable) plus a checklist of real exploits and pitfalls already found the hard way. Use whenever the user wants to build a new evaluation task, turn an agent/skill into a benchmark, design test cases for a tool, or asks things like "can we make a task out of this", "how do I evaluate my agent on trapstreet", "design a benchmark for X" -- even if they don't say "task" or "trapstreet-tasks" by name.
