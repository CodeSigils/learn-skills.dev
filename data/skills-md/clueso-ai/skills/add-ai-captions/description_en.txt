Auto-transcribes an existing video's spoken audio into a word-level transcript with real timing, then builds styled, on-brand, animated captions as timed text elements synced to that timing: brand fonts and colors, entry and exit animation, per-word impact styling. This is the deep brand-styled captioning skill; for plain burned-in subtitles with default styling, see add-animated-captions. Use when the user says "add captions to this video and make them match our brand", "fancy animated captions", "burn in on-brand subtitles", "caption this with per-word emphasis", or "put styled subtitles on this in our brand style".
