Pre‑release health check. After a user uploads a trimmed music short video, lyrics, prompt, reference song, or benchmark video, it comprehensively verifies whether the visuals, musical style, vocals, lyrics, prompt, basic audio safety, release package, and testing assumptions are consistent. Trigger commands: /bmz‑preflight, /pre‑release‑check, “pre‑release inspection”, “help me check this video”, “can this be posted?”.
