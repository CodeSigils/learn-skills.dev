A single‑entry AI decision workflow for music composition, lyric quality inspection, pre‑release health check, release package generation, and post‑release data review. The main Agent only handles routing and unified responses, while all expert judgments are delegated to newly spawned sub‑Agents each time, ensuring the knowledge base is not compressed by long‑session memory. It is used for original music demos, short‑video song testing, and release review.
