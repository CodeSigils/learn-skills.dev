Use when the user wants a research paper, arXiv/DOI link, or research topic turned into academic slides — journal club / lab meeting (组会汇报), thesis defense (答辩幻灯片), conference or job talk (学术报告), poster, or grant pitch — or shares a paper and asks to "make slides / 做成 PPT / 讲一下这篇论文", especially when equations, numbers, figures, and citations must stay accurate and editable. NOT for marketing/pitch decks with no scholarly source, and NOT for writing the paper itself.
