Combat the default bias of “only adding things” by enforcing a subtraction review before, during, and after any output. Subtraction has two directions: removing things that have no reason to exist, and simplifying existing things to make them more direct and effective. It applies to any situation that requires “creating something” or “improving something”—including business decisions, service planning, organizational design, marketing plans, proposals, processes, products, reports, and software development. It is automatically triggered when the user wants to add, improve, or asks “what’s still missing.”
