Writing and editing skills for Chinese blogs, articles, product analyses, technical documentation, and UI copy. Use a prioritized set of universal rules to retain verifiable facts and the author’s original viewpoints, and to handle topic drift, sudden changes in subject, ambiguous references, missing inter‑sentence relations, vague repetitions, terminology drift, unnecessary mixing of Chinese and English, formulaic rhythm, fabricated human tone, and procedural meta‑narratives; the first pass applies only the universal rules, and only after repeated revisions still fail should case references be consulted.
