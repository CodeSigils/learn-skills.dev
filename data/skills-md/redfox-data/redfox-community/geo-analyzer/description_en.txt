GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) brand analysis tool. Automatically queries the three AI search engines Doubao, Kimi, and DeepSeek, analyzing the brand's appearance rate, sentiment, source citations, and competitor comparisons in AI responses, and generates an interactive HTML report. Use this when users need to analyze a brand's performance in AI search engines, GEO optimization, AI search brand monitoring, or brand AI visibility analysis.
