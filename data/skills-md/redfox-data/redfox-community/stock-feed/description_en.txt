A-Share social media short news research tool. It searches A-Share‑related short news across three major platforms—Xiaohongshu, Douyin, and public accounts—using 17 built‑in core A‑Share keywords for a single query. By default it covers data from the past seven days and provides cross‑platform comparative analysis of market sentiment. It is used when users need to study A‑Share sentiment, stock‑market discussions, market analysis, stock‑picking strategies, and price‑movement reviews. Trigger words: A‑Share, A‑Share sentiment, stock‑market short news, market analysis, limit‑up, stock picking, A‑Share review, stock discussion, market hot topics.
