Chinese social media platform topic research tool. It searches authentic discussions from the past 30 days across the three major platforms—Xiaohongshu, Douyin, and WeChat public accounts—and performs cross-platform comparative analysis of public opinion trends. It is used when users need to study hot topics on Chinese social media, analyze public opinion trends, and compare brand reputation. Trigger words: Xiaohongshu hot topics, Douyin trends, WeChat public account public opinion, cross-platform analysis, social media research, topic analysis, public opinion monitoring.
