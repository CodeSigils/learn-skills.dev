TikTok account subscription tracking — Subscribe to accounts via TikTok IDs (up to 20). The agent automatically fetches data at 9:00 AM daily and generates an HTML report. Account IDs are embedded directly in the automation command, no file storage needed. Supports batch subscription of multiple TikTok accounts, automatically creating a polished HTML report of works. The terminal/Markdown table lists, per account, the work title, favorites, comments, shares, likes, publication time, and other data. Used when users subscribe to TikTok accounts, track updates of TikTok works, or monitor competing TikTok accounts.
