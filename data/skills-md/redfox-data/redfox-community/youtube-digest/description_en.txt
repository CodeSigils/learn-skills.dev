YouTube Script Extraction — Input a YouTube video URL or ID and extract the video transcript (subtitles/narration script) with one click. By default, it outputs a pure script without timestamps; Chinese subtitle tracks are prioritized, and if no Chinese track is available, it falls back to English and automatically translates to Chinese. The results are presented in a table (title/duration/script content), with support for exporting to Excel and an optional version with timestamps. Use this when you need to extract YouTube video scripts, convert videos to text, or create video notes, summaries, translations, or derivative works.
