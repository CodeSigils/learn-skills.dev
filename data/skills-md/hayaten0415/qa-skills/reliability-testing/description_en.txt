Design and implement reliability and resilience tests — recovery, failover, fault injection / chaos experiments, and endurance — for the ISO/IEC 25010:2023 Reliability characteristic. Use when: "reliability," "resilience," "信頼性," "chaos testing," "failover," "disaster recovery," "what happens if the DB goes down," or defining SLOs/error budgets. Produces a resilience test plan with a steady-state hypothesis (design mode) and fault-injection experiments / recovery test scripts (implementation mode). This skill covers recovery AFTER a fault. Not for: finding the load level at which it falls over or throughput/latency SLOs — use performance-testing; or guarding intentional destructive/irreversible actions (bulk-delete, kill switch) — use safety-analysis. Related: performance-testing, safety-analysis, test-strategy-doc.
