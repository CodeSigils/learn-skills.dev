In the A‑share main board liquidity pool, buy candidates and position‑sell signals are generated based on trend pullback (trend_pullback); realtime_quotes is also available for batch pulling of current‑price snapshots. It is intended for pre‑order decision making—no backtesting, no automatic order placement. Use when the user wants to select stocks, view real‑time quotes, assess buy/sell signals, or check whether a position has triggered an exit condition.
