WeChat public account subscription and article management (based on the Mp2rss service that turns public accounts into RSS) — subscribe / list / unsubscribe WeChat public accounts, query a public account’s historical articles, and fuzzy‑search subscribed sources by keyword. Recognized commands include “subscribe to this public account <article URL>”, “convert this public account to RSS”, “subscribe <mp.weixin.qq.com/s/...>”, “which public accounts have I subscribed to”, “list my public‑account RSS”, “what has this public account posted recently”, “<some public account>’s historical articles”, “pull articles from <some public account>”, “unsubscribe <some public account>”, “remove <some public account> from subscriptions”, “search my subscribed public accounts for <some keyword>”, and “mp2rss mp list / search / subscribe / remove / articles”. When subscribing, the input is a public‑account article URL (mp.weixin.qq.com/s/...), not the account name itself. ⚠️ Only handle “WeChat public account” semantics; mentions of “X account / Twitter / xUserId / long articles / tweets” should be routed to mp2rss‑x instead.
