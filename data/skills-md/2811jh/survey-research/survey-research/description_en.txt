Automatically analyze questionnaire raw data end-to-end, covering basic statistics (frequency distributions + demographic overview), cross-analysis (comparing differences across groups), and text analysis (thematic summarization of open-ended responses).  
Supports two data sources: users upload local CSV/Excel files, or download (with optional cleaning) directly from the survey system via survey ID/name before analysis.  
Outputs a Markdown summary report + Word analysis report (default), convertible to Excel/TXT.  
Triggers when users request survey data analysis, survey report generation, group comparisons, or open-text analysis. Activates even without the word “questionnaire” for research data analysis, user-feedback analysis, satisfaction analysis, NPS analysis, etc.  
Also triggers on download-and-analyze requests like “download and analyze the survey,” “clean the survey then generate a report,” “download survey 90450 for me,” “download domestic survey data,” or “pull data from the survey system.”  
Always invoke this skill for phrases such as “analyze this survey,” “compare differences by gender,” “analyze with text responses,” “download survey 90450 and analyze,” or “clean and export the survey.”
