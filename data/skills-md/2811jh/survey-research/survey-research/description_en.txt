Automated end-to-end analysis of raw survey data, covering basic statistics (frequency distribution + demographic overview), cross-tabulation (comparing differences across groups), and text analysis (thematic coding of open-ended responses).  
Supports two data sources: user-uploaded local CSV/Excel files or direct download from the survey platform by survey ID/name (with cleaning) before analysis.  
Outputs a Markdown summary report + Word analysis report (default), with optional export to Excel/TXT formats.  
Triggered when users request survey analysis, survey research report generation, group comparisons, or open-ended text analysis. Activates even without the word “survey” for research data analysis, user feedback analysis, satisfaction analysis, NPS analysis, etc.  
Also triggered by commands like “download and analyze survey,” “clean survey then generate report,” “download survey 90450 for me,” “download domestic survey data,” “pull data from survey system,” etc.  
Always use this skill for requests such as “analyze this survey,” “compare differences by gender,” “include text analysis,” “download survey 90450 then analyze,” “clean and export survey.”
