Teambition Bug management skill, using a personal account userToken to directly call the official OpenAPI to read, analyze, comment on, and advance project defect tasks, without relying on teambition_cli or MCP.  
Use when: (1) you need to read Teambition bug/defect task details, remarks, screenshot image attachments, and comment activity; (2) you need to identify screenshot content to determine the bug cause; (3) you need to queue bugs for processing based on urgency; (4) you need to reply to questions and progress updates in language understandable by planners and other non‑technical staff; (5) you need to change task status to “In Modification,” “In Fixing,” “Claimed,” “Pending Acceptance,” etc., or update the title, remarks, assignee, priority, and due date.
