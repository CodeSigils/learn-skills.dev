News scraping and aggregation skill: uses a master-agent + sub-agent parallel model to scrape news from multiple sites.  
Use it when the user needs bulk news scraping, the latest content from many sites, or aggregated news.  
The master agent reads the config, spawns a sub-agent for each site to scrape in parallel; sub-agents use Playwright to visit the site and summarize each article (~50 Chinese characters), then the master agent consolidates and outputs the results.  
Proactively invoke this skill for news collection, content monitoring, and information aggregation.
