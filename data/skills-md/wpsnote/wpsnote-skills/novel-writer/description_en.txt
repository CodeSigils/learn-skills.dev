AI companion for serialized long-form novel creation, integrated with WPS Notes for memory, context awareness, and continuity without contradictions.  
Trigger phrases: “Help me write a novel,” “I want to write a novel,” “Continue the novel,” “Write the next chapter,” “I have a story idea,” “Help me create.”  
Core features: cold-start world-building (world setting + character design + AI-generated images), chapter-by-chapter writing, automatic recap of previous content to avoid contradictions, full archival in WPS Notes.  
Not suitable for short pieces, essays, poetry, or non-novel formats.
