AI companion for serialized novel-writing, integrated with WPS Notes for memory-rich, context-aware, continuity-perfect creation.  
Trigger phrases: help me write a novel, I want to write a novel, continue writing the novel, write the next chapter, I have a story idea, help me create.  
Core functions: cold-start建档 (world-building + character design + AI image generation), chapter-by-chapter writing, automatic recap of previous text to avoid plot holes, full archive in WPS Notes.  
Not for: short pieces, essays, poetry, or non-serialized fiction.
