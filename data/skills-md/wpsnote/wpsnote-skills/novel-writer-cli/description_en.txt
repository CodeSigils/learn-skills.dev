AI companion for serialized novel writing (CLI version). Operates WPS Notes via the command-line tool wpsnote-cli, enabling continuous, context-aware, continuity-safe creation. Triggers: “help me write a novel,” “I want to write a novel,” “continue the novel,” “write the next chapter,” “I have a story idea,” “help me create.” Core features: cold-start建档 (world-building + character design + AI image generation), chapter-by-chapter writing, automatic recap of previous text to prevent continuity errors, full archival in WPS Notes. Not for: short pieces, essays, poems, or non-serialized fiction.
