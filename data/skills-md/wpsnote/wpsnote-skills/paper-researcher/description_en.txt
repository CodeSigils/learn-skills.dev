Academic Paper Full-Process Assistant: search papers, download PDFs, save to WPS notes, conduct in-depth reading and analysis. It is triggered when the user says "search paper", "find paper", "download paper", "read paper", "help me find paper", "search for papers related to XXX", "save this paper to notes", "analyze this paper", "help me do literature review". Supports arXiv and OpenAlex as data sources, automatically completing the full closed loop of search → download → convert to text → write into WPS notes → analysis.
