Import webpage content into WPS Notes with high quality, preserving the original text’s color, bold styling, heading format, and inserting images at their original positions. Supports WeChat public account articles, X/Twitter posts/threads, and any generic web page, with a unified entry that automatically recognizes them, using web_fetch, web_search, batch_web_search, create_note, batch_edit, insert_image, and sync_note to write directly into WPS Notes. Triggered when the user says “Save this webpage to notes,” “Import this article,” “Capture this page to notes,” “Save the public account article to WPS Notes,” “Save this tweet to notes,” “Bookmark this link,” or “Webpage to notes.” Not applicable for: news intelligent interpretation (use news-to-note), bulk import of local documents (use doc-importer).
