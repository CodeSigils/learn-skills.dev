Batch import local documents into WPS Notes. Supports scanning Obsidian Vaults, Siyuan Notes, WeChat public account archives, download directories, or any user-specified folder, automatically recognizing formats such as HTML, Markdown, PDF, DOCX, PPTX, XLSX, etc., converting them into content readable by WPS Notes while preserving images and rich-text formatting. Triggered when the user says “import documents to WPS Notes,” “import my Obsidian notes,” “import Siyuan notes,” “import downloaded PDF/Word/PPT into notes,” “import public account articles into notes,” or “sync local documents to WPS Notes.” Not applicable for directly editing WPS Note content or document format conversion (without importing into notes).
