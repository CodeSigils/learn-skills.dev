Liaison Cloud entries (a.k.a. proxies): create, list, update, enable/disable, delete the public-facing endpoints that expose a connector's applications to the internet. An entry binds an application to a public port (TCP) or domain (HTTP). Use this skill for intents like 'expose my app via a public URL', 'open port 8080 to the world', 'list my entries', 'take entry 10 offline'. Always read ../liaison-shared/SKILL.md first.
