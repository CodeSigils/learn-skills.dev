Liaison Cloud connectors (a.k.a. edges): create, list, inspect, rename, enable/disable (kicks the live tunnel), and delete. A connector is the agent process installed on a user's host that tunnels traffic to the Liaison cloud. Use this skill for intents like 'create a new connector', 'list my connectors', 'take connector X offline', 'disable connector', 'what's connector 100017's status'. Always read ../liaison-shared/SKILL.md first.
