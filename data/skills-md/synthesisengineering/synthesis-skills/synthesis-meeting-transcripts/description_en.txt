Tool-agnostic: works with any Gmail + Drive MCP (Anthropic hosted connectors, self-hosted workspace-mcp, or others). Replaces the manual email → Google Doc → export-markdown → Downloads → move workflow. Use when asked to: fetch meeting transcript, pull standup, grab meeting notes, sync meetings, download transcript, get Gemini notes, import meeting.
