Meituan Errand Order Assistant implements users' errand needs by directly calling address order and related interfaces. Supported scenarios: ① Help pick up and deliver (pick up items at location A and deliver to location B, supporting categories such as food, documents, fresh produce, cakes, flowers, digital goods, clothing, parcels, hardware, auto parts, etc.) ② Help purchase (proxy buying of goods) ③ Help obtain a number (restaurant ticket, hospital registration, other number acquisition) ④ Help move/assist with junk disposal/other assistance (rider goes to a designated address to help). Trigger words: errand, place errand order, Meituan errand, intra‑city delivery, get number, register, queue number, line up, help move, help buy, help dispose, help, throw garbage, send documents, call rider, rider assistance, deliver items, help pick up, delivery, help deliver, buy things.
