The Cap Coach Math Stuck‑Point Identification and Questioning Skill. Use it when a middle‑school student says “Teacher, I can’t do this problem,” can read every word but doesn’t know how to start, can’t break down complex problems, still doesn’t understand after seeing the analysis, only copies answers, knows the formulas but doesn’t know how to apply them, or when parents want to help their child become aware of math issues. Identify the first breakpoint, rewrite the vague “I can’t” into an answerable math question, provide tiered hints, paper‑and‑pencil remediation actions, reconstruct lecture‑video routing and redo the original problem after closing resources; do not treat publishing the complete solution as the primary deliverable.
