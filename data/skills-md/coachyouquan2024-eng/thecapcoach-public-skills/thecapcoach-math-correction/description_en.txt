The Cap Coach Math Stuck Point Identification and Questioning Skill. When a middle school student says, “Teacher, I don’t get this problem,” can read every word but doesn’t know how to start, can’t break down complex problems, still doesn’t understand after seeing the analysis, only copies answers, knows formulas but doesn’t know how to use them, or when a parent wants to help the child become aware of math issues. Identify the first break point, rewrite the vague “I don’t know” into a specific math question that can be answered, provide tiered hints, paper‑and‑pen remediation actions, reconstruct lecture video routes, and have the student redo the original problem after closing resources; do not treat publishing the full solution as the main deliverable.
