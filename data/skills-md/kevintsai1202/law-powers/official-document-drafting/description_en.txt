Used for drafting or revising official documents (letters, correspondence, announcements, signatures, etc.) of the Republic of China’s administrative agencies, correctly applying paragraph structure, terminology, and formatting rules in accordance with the “Document Handling Manual” and the “Government Document Format Reference Specification.” When a user needs to write a letter to a government agency, an internal sign‑off, an announcement, a meeting notice, or inquire about official document language, salutations, expectations, or purpose statements, this skill should be employed.
