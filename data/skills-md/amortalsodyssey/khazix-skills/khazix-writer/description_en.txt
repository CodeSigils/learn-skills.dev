Digital Life Khazix's WeChat Official Account long-form writing skill. Use when users need to write official account articles, draft content, continue writing, or produce long-form content based on materials. Trigger words include but are not limited to: write article, draft, help me write, continue writing, expand writing, official account article, long-form, deliver draft, write in my style. Even if users simply say "help me turn this into an article" or "write this in my style," as long as the context involves content creation and official account output, it should be triggered. Also applies when users send a PDF, brief, news link, voice-to-text, or any material saying "help me write an article." Do not use for short content (Xiaohongshu posts, tweets, Moments) or pure title/summary generation (use wechat-title skill for that).
