Khazix's digital life public account long-form writing skill. Use it when a user needs to write a public account article, draft, continue an article, or produce a long piece from material. Trigger words include, but are not limited to: write article, draft, help me write, continue, expand, public account article, long article, produce draft, write in my style. Even if the user only says "help me turn this into an article" or "write it in my style," as long as the context involves content creation and public account output, it should be triggered. It also applies to scenarios where the user provides a PDF, brief, news link, voice-to-text, or any material and says "help me write an article." Do not use it for short content (Xiaohongshu posts, Twitter, Moments) or pure title/summary generation (use the wechat-title skill for that). 

Please always respond in the user's language.
