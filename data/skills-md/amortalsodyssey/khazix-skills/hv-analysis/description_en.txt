Horizontal-Vertical Analysis (Horizontal-Vertical Analysis) is a deep‑research skill. Proposed by digital‑life Khazix, it blends Saussure’s diachronic‑synchronic analysis, the longitudinal‑cross‑section research design of the social sciences, business‑school case‑study methods, and the core ideas of competitive‑strategy analysis. It is used when a user wants to systematically study a product, company, concept, technology, or individual. The core is a dual‑axis analysis: the vertical axis tracks the complete life trajectory from birth to the present (presented as a narrative story), the horizontal axis makes a systematic side‑by‑side comparison with competing or similar items at the current time slice, and the intersection of the two axes yields unique insights. The final output is a beautifully formatted PDF research report.  

Trigger words include but are not limited to: horizontal‑vertical analysis, research this, help me analyze, deep research, do a study, conduct a survey, competitive analysis, help me see how this thing is, what’s this product/company/concept about, help me figure it out, help me understand, help me do a deep research.  

Even if the user only says “help me learn about XX” or “what’s the background of XX,” as long as the context implies a need for systematic deep research (rather than a simple definition), it should be triggered. It also applies when a user drops a product name, company name, or technical term and says “help me research this.”  

Do not use it for simple term explanations (when the user only asks “what is XX”), do not use it for public‑account writing (use khazix‑writer for that), and do not use it for pure title‑summary generation (use wechat‑title).  

Please always respond in the user’s language.
