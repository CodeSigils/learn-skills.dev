Horizontal-Vertical Analysis (HVA) is a deep-research Skill proposed by the digital life-form Kazk. It fuses Saussure’s diachronic-synchronic analysis, the longitudinal–cross-sectional research design of social science, the business-school case method, and the core ideas of competitive-strategy analysis.

Use it when the user wants to systematically study a product, company, concept, technology, or person. The core is a two-axis analysis: the vertical axis traces the complete life story from birth to the present (told as a narrative); the horizontal axis conducts a systematic side-by-side comparison with rivals or peers at the present moment. The two axes are then crossed to yield unique insights. The final deliverable is a beautifully laid-out PDF report.

Trigger phrases include but are not limited to: horizontal-vertical analysis, research this for me, help me analyze, deep research, do a study, investigate, competitive analysis, how is this thing, what’s the story with this product/company/concept, help me figure this out, help me understand, do a deep research for me.

Even if the user only says “help me understand XX” or “what’s the background of XX,” trigger the Skill whenever the context implies a need for systematic, in-depth research (rather than a simple definition). It also applies when the user drops a product name, company name, or tech term and says “research this for me.”

Do not use for simple noun explanations (user just asks “what is XX”), do not use for WeChat article writing (use khazix-writer), do not use for pure headline/summary generation (use wechat-title).
