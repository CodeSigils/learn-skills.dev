Horizontal-Vertical Analysis (Horizontal-Vertical Analysis) deep research Skill. Proposed by Digital Life Khazix, integrating Saussure's diachronic-synchronic analysis, longitudinal-cross-sectional research design in social sciences, business school case study methodology, and core ideas of competitive strategy analysis.

Use when users want to systematically study a product, company, concept, technology, or person. The core is dual-axis analysis: the vertical axis tracks the complete life journey from birth to present (presented as narrative storytelling), the horizontal axis conducts systematic lateral comparison with competitors/peers at the current time point, and finally the two axes intersect to produce unique insights. The final output is a beautifully formatted PDF research report.

Trigger words include but are not limited to: horizontal-vertical analysis, research it, help me analyze, deep research, do a research, investigate, competitive analysis, help me see how this thing is, what's the deal with this product/company/concept, help me figure it out, help me understand, help me do a deep research.

Even if the user simply says "help me understand XX" or "what's the background of XX," as long as the context implies the need for systematic deep research (rather than simple concept explanation), it should be triggered. Also applicable when users drop a product name, company name, or technical term and say "help me research this."

Do not use for simple noun explanations (user just asking "what is XX"), do not use for WeChat official account writing (use khazix-writer for that), do not use for pure title and summary generation (use wechat-title for that).
