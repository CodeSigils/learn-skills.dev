Paper workflow: read papers + cast cards in one go. Takes one or more arxiv links, paper URLs, PDFs, or paper names. For each paper, runs ljg-paper (generates org analysis) then ljg-card -c (generates comic-style card PNG). Use when user says '論文流', 'paper flow', '讀論文並做卡片', '論文卡片', or provides multiple papers wanting both analysis and cards.
