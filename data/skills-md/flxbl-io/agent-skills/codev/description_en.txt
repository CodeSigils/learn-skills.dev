Salesforce development workflows for sf-core backed by the sfp dev pool. Three modes — (1) implement, autonomous end-to-end build/fix on a fresh sandbox with deploy and report; (2) sandbox-fetch, grab a fresh sandbox for ad-hoc work like exploring, debugging, manual testing; (3) sandbox-open, reopen a sandbox already assigned to you. Use when the user asks to build/fix in this repo, asks for a sandbox/org, or asks to reopen one they already have.
