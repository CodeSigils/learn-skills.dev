WeChat Official Account Management Toolset, supporting material management (upload/fetch/delete permanent and temporary images, videos, audio, etc.), draft management (create/edit/delete/publish drafts), and data statistics (user/article/message analytics). Designed for Official Account creators to automate content management and publishing workflows via Claude Code. Trigger scenarios: user mentions WeChat Official Account, Official Account materials, Official Account drafts, publishing articles, uploading images to Official Account, Official Account data statistics, etc.
