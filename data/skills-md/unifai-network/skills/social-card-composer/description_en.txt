A universal social media card and layout generator with automatic AI background removal. Use this when a user wants to remove backgrounds from subjects (images) and composite them into customizable, aesthetic posters for social media (e.g., comparison cards, lookbooks, product showcases). Supports fully customizable titles, subtitles, background images, and text boxes. Now natively supports twin engines satori (default for text-heavy layouts) and pillow (for shadow-heavy 1:1 image comparisons).
