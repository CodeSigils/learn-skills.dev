Use this skill to orient yourself — where things stand and what's safe to pick up — across a paused session or a team. Run /status when resuming ('where was I?', 'what's left?', 'catch me up'), joining a shared repo ('what's in-progress?', 'am I behind?'), or before starting to avoid colliding with a teammate. Reads git state, the feature roadmap, and ADRs; reports what's done, what's in-progress with its resume point, and collaboration hazards (behind the remote, a feature someone else is mid-build on). Read-only.
