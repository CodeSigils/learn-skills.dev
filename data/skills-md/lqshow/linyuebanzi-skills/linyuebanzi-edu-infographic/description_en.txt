Convert any disciplinary knowledge point into a vertical educational infographic (9:16). The user only needs to input the name of a knowledge point (e.g., "sound phenomenon," "Pythagorean theorem," "photosynthesis"), and the Skill automatically completes: recognizing the subject/grade/chapter, breaking it down into 3‑5 core sub‑concepts, assigning a theme color scheme, generating specific illustration descriptions for each sub‑concept, constructing a complete prompt, and invoking linyuebanzi-image-gen to generate the image. It includes a built‑in two‑layer subject accuracy check: before image generation, it self‑checks the prompt against a subject checklist; after generation, it reads back the PNG for visual verification (text/formula/diagram) and automatically retries if it fails, ensuring the content delivered to students is accurate. It provides illustration guidelines for physics (sound, light, heat, force, electricity, lenses, mechanical motion), mathematics, chemistry, biology, Chinese language, history, English, and other subjects. Batch generation is supported—"all chapters of 8th‑grade physics first semester" will be generated chapter by chapter, each chapter with a different color scheme but a unified style. Trigger phrases include: "make an infographic," "knowledge card," "subject diagram," "physics infographic," "chemistry infographic," "make a knowledge map," "draw a knowledge card," "turn this knowledge point into a diagram." Do not use it for dynamic interactive demos (use the interactive demo Skill), article cover images (use linyuebanzi-cover-generator), or hand‑drawn‑style article illustrations (use linyuebanzi-inline-diagram).
