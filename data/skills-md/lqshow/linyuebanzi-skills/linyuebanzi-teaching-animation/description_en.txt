Automatically turn a single subject concept (in Chinese or English, e.g., “sound phenomenon,” “lever principle,” “photosynthesis”) into dynamic teaching content. Both outputs are rendered by HyperFrames and share the same index.html (mode variable switch): ① narrated teaching video — storyboard + Minimax TTS Chinese narration + subtitles + full MP4 (1080p, ~90 s, for posting on video channels or for children to watch); ② silent looping animation — a compact silent version of the same content as MP4 (1080p, ~32 s, looped, embedded in a public‑account article with auto‑play). They share a single 7‑scene storyboard and the same set of theme colors (sound/light/force/electricity/heat/biology/mathematics each have a color scheme), and the video and animation styles are completely unified. When a user asks for “teaching video,” “narrated video,” “teaching animation,” “silent animation,” “looping video,” “public‑account embedded animation,” “concept → animation,” or provides a subject concept to be turned into dynamic content, use this skill.
