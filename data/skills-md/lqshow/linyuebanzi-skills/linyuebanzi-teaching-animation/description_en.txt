Automatically turn a single subject concept (in Chinese or English, e.g., “sound phenomenon,” “lever principle,” “photosynthesis”) into dynamic teaching content. Both outputs are rendered by HyperFrames and share the same index.html (mode variable switch): ① Voice‑over teaching video — storyboard + Minimax TTS Chinese voice‑over + subtitles + full MP4 (1080p, ~90 s, for posting on video channels or for children to watch); ② Silent looping animation — a compact silent version of the same content in MP4 (1080p, ~32 s, looping, auto‑play embedded in a public‑account article). They share a set of 7 storyboard segments and the same thematic color scheme (each of sound/light/force/electric/thermal/biology/mathematics has its own color series), and the video and animation styles are completely unified. When a user mentions “teaching video,” “voice‑over video,” “teaching animation,” “silent animation,” “looping video,” “public‑account embedded animation,” “concept → animation,” or provides a subject concept that needs to be turned into dynamic content, this skill is used.
