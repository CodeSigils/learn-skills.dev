An exploratory tool for the ambiguous-requirement stage, used before the direction is set. It asks about every decision branch of a plan, design, or requirement one by one, actively challenging assumptions and exposing potential weaknesses until every branch has been questioned and every assumption tested. Use it when users say they “want to discuss” or “need help clarifying,” or when a plan needs stress-testing or design assumptions need validation. Do not use it when the direction is already fixed and needs documenting, or when the task is ready for direct implementation.
