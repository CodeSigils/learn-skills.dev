Exploration tool for the fuzzy requirements phase, used before direction is established. Systematically interrogates every decision branch in plans, designs, or requirements, actively challenging assumptions and uncovering potential weaknesses until all branches are exhausted and assumptions are tested. Applicable when users say "want to discuss" or "help clarify," or when stress-testing plans and validating design assumptions is needed. Not applicable when direction is already established and needs documentation, or when direct implementation is required.
