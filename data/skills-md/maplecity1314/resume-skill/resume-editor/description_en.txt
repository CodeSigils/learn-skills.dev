Resume overall review and diagnosis entry. Triggered when the user says “help me check my resume,” “resume diagnosis,” “resume score,” “overall resume optimization,” “help me generate a resume file,” “I’m about to submit my resume,” “what’s wrong with my resume,” “resume review,” or “CV check.” Also serves as the entry point for the full-cycle resume-writing wizard (starting from scratch). For rewriting specific modules, please go to the corresponding skill: work experience → resume-experience, project experience → resume-project, skills section → resume-skills, self-evaluation → resume-summary. Applicable to all professions: operations, advertising, marketing, product, design, front-end/back-end, AI, sales, HR, finance, etc.
