Skill for drafting regional development plans, local special plans, and “15th Five-Year” plan texts and supporting materials. When users mention regional development plans, local special plans, development plans for a city/district/park, five-year plans in fields such as technology, industry, education, transportation, cultural tourism, elderly care, digitalization, as well as plan outlines, plan texts, plan preparation explanations, indicator systems, major project lists, implementation guarantees, consultation drafts, and expert argumentation drafts, this skill must be used. It supports automatically distinguishing “regional plans” from “special plans,” online retrieval of higher-level plans, latest policies, statistical data, and practices from comparable regions, generating formal plan texts, indicator tables, project lists, implementation matrices, and self‑review drafts. If the task is essentially a corporate strategy, business plan, investment promotion copy, or land‑space planning, this skill should not be used.
