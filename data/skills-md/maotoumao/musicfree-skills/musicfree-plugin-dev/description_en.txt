A complete guide to developing MusicFree music-player plugins from scratch. Triggered whenever a user asks to write, create, or develop a MusicFree plugin or to adapt a music site/API into a MusicFree plugin. Covers: plugin protocol, media-type definitions, method signatures, sandbox environment, site analysis, local debugging, publishing, and updates. This Skill is designed for AI execution, guiding AI and zero-experience users to collaborate on plugin development.
