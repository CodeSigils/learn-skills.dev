A complete guide to developing MusicFree desktop theme packs from scratch. Triggered when the user asks to write, create, or design a MusicFree desktop theme pack, or to generate a theme based on reference images/colors/style keywords. Covers: CSS variable system, color design paradigms, static themes, dynamic iframe themes, resource optimization, packaging & testing, submitting to the theme marketplace, and the full workflow. This Skill is aimed at AI execution, guiding AI to collaborate with community contributors (who may have no front-end experience) to complete theme pack development.
