Product Manager Competitive Product Analysis Report Generator. It is triggered when the user mentions competitive product analysis, competitor research, competitive comparison, competitor analysis, market competition analysis, or industry competitive product research. It also applies to scenarios where the user requests to compare the features, business models, market positioning, etc., of multiple products/platforms/companies. Even if the user simply says “help me analyze XX and YY” or “what are the competitors of XX,” as long as it involves comparative analysis between products/companies, this skill should be used. Supports any industry and offers both quick analysis and deep analysis modes.
